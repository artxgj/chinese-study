# 第二部分 – 雙語回答（英文與中文分開）

## English Answer

**High-level summary** 
The poem **"Seeking the Hermit in Vain"** (尋隱者不遇 / 寻隐者不遇) was written by the Tang dynasty poet **Jia Dao** (賈島 / 贾岛). It famously captures a failed visit to a reclusive master through a brief, suggestive dialogue with his young disciple. Below you will find the poem in **Traditional and Simplified characters**, the **pinyin for the title, author, and each line (presented separately)**, and a detailed explanation of its vocabulary, translation, and artistic depth.

---

**Poem Text & Pinyin**

**Title & Author (Pinyin):** 
Xún Yǐnzhě Bú Yù 
Jiǎ Dǎo

**Poem (Pinyin – presented by line, not by individual character):**
Sōng xià wèn tóng zǐ, 
Yán shī cǎi yào qù. 
Zhǐ zài cǐ shān zhōng, 
Yún shēn bù zhī chù.

---

**Poem – Traditional Characters (繁體字)**
**尋隱者不遇** 
賈島 
松下問童子， 
言師採藥去。 
只在此山中， 
雲深不知處。

**Poem – Simplified Characters (简体字)**
**寻隐者不遇** 
贾岛 
松下问童子， 
言师采药去。 
只在此山中， 
云深不知处。

---

**Detailed Explanation**

**1. Author & Background** 
- **Jia Dao (779–843)** – A Tang dynasty poet known for his obsessive devotion to the craft of poetry. The famous idiom **「推敲」 (tuīqiāo – "to deliberate / to weigh words")** originates from his intense focus on choosing the perfect character.
- **The poem** – Written during his visit to a mountain hermit. He did not find the hermit, but this "failure" became the very essence of the poem, capturing the mystery and transcendence of the reclusive life.

**2. Vocabulary Breakdown**
- **隱者 (yǐnzhě) / 隐者** – *Hermit / recluse* (one who hides from worldly affairs).
- **不遇 (bú yù) / 不遇** – *Did not meet* (遇 = to encounter). Note the tone change: *bú* before *yù* (4th tone).
- **松下 (sōng xià) / 松下** – *Under the pine tree* – pines symbolise longevity and resilience in Chinese culture, often associated with hermits.
- **童子 (tóng zǐ) / 童子** – *Boy / young disciple* (the hermit's apprentice).
- **言 (yán) / 言** – *Says / said* (introducing the boy's reply).
- **師 (shī) / 师** – *Master* (the hermit himself).
- **採藥 (cǎi yào) / 采药** – *Gather medicinal herbs* – a classic activity of Daoist hermits seeking immortality or healing.
- **只在此山中 (zhǐ zài cǐ shān zhōng) / 只在此山中** – *Only (he is) within this mountain* (只 = only; 此 = this).
- **雲深 (yún shēn) / 云深** – *Clouds are deep / dense clouds* – symbolises the unfathomable, mysterious realm.
- **不知處 (bù zhī chù) / 不知处** – *Do not know the place / whereabouts* (處 = location).

**3. Full Translation (English)**
*Under the pine trees I asked the young boy.
He said: "My master has gone to gather herbs.
He is only within this mountain,
But the clouds are so deep – I do not know where."*

**4. Artistic Depth (Why this poem is famous)**
- **The unasked questions** – The poem cleverly omits the poet's questions. From the boy’s three replies, we infer three questions: *“Where is your master?” → “He went to gather herbs.” → “Where did he go?” → “Only in this mountain.” → “Where exactly?” → “I don’t know – the clouds are deep.”*
- **The "unmet" is the point** – The poet never meets the hermit, but the hermit’s presence is felt through the boy, the pine trees, the herbs, and the deep clouds. The **deep clouds** (雲深) represent both the physical barrier and the hermit’s lofty, unreachable state of being.
- **Simplicity and suggestion** – With only 20 characters, the poem paints a vivid, tranquil scene and leaves the reader with a sense of mystery and yearning.

## 中文回答

**高層概述**
〈尋隱者不遇〉是唐代詩人**賈島**的名作。全詩以簡練的對話，寫出他入山訪隱士而未遇的過程，卻在「未遇」中勾勒出隱者的高潔與神秘。以下提供**繁體與簡體**詩文、**單獨列出**的拼音，以及詳盡解析。

---

**詩文與拼音**

**標題與作者（拼音）：**
Xún Yǐnzhě Bú Yù
Jiǎ Dǎo

**全詩拼音（分行呈現，不與中文字並列）：**
Sōng xià wèn tóng zǐ,
Yán shī cǎi yào qù.
Zhǐ zài cǐ shān zhōng,
Yún shēn bù zhī chù.

---

**詩文 – 繁體字**
**尋隱者不遇**
賈島
松下問童子，
言師採藥去。
只在此山中，
雲深不知處。

**詩文 – 簡體字**
**寻隐者不遇**
贾岛
松下问童子，
言师采药去。
只在此山中，
云深不知处。

---

**詳細解析**

**1. 作者與背景**
- **賈島（779–843）** – 唐代詩人，以刻苦煉字聞名。成語 **「推敲」** 即源自他對用字反覆斟酌的故事。
- **創作背景** – 此詩寫於他入山尋訪隱士之時。雖未遇隱者，但「不遇」本身成為詩眼，反襯出隱士生活的超凡與神秘。

**2. 詞彙逐解**
- **隱者** – 隱居不問世事的人。
- **不遇** – 沒有遇到。「不」在此讀作 **bú**（因遇為第四聲，變調）。
- **松下** – 松樹之下。松樹在中國文化中象徵長壽、堅韌，常與隱士關聯。
- **童子** – 學徒、少年（隱者的弟子）。
- **言** – 說（引出童子的回答）。
- **師** – 師父（指隱者本人）。
- **採藥** – 採集草藥。道教隱士常以採藥煉丹、濟世或追求長生。
- **只在此山中** – 就在這座山裡面（只＝僅僅；此＝這個）。
- **雲深** – 雲霧很深。此處象徵深不可測、縹緲神祕的境界。
- **不知處** – 不知道確切的地方（處＝位置／所在）。

**3. 全詩白話翻譯**
*我在松樹下詢問童子，
他說師父已經去山中採藥了。
（童子又說）他就在這座大山裡，
可是雲霧太深，我不知道他確實在哪裡。*

**4. 藝術深度（為何此詩千古傳誦）**
- **省略的問句** – 全詩完全省略了詩人的問話，但從童子的三句回答，我們可以反向推知三問：
*「師父在哪？」→「去採藥。」→「去哪兒採？」→「就在這山裡。」→「具體何處？」→「雲深不知。」*
- **「不遇」正是詩眼** – 詩人雖未見到隱者，但透過童子、松樹、採藥、雲深等意象，隱者的形象反而更加鮮明。**雲深**既是地理上的迷霧，也象徵隱士境界的高遠莫測。
- **以少勝多** – 全詩僅二十字，卻寫活了山林的幽靜、童子的天真與隱者的飄逸，留下無盡的餘韻與想像空間。
