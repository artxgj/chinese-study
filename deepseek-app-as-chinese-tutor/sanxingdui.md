**三星堆** 是位于中国四川省广汉市的一处考古遗址，属于距今**4800年至2600年**的古蜀文化遗存。它是长江流域迄今发现的规模最大、文化内涵最丰富的古蜀国都城遗址，被称为“20世纪人类最伟大的考古发现之一”。

### 📜 发现与发掘历程

三星堆的发现充满了偶然。1929年，当地农民燕道诚在挖水沟时偶然发现一坑玉器，揭开了古蜀国的面纱。1934年，首次科学发掘由美国学者葛维汉主持。1986年，两个“祭祀坑”的发现出土了上千件文物，轰动世界。2020年以来，新发现的**3至8号祭祀坑**又出土了超过**1.5万件**珍贵文物。

### 👑 主要文物与特征

三星堆文物风格独特，颠覆了人们对古代文明的认知：

· **青铜神树**：高达**3.95米**的青铜神树，被认为是古人眼中连接天地的“扶桑”神树。

· **青铜大立人像**：通高**2.62米**，是目前发现的最高、最完整的青铜人像。

· **青铜纵目面具**：拥有夸张的柱状眼球，可能与古蜀王“蚕丛纵目”的传说有关。

· **金杖**：用黄金捶打而成的金皮包裹木杖，象征权力。

###  历史与文化意义

三星堆的发现实证了**中华文明“多元一体”的格局**。它证明中华文明的起源并非局限于中原黄河流域，而是多源并进的。

同时，考古发现也证实三星堆并非外星文明，而是**中华文明的重要组成部分**。其出土文物既包含了大量**中原商文化的元素**（如云雷纹、青铜尊等），也体现了古蜀人**独具特色的信仰和创造力**。

### 🔬 最新研究

即使在今天，三星堆仍不断带来惊喜。**2026年7月**，专家对**青铜顶尊跪坐人像**的最新研究发现，它并非抽象神话符号，而是**真实祭祀仪典的生动再现**。科技检测还发现，这件器物是由中国不同地区的矿料分铸组合而成，是古蜀与中原、长江流域青铜文化交流互动的直接证据。

### Sanxingdui Introduction (English)

Sanxingdui is an archaeological site in Guanghan, Sichuan Province, China. Dating back 4,800 to 2,600 years, it is the largest and most culturally rich ancient capital of the Shu Kingdom from that era on the Yangtze River basin. It is hailed as "one of the greatest archaeological discoveries of the 20th century".

### 📜 Discovery and Excavation

Discovered by accident in 1929 when a farmer unearthed jade artifacts while digging a ditch, the first scientific excavation was led by American scholar David Crockett Graham in 1934. In 1986, the discovery of two "sacrificial pits" yielded over 1,000 relics, stunning the world. Since 2020, six newly discovered pits (No. 3 to No. 8) have unearthed over 15,000 more artifacts.

### 👑 Key Artifacts and Features

Sanxingdui's artifacts are stylistically unique:

· **Bronze Sacred Tree**: A 3.95-meter tall bronze tree, believed to be a "ladder to heaven".

· **Bronze Standing Figure**: At 2.62 meters tall, it's the tallest and most complete bronze human figure found to date.

· **Bronze Masks with Protruding Eyes**: Feature exaggerated cylindrical eyeballs, possibly linked to the legendary Shu king, Cancong.

· **Golden Scepter**: A wooden staff wrapped in hammered gold foil, symbolizing power.

### 🌍 Historical and Cultural Significance

Sanxingdui provides concrete evidence that Chinese civilization has "diverse origins within a unified framework" (多元一体), proving it originated from multiple centers, not just the Yellow River basin.

Furthermore, discoveries confirm Sanxingdui was an integral part of Chinese civilization, not an alien one. Its relics show influences from the central plains Shang culture (like cloud-and-thunder patterns), alongside the unique beliefs and creativity of the ancient Shu people.

### 🔬 Latest Research

In July 2026, new research on the "Bronze Figure Kneeling and Holding a Vessel on Its Head" revealed it was not a mythical symbol but a vivid representation of real sacrificial rituals. Scientific analysis also showed this artifact was assembled from materials sourced from different regions of China, providing direct evidence of cultural and technical exchange between ancient Shu and other Bronze Age cultures.
