# 上古漢語、中古漢語與文言文之區別及其影響（中英完整對照版）

# Differences and Influences of Old Chinese, Middle Chinese, and Literary Chinese (Full Bilingual Edition)

---

## 一、定義與時間範圍

## I. Definitions and Timeframes

### 上古漢語（Old Chinese）
指周代至漢代（約公元前11世紀–公元3世紀）的漢語口語與書面語系統，以《詩經》《尚書》《左傳》等經典為代表。其語音系統由先秦韻文和諧聲字推知。
Old Chinese refers to the spoken and written language system from the Zhou to the Han dynasties (c. 11th century BCE – 3rd century CE), represented by classics such as the Book of Songs, Book of Documents, and Zuo Zhuan. Its phonology is reconstructed from rhymed texts and phonetic-series characters.

### 中古漢語（Middle Chinese）
指隋唐至宋代（約公元6–12世紀）的漢語，以《切韻》（601年）等韻書為核心語音標準，反映當時長安/洛陽一帶的讀書音。它是詩律、佛經翻譯和科舉用語的基礎。
Middle Chinese refers to the Chinese language from the Sui–Tang to the Song dynasties (c. 6th–12th century CE), with the phonological standard codified in rhyme dictionaries like Qieyun (601 CE), reflecting the literary pronunciation of the Chang’an/Luoyang area. It underpins Tang poetry, Buddhist translation, and imperial examination language.

### 文言文（Literary Chinese / Classical Chinese）
指以先秦口語為基底、經後世模仿而固定的書面語體，從戰國一直使用到20世紀初。它不是一個歷史階段，而是一種跨越時代的「書面共同語」，與各時代的口語脫節。
Literary Chinese (Classical Chinese) is a written register rooted in Pre-Qin spoken language, then fixed through imitation and used from the Warring States period until the early 20th century. It is not a historical stage but a “written koine” that transcends eras, increasingly diverging from vernacular speech.

## 二、核心區別（語音、詞彙、語法）

## II. Core Differences (Phonology, Lexicon, Grammar)

### 1\. 語音系統（Phonology）

| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **聲母 Initials** | 複輔音豐富（如 *kl-, *sn-），無輕唇音、無舌上音 / Rich consonant clusters (e.g., *kl-, *sn-), no labiodentals or retroflex palatals. | 複輔音消失，產生輕唇（非、敷、奉、微）和舌上（知、徹、澄） / Clusters lost; labiodentals (fei, fu, feng, wei) and retroflex palatals (zhi, che, cheng) emerge.                            | 不反映實際讀音；歷代誦讀採中古或近代音系 / Does not reflect actual pronunciation; recitation follows Middle Chinese or later vernacular systems. |

### 2\. 詞彙（Lexicon）

&bull; **上古漢語 Old Chinese**：大量單音節詞（如「日」「月」「雨」），意義具體；抽象詞多通過引申或假借；複合詞少。
  Predominantly monosyllabic words (e.g., rì ‘sun’, yuè ‘moon’, yǔ ‘rain’), concrete meanings; abstract terms derived via semantic extension or phonetic loan; few compound words.

&bull; **中古漢語 Middle Chinese**：雙音節化加速，產生大量複合詞（如「太陽」「月亮」）；佛教詞彙（如「菩薩」「涅槃」）大量湧入。
  Rapid disyllabification, many compounds (e.g., tàiyáng ‘great sun’→sun, yuèliàng ‘moon brightness’→moon); massive influx of Buddhist loanwords (e.g., púsà ‘bodhisattva’, nièpán ‘nirvana’).

&bull; **文言文 Literary Chinese**：詞彙上強烈仿古，偏好單音節及先秦用詞（如「曰」非「說」，「之」非「的」），但後世文言也吸收中古新詞，形成層累混合。
  Lexically archaizing, favouring monosyllabic and Pre-Qin words (e.g., yuē vs. shuō for ‘say’, zhī vs. de for possessive), yet later literary texts also absorbed Middle Chinese neologisms, creating a stratified mixture.

### 3\. 語法（Grammar）

&bull; **上古漢語 Old Chinese**：語序以 SVO 為主，但疑問代詞賓語前置（「吾誰欺？」）；否定句中代詞賓語前置（「不我欺」）；大量句末語氣詞（「也」「矣」「乎」）；無量詞系統。
  Basic SVO word order, but object-fronting of interrogative pronouns (wú shéi qī? ‘whom do I deceive?’) and pronoun objects in negated sentences (bù wǒ qī ‘does not deceive me’); rich final particles (yě, yǐ, hū); no classifier system.

&bull; **中古漢語 Middle Chinese**：賓語前置消失，量詞（個、條、枚）普遍使用；語氣詞簡化；產生「了」「著」等動貌標記（但尚未完全語法化）。
  Object-fronting lost; classifiers (gè, tiáo, méi) become common; final particles reduced; aspect markers like le and zhe emerge (though not fully grammaticalised).

&bull; **文言文 Literary Chinese**：語法基本承襲上古漢語（賓語前置、語氣詞等），但後世作者常摻入中古句法（如使用「於」的處置式），總體是保守的混合體。
  Grammatically largely follows Old Chinese (retains fronting and final particles), but later writers sometimes incorporate Middle Chinese constructions (e.g., disposal forms with yú); overall a conservative hybrid.

## 三、三者的內在關係

## III. Intrinsic Relationships

**文言文 ≠ 上古漢語 ≠ 中古漢語**
文言文是「書面祖型」，以上古漢語為本，但中古以後的文人所寫文言並非純粹上古口語，而是經由中古音讀和後世詞彙改造的「擬古體」。中古漢語則是當時真實的官話口語，同時也是文言文在中古時期的誦讀音系。

**Literary Chinese ≠ Old Chinese ≠ Middle Chinese**. Literary Chinese is a “written archetype” rooted in Old Chinese, but post-Middle Chinese literati writing in it did not reproduce pure Old Chinese speech—rather, it is an archaising register mediated by Middle Chinese pronunciations and later lexical influences. Middle Chinese was the actual prestige spoken language of its time and also served as the reading pronunciation for Literary Chinese.

簡言之：

&bull; 上古漢語 = 先秦兩漢的真實口語 + 書面記錄 / Actual spoken language of Pre-Qin and Han, both spoken and written.

&bull; 中古漢語 = 隋唐宋的真實官話口語 / Actual prestige spoken language of Sui–Tang–Song.

&bull; 文言文 = 以先秦語法為骨、中古音讀為膚、歷代仿作為脈的書面傳統 / A written tradition with Old Chinese syntax as its skeleton, Middle Chinese phonology as its skin, and successive imitations as its lifeblood.

## 四、影響與歷史作用

## IV. Influences and Historical Roles

### 1\. 上古漢語的影響（Influence of Old Chinese）

&bull; 奠定了漢語的基本詞序、語氣詞系統和大量的核心詞根。<br>
Established basic word order, particle system, and core root lexicon.

&bull; 塑造了中國經典哲學（儒家、道家）的話語方式，影響東亞儒家文化圈。<br>
 Shaped the discourse of Chinese classical philosophy (Confucianism, Daoism), influencing the East Asian Confucian sphere.

&bull; 通過漢字諧聲和詩韻，為中古音韻學提供追溯基礎。<br>
 Through phonetic series and rhyme patterns, provided the basis for Middle Chinese historical phonology.

### 2\. 中古漢語的影響（Influence of Middle Chinese）

&bull; 確立了四聲和平仄對立，直接催生近體詩（律詩、絕句）的格律體系。<br>
  Established the four tones and level/oblique contrast, directly giving rise to regulated verse (lüshi and jueju) prosody.

&bull; 大量佛教譯經詞彙豐富了漢語抽象表達，並東傳日本、韓國、越南，形成漢字音系統（日韓越音讀）。<br>
  Abundant Buddhist translation terminology enriched abstract expression and spread to Japan, Korea, and Vietnam, forming their Sino-xenic pronunciations.

&bull; 《切韻》音系成為千年來字典正音標準，直至民國初年。<br>
  The Qieyun phonological system served as the orthoepic standard for dictionaries for over a millennium until the early Republic.

### 3\. 文言文的影響（Influence of Literary Chinese）

&bull; 作為東亞（中、日、韓、越）的通用書面語，維持了超過兩千年的政教、文學、法律文書的統一性。<br>
  Served as the pan-East Asian written lingua franca (China, Japan, Korea, Vietnam), maintaining uniformity in governance, education, literature, and legal documents for over 2,000 years.

&bull; 儲存了浩如煙海的歷史、文學、科技典籍，是傳統文化的主要載體。<br>
  Preserved an immense corpus of historical, literary, and scientific texts—the primary vehicle of traditional culture.

&bull; 文言與白話的長期分離，造成「言文不一致」，但也促使白話文學（如明清小說）的獨立發展。
  The long-standing diglossia between Literary and Vernacular Chinese led to a “speech-writing gap,” yet also spurred the independent development of vernacular literature (e.g., Ming-Qing novels).

&bull; 五四運動後被白話文取代，但其句法、成語和典故仍深植於現代漢語，且對日韓越的書面語詞彙有深遠影響。
  Replaced by Vernacular Chinese after the May Fourth Movement, yet its syntax, idioms, and allusions remain deeply embedded in Modern Chinese, and its lexical influence on Japanese, Korean, and Vietnamese written language is profound.

## 最終結論

## Final Conclusion

上古漢語是「源」，中古漢語是「流」，文言文是「渠」——一條人工修築的書面運河，將上游的古語水源源源不斷地輸送到後世，即便口語早已改道。三者共同鑄就了漢語文明的連續性與多樣性。

Old Chinese is the “source,” Middle Chinese the “stream,” and Literary Chinese the “canal”—a man-made written waterway that channels the ancient linguistic waters to later generations, even when spoken language has long changed its course. Together, they constitute the continuity and diversity of Chinese civilisation.
