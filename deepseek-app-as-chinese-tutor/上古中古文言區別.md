### 壹、定義與時間定位 (Definitions & Timeframes) — 完整版

#### 上古漢語 (Old Chinese / Archaic Chinese)

- **時間 (Timeframe)**：商朝、周朝至西漢時期（約公元前1300年 – 公元後200年）。精確來說，以《詩經》押韻和青銅器銘文為年代錨點。
  - _Time_: Shang Dynasty, Zhou Dynasty to the Western Han Dynasty (c. 1300 BCE – 200 CE). Precisely, it is anchored by the rhyming patterns of the Book of Songs (Shijing) and bronze inscriptions.
- **本質 (Nature)**：這是一種**實際活躍於口頭的母語**。它以先秦時期（春秋戰國）的「雅言」（標準官話）為基礎，是當時華夏民族日常交際、政治盟會、行軍打仗所使用的真正有聲語言。
  - _Nature_: This was a **genuinely spoken living vernacular**. Based on the "Elegant Speech" (Yayan) of the Pre-Qin period (Spring and Autumn & Warring States), it was the actual acoustic language used by the Huaxia people for daily communication, political alliances, and military campaigns.
- **載體 (Textual Carriers)**：甲骨文（商代）、金文（周代）、《尚書》、《詩經》以及諸子百家（如《論語》、《孟子》）的原始口語記錄。
  - _Carriers_: Oracle bone scripts (Shang), Bronze inscriptions (Zhou), the _Book of Documents_, the _Book of Songs_, and the vernacular-recorded dialogues in the Hundred Schools of Thought (e.g., _Analects, Mencius_).

#### 中古漢語 (Middle Chinese)

- **時間 (Timeframe)**：魏晉南北朝、隋朝、唐朝至宋朝（約公元200年 – 1200年）。尤其以隋唐（581–907 AD）為黃金標準。
  - _Time_:The Six Dynasties, Sui, Tang, and Song Dynasties (c. 200 CE – 1200 CE), with the Sui-Tang period (581–907 AD) as the golden standard.
- **本質 (Nature)**：這同樣是**實際口語**，但已從上古演變而來。它是隋唐首都長安/洛陽地區的通用語（相當於當代普通話）。其標準音系被陸法言於公元601年編纂的《切韻》(Qièyùn) 完整保存下來，這本韻書是中國音韻學的聖經。
  - _Nature_:This was also an **actual spoken lingua franca**, evolved from Old Chinese. It was the common speech of the Sui-Tang capitals (Chang'an/Luoyang). Its phonological system was meticulously codified by Lu Fayan in the Qièyùn Rhyme Dictionary (601 AD), which serves as the bible of Chinese historical phonology.
- 特徵 (Feature)：此時口語已與先秦截然不同，大量雙音節詞湧現，且開始使用白話虛詞（如“了”、“著”的雛形）。
  - _Feature_: The colloquial speech was drastically different from Pre-Qin times, witnessing a surge of disyllabic words and the budding of vernacular particles (e.g., early forms of "le" and "zhe").

#### 文言文 / 古典中文 (Literary Chinese / Classical Chinese)

- **時間 (Timeframe)**：戰國晚期（作為書面標準確立）一直到清末（公元1919年），甚至今天仍在對聯、詩詞創作中有限使用。跨度超過兩千年。
  - _Time_:From the late Warring States period (when it solidified as a written standard) all the way to the end of the Qing Dynasty (1919 CE), and still used in limited contexts like couplets and calligraphy today. It spans over two millennia.
- **本質 (Nature)**：這不是任何人的母語口語，而是一種純粹的書面寫作語言（古典標準語）。它嚴格模仿先秦的口語語法，但後代文人（如唐宋八大家）只在紙上「寫」它，絕不在街上「說」它。
  - _Nature_:**This is NOT anyone's native spoken tongue, but a purely written literary language (a classical standard)**. It rigidly imitates the syntax of Pre-Qin colloquial speech, yet later literati (e.g., the Tang-Song Eight Masters) only wrote it on paper and never spoke it on the streets.
- **脫鉤特性 (Decoupling)**：它徹底與口語的自然演變脫鉤。換句話說，唐朝人說的是中古漢語，但寫的文章（古文運動）卻力圖模仿西周的口語——這就像一位現代英國詩人強迫自己用貝奧武夫的古英語語法寫作。
  - _Decoupling_: It completely decoupled from the natural evolution of spoken Chinese. In other words, Tang people spoke Middle Chinese, but their essays (Guwen Movement) forcibly imitated Western Zhou speech—just as if a modern English poet forced himself to write in Beowulf's Old English syntax.

### 貳、核心語言學區別 (Core Linguistic Differences) — 完整細節

1. 語音系統 (Phonology) — 完整中英對照

- **上古漢語 (OC)**：現代語言學（如白一平-沙加爾系統）構擬出**複雜的複輔音聲母 (consonant clusters)**。例如，「各」被構擬為 *k-lak (k和l的複合)。它尚**未發展出聲調 (toneless)**，僅靠輔音韻尾（如 -s, -ʔ）來區分詞義，類似今天的藏語。音節結構非常不穩定。
  - _OC_: Modern linguistics (e.g., Baxter-Sagart system) reconstructs **complex consonant clusters** for initials. For example, 各 (each) is reconstructed as *k-lak (a cluster of 'k' and 'l'). It was **toneless**; instead, it relied on consonantal endings (like -s, -ʔ) to differentiate meaning, similar to modern Tibetan. Syllable structures were highly unstable.
- **中古漢語 (MC)**：複輔音**完全消失 (completely dissolved)**，簡化為單輔音聲母。**四聲 (Four Tones: 平 level, 上 rising, 去 departing, 入 entering) 正式成為區別意義的音位**。音節結構變得工整對稱：（介音）+（主要元音）+（韻尾 -i/-u/-m/-n/-ŋ/-p/-t/-k）。
  - _MC_: Consonant clusters completely dissolved into single initials. The Four Tones (level, rising, departing, entering) formally became phonemic (meaning-distinctive). Syllable structures became neatly symmetrical: (Medial) + (Main Vowel) + (Coda: -i/-u/-m/-n/-ŋ/-p/-t/-k).
- **文言文 (LC)**：沒有固定的標準讀音 (No fixed phonetic standard)。因為它是書面語，你可以用任何現代方言（普通話、粵語、閩南語）或日語漢音來朗讀它。其語音完全依附於朗讀者的母語音系。
  - _LC_: No fixed phonetic standard. Since it's purely written, you can recite it in any modern dialect (Mandarin, Cantonese, Hokkien) or even Sino-Japanese On-readings. Its phonetic value entirely depends on the reader's native phonology.
- 實例比對 (Character Example: 國 / Nation)：
  - 上古擬音 OC Reconstruction: *kʷɯːɡ (包含圓唇介音與濁濁尾音 -g)。
  - 中古切韻 MC (Qieyun): /kwək/ (清晰的入聲 -k 韻尾)。
  - 文言文朗讀 LC recitation: 普通話 Guó（完全失去入聲韻尾，變為元音結尾） vs. 粵語 Gwok（完美保存中古 -k 入聲）→ 這證明了文言文本身不規定音值，讀音因方言而異。

2. 詞彙與構詞 (Lexicon & Morphology) — 完整中英對照

- **上古漢語 (OC)**：詞彙以單音節詞根 (monosyllabic roots) 為絕對主導。具有豐富的派生形態 (derivational morphology)，通過輔音的清濁交替 (voicing alternation) 或加*前綴 (prefixes, 如 s- 使動前綴) 來改變詞性或使動化（例如名詞變動詞）。存在大量「通假字」(phonetic loan characters)，即純粹借音不借義。
  - _OC_: Lexicon was absolutely dominated by monosyllabic roots. It possessed rich derivational morphology, using voicing alternation of initials or prefixes (e.g., *s- causative prefix) to change parts of speech or create causative verbs (e.g., noun → verb). A massive number of "phonetic loan characters" existed (borrowing a character solely for its sound).
- **中古漢語 (MC)**：形態派生痕跡完全消失 (completely vanished)。由於語音簡化導致大量同音字，雙音節詞 (disyllabic words) 爆炸性增長。大量吸收外來語，主要是梵語佛教詞彙（如「菩薩」Bodhisattva、「涅槃」Nirvāṇa）。
  - _MC_: All derivational morphological traces completely vanished. Because phonetic simplification created massive homophones, disyllabic words exploded in number. It heavily absorbed foreign loanwords, primarily Sanskrit Buddhist terminology (e.g., 菩薩 púsà for Bodhisattva, 涅槃 nièpán for Nirvāṇa).
- **文言文 (LC)**：詞彙使用極端復古 (extremely archaic)。嚴格拒斥中古口語新詞。例如，表達「頭」必須用上古詞「首」而非中古口語「頭」；表達「吃」必須用「食」或「啖」。詞彙選擇是一種「博學的密碼」，而非當代溝通的工具。
  - _LC_: Lexicon is extremely archaic. It strictly rejects later colloquial neologisms. For example, for "head," it strictly uses the Old Chinese term 首 (shǒu) instead of the Middle Chinese colloquial 頭 (tóu); for "eat," it uses 食 (shí) or 啖 (dàn). Vocabulary choice is an "erudite code," not a tool for contemporary communication.
- 實例：動詞「行走」的細微差異 (Nuance of walking)：
  - 上古／中古口語 Old/MC colloquial: 行 (xíng, generic walk) / 步 (bù, step)。
  - 文言文規範 LC norm: 步 (slow walk)、趨 (quick walk)、走 (run)、奔 (gallop/flee)。每個動詞皆有嚴格的古義層級，中古口語早已將這些模糊化；而文言文強制作者使用精準的古代分類。

3. 語法結構 (Syntax & Grammar) — 完整中英對照

- **上古漢語 (OC)**：基本語序為 SVO（主謂賓），但有較高靈活性。疑問代詞 (interrogatives: 誰、何) 作賓語時必須前置到動詞前（例如：「吾誰欺？」= 我欺騙誰？）。擁有複雜的「之」字結構用來取消句子獨立性（名詞化標記）。語氣詞體系極其發達（乎、邪、哉）來表達微妙的情態。
  - _OC_: Basic word order was SVO, but with high flexibility. Interrogative pronouns (誰 who, 何 what) MUST be fronted before the verb when serving as objects (e.g., 吾誰欺？ = "Whom do I deceive?" literally "I whom deceive?"). It possessed a complex 之 (zhī) structure to nominalize clauses. The particle system for moods (乎, 邪, 哉) was highly elaborate.
- **中古漢語 (MC)**：語序固定為嚴格的 SVO (strictly fixed)。失去上古「之」的複雜用法。動態助詞 (aspectual particles: 了 perfective, 著 continuous) 開始在口語中萌芽，標誌著朝向分析語（孤立語）更進一步。「被」字被動式（被動標記）在口語中廣泛使用。
  - _MC_: Word order strictly fixed into SVO. It lost the complex usage of the Old Chinese 之 (zhī). Aspectual particles (了 for perfective, 著 for continuous) began to bud in colloquial speech, marking a further step toward an analytic/isolating language. The 被 (bèi) passive construction became widely used in speech.
- **文言文 (LC)**：嚴格遵循上古語法模板 (rigidly follows OC grammar)。它強制使用賓語前置（「唯利是圖」= 只圖利）和定語後置。它徹底拒絕 (absolutely rejects) 中古新生的口語助詞（了、著、過）。句法結構凝結為駢文（四六文）的固定對仗公式。
  - _LC_: Rigidly follows Old Chinese grammatical templates. It enforces object-fronting ("唯利是圖" = "only profit is sought") and post-positioned modifiers. It absolutely rejects Middle Chinese's new colloquial particles (了, 著, 過). Syntactic structures crystallize into fixed parallel couplet formulas (Four-Six Prose).
- 實例：表達「把這個給大王看」(Show this to the King)：
  - 上古 OC: 示王 (直接動詞+間接賓語，無介詞) => Show King.
  - 中古口語 MC spoken: 與大王看 (引入介詞「與」= give/to, 類似 "to show to the King").
  - 文言文 LC writing: 示之於王 (嚴格使用代詞「之」和介詞「於」，語法工整古典，絕不使用白話「與」).

### 參、三者的本質關係 (The Essential Relationship) — 完整英譯

- 文言文 ≠ 上古漢語 (LC ≠ OC)：文言文只是上古口語的「書面凍結版」(frozen written snapshot)。真正的上古口語含有我們永遠無法真實發音的複輔音（*kl-, *pl-）。用現代普通話朗讀《論語》，孔子本人會完全聽不懂。
  - LC is NOT OC: LC is merely a "frozen written snapshot" of Old Chinese colloquialism. The real OC contained consonant clusters (*kl-, *pl-) that we can never truly pronounce. If you recite the Analects in Modern Mandarin, Confucius himself would understand absolutely nothing.
- 文言文 ≠ 中古漢語 (LC ≠ MC)：唐詩（如李白、杜甫）雖然用文言詞彙書寫，但其平仄格律 (tonal prosody) 是嚴格基於中古漢語的四聲（平仄）設計的。然而，唐宋的「古文運動」散文（如韓愈）則故意拒絕中古口語，刻意追趕上古句法。
  - LC is NOT MC: Tang poems (Li Bai, Du Fu) are written with literary lexicon, yet their tonal prosody (level-oblique rules) were strictly designed based on the Four Tones of Middle Chinese. However, Tang-Song "Ancient Prose Movement" essays (Han Yu) deliberately reject MC colloquialism and artificially chase OC syntax.
- 經典比喻 (Classic Analogy)：
  - 上古漢語 = 古典拉丁語的口語街頭版 (the Vulgar Latin spoken on Roman streets)。
  - 中古漢語 = 通俗拉丁語 (the transitional vernacular evolving into Italian dialects)。
  - 文言文 = 西塞羅的古典拉丁語書面語 (Cicero's polished Classical Latin) — 後代歐洲學者只會用於學術寫作，絕不會用於買菜或吵架。

#### 肆、深遠影響 (Profound Influences) — 每一條皆有完整英文

#### A. 對中國本身的影響 (Impact on China Proper)

1. 文化統一與連續性 (Cultural Unity and Continuity)：文言文作為統一的書面媒介，使得操著完全無法互通方言（如吳語、粵語、閩語）的士大夫階級能夠通過筆談無礙溝通。這套系統讓中華文明成為全球唯一延續四千年未曾中斷的古典文明。
   - Cultural Unity: LC served as a unified written medium, enabling literati who spoke mutually unintelligible dialects (Wu, Yue, Min) to communicate seamlessly via brush-talk. This system made Chinese civilization the only classical civilization globally to survive continuously for over four millennia without rupture.
2. 科舉與階級流動 (Imperial Exams and Social Mobility)：文言文的掌握門檻極高（需背誦數十萬字上古經典），因此創造了「士紳階級」(Scholar-gentry class)。然而，這種對上古口語的模仿也導致了嚴重的「言文分離」(diglossia) —— 書面系統與民間口語（中古/近代白話）徹底分道揚鑣。
   - Exams and Mobility: Mastering LC required a tremendously high threshold (memorizing hundreds of thousands of characters of OC classics), thus creating the scholar-gentry class. However, this imitation of ancient speech caused severe diglossia — the written system completely diverged from the folk vernacular (MC/Modern colloquial).
3. 五四白話文運動 (May Fourth Vernacular Movement)：1919年的新文化運動徹底廢除了文言文作為官方書面語的地位，轉向「現代白話文」（基於中古及現代北方官話的口語）。影響 (Effect)：知識全面大眾化，識字率飆升；但副作用是，現代中國人若未經專業訓練，已無法流暢閱讀唐代以前的原始古籍。
   - May Fourth: The 1919 New Culture Movement completely abolished LC as the official written language, shifting to "Modern Vernacular Chinese" (based on MC and modern Northern Mandarin colloquialism). Effect: Mass democratization of knowledge and skyrocketing literacy; however, the side effect is that modern Chinese cannot read pre-Tang ancient texts fluently without specialized training.

#### B. 對東亞文明（漢字文化圈）的影響 (Impact on East Asia / Sinosphere)

1. 日本 (Japan)：文言文（日本稱「漢文」）是江戶時代以前日本的官方書面語。影響 (Impact)：日語至今保存兩套中古漢語音讀（吳音 Go-on 與漢音 Kan-on）。現代日語中的「四字熟語」(yojijukugo) 幾乎全是純粹的文言語法結構（如「森羅萬象」）。
   - Japan: LC (Kanbun) was Japan's official written language until the Edo period. Impact: Modern Japanese retains two full sets of Sino-Japanese readings (Go-on and Kan-on) which are direct reflexes of Middle Chinese. Modern Japanese 4-character idioms are almost all pure LC syntactic structures.
2. 韓國 (Korea)：朝鮮半島使用文言文（吏讀/Idush）長達千年。世宗大王雖創制了諺文（Hangul），但官方文書直至1894年甲午更張才廢止文言。影響 (Impact)：韓語中超過60%的詞彙是漢字詞（Sino-Korean），且其發音嚴格對應中古漢語的入聲韻尾（如 -k, -l），這是中古音的直接化石。
   - Korea: The Korean Peninsula used LC (Idu script) for a millennium. Though King Sejong created Hangul, official documents used LC until the Gabo Reform of 1894. Impact: Over 60% of Korean vocabulary is Sino-Korean, and its pronunciations strictly preserve MC entering-tone codas (-k, -l) — a direct fossil of Tang phonology.
3. 越南 (Vietnam)：越南科舉制度完全仿效中國，直至1919年阮朝才廢止。影響 (Impact)：越語中的「漢越音」(Sino-Vietnamese) 保存了中古漢語最完整的聲母體系（例如「師」讀 sư，完美保存了舌頭音），使其成為歷史語言學家復原中古音的重要鑰匙。
   - Vietnam: Vietnam's imperial exams followed China's model and were only abolished in 1919 under the Nguyễn dynasty. Impact: Sino-Vietnamese pronunciations preserve the most complete initial consonant system of Middle Chinese (e.g., 師 reads as sư, perfectly retaining the tongue-tip sound), making it a crucial key for historical linguists reconstructing MC.

#### C. 對現代標準漢語（普通話）的深層遺產 (Deep Legacy in Modern Mandarin)

1. 成語與典故 (Idioms and Allusions)：現代白話文中的成語（如「刻舟求劍」carving a boat for a sword, 「朝三暮四」three in the morning, four in the evening）全部是上古／文言語法的活化石 (living fossils of OC/LC syntax)——保留了賓語前置、省略主語等特徵。不懂文言文，就無法透徹理解現代書面語的深層邏輯。
   - Idioms: All modern Mandarin idioms are living fossils of OC/LC syntax — retaining object-fronting, subject-ellipsis, and archaic word-choices. Without knowing LC, one cannot grasp the deep logic of modern formal writing.
2. 簡化字的文字學邏輯 (Philological Logic of Simplified Characters)：許多簡化字其實是「回歸上古初文」(reverting to the OC primitive graphs)。例如「雲」（cloud）簡化為「云」，後者其實是甲骨文中表示「說」的本字，後來被假借為雲，再後來才加上「雨」部。簡化是文字學上的返祖。
   - Simplified characters: Many simplified characters are actually a "return to Old Chinese primitive graphs." For example, 雲 (cloud) simplified to 云, which was originally the oracle-bone pictograph for "to speak," later phonetically borrowed for "cloud," and only later received the "rain" radical. Simplification is a philological atavism.
3. 語音層次與方言化石 (Phonetic Strata and Dialectal Fossils)：現代普通話雖然失去了入聲（-p, -t, -k），但完全保留了中古漢語的「平仄」對立體系（這是詩詞格律的基礎）。而南方方言（粵語、閩南語、客家話）則完整保留了中古的入聲韻尾。這證明了現代漢語方言不是上古漢語的後代，而是中古漢語的不同後裔分支。
   - Phonetic Strata: Modern Mandarin has lost the entering tone (-p, -t, -k), yet it fully preserves the MC "level-oblique" opposition system (the basis of Tang poetic meter). Southern dialects (Cantonese, Hokkien, Hakka) have perfectly preserved MC entering-tone codas. This proves that modern Chinese dialects are NOT descendants of Old Chinese, but rather different descendant branches of Middle Chinese.

### 總結 (Final Conclusion) — 完整中英詩意對照

- **上古漢語 (OC)** 是源頭 (the Origin)——一門已經滅絕的有聲口語，我們只能透過比較法（comparative method）去構擬猜測它的聲音，永遠無法親耳聽聞。
  - OC is the Origin — an extinct spoken tongue that we can only reconstruct through comparative methods but can never truly hear.
- **中古漢語 (MC)** 是主幹 (the Trunk)——隋唐盛世下的繁華市井之聲，它規定了現代所有漢語方言以及日韓越外來漢字音的基本聲韻框架。
  - MC is the Trunk — the bustling street-voice of the Sui-Tang golden age, which set the fundamental phonological framework for all modern dialects and Sino-Xenic pronunciations.
- **文言文 (LC)** 是琥珀 (the Amber)——一種永恆不變的書寫載體，它將三千年前的上古句法凍結在紙面上，成為東亞儒家文明共享的基因密碼（genetic code），跨越時空，凝聚了整個東亞知識分子共同體。
  - LC is the Amber — an eternally unchangeable written medium that froze Old Chinese syntax onto paper three thousand years ago, serving as the shared genetic code of East Asian Confucian civilization, transcending time and space to unite the entire East Asian intellectual community.
