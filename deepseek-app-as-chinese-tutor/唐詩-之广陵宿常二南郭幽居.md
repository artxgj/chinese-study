## English Answer

**High-level summary**  
"**Staying at Chang the Second's Secluded South-City Dwelling on My Way to Guangling**" (〈之广陵宿常二南郭幽居〉) is a five-character ancient verse (五言古诗) written by **Li Bai** in **754 AD** [1], during his later years of wandering. On his way to Guangling (modern-day Yangzhou) [2], he lodged at the southern-city retreat of his friend **Chang the Second** (常二). The poem depicts a tranquil, hermit-like dwelling that recalls Tao Yuanming's Peach Blossom Spring, expressing both admiration for the friend's unworldly character and the bittersweet melancholy of parting. It is a quiet, intimate work—less spectacular than Li Bai's famous dream-visions, but no less touching in its sincerity and poetic precision.

李白  

綠水接柴門，有如桃花源。

**Detailed Explanation**

**1. Background and Context** [1]  
This poem was written in **754 AD**, when Li Bai was in his fifties and wandering extensively through eastern and southern China. **Guangling** (廣陵) is the ancient name for **Yangzhou** in Jiangsu Province [2]. The title tells us that on his way there, Li Bai stayed overnight at the secluded city-south dwelling of a friend surnamed **Chang**, who was the second son in his family (hence "常二" – Chang the Second). The poem captures a single evening of friendship, wine, and poetry, set against a backdrop of rustic tranquility—and looks ahead to the sorrow of departure the next morning.

| 綠水接柴門   | Lǜ shuǐ jiē chái mén    | Green water reaches the rustic wicker gate.                  |
| 有如桃花源   | yǒu rú táo huā yuán     | It is just like the Peach Blossom Spring.                    |
| 忘憂或假草   | wàng yōu huò jiǎ cǎo    | Perhaps he plants herbs to forget his cares.                 |

| 暝色湖上來   | míng sè hú shàng lái    | Dusk colors rise from the lake.                              |
| 微雨飛南軒   | wēi yǔ fēi nán xuān     | A light rain drifts across the southern veranda.             |

**3. Key Literary Devices and Allusions** [1]

- **桃花源 (Peach Blossom Spring)**: This alludes to Tao Yuanming's famous utopian vision—a hidden paradise of peace and simplicity. By comparing his friend's humble cottage to this ideal, Li Bai elevates Chang the Second's hermit-like life to the highest moral plane.
- **萱草 (daylily / forget-sorrow herb)**: In classical Chinese lore, the daylily (萱草 / 忘憂草) was believed to make one forget one's worries. The "clusters of daylilies" in the courtyard suggest that the friend has deliberately cultivated a life of detachment from worldly troubles.
- **楊園 (poplar garden)**: This is a classical reference from the *Book of Songs* (《詩經·小雅》), lending the scene a layer of ancient cultural resonance.
- **「深為江海言」**: The friend's parting words are "as deep as rivers and seas"—a powerful metaphor for sincere, weighty friendship that will sustain Li Bai on his journey.

**4. Artistic Features and Emotional Core** [1]

- **From scenery to sentiment**: The poem moves gracefully from external landscape (green water, dusk, rain, birds) to internal emotion (cherishing the farewell, remembering the wine). The natural imagery is not just decorative; it mirrors the peaceful, unworldly character of the host.
- **Restrained yet deep feeling**: Unlike Li Bai's more flamboyant poems, this one is quiet and controlled. The final line—*"Alone I shall remember this emptied wine cup"*—is understated but carries immense weight. It suggests that the memory of this one evening will stay with the poet long after he has traveled on.
- **Precision of observation**: The poem's description of the scene is remarkably precise: the green water reaching the gate, dusk rising from the lake, rain flying across the southern window. These small details create a vivid, intimate portrait of a single moment in time.

**5. Why This Poem Matters**  
This poem shows a different side of Li Bai—not the ecstatic dreamer riding dragons through the sky, but a warm, reflective friend savoring a quiet evening with a companion. It reminds us that Li Bai's genius was not limited to grandiosity; he could also capture the subtle beauty of ordinary human connection with equal mastery. The poem is a testament to the enduring power of friendship, poetry, and wine—three things Li Bai valued above all else.

**詳細解析**

**1. 創作背景** [1]  
此詩作於**天寶十三載（754年）**，當時李白已年過半百，正處於晚年漫遊時期。**廣陵**是唐代揚州的古稱 [2]。詩題表明，李白在前往廣陵的途中，投宿於一位姓**常**、排行第二的友人在城南的幽居。全詩記錄了一個與友人詩酒相酬、即將離別的夜晚，場景恬淡而情感真摯。

**3. 重要典故與意象** [1]

- **桃花源**：典出東晉陶淵明〈桃花源詩並記〉，代表一個與世隔絕、安寧祥和的理想世界。李白將友人的簡樸居所比作桃花源，是對其超然品格的高度讚美。
- **萱草（忘憂草）**：古人認為萱草可以使人忘卻憂愁。庭院中「滿院羅叢萱」暗示友人刻意選擇了一種超脫世俗、忘卻紛擾的生活方式。
- **楊園**：出自《詩經·小雅》「楊園之道」，引用經典為全詩增添了古雅的韻味。
- **「深為江海言」**：以江海之深比喻友人臨別囑咐的真摯與厚重，意象宏大而情感深沉。

**4. 藝術特色與情感核心** [1]

- **由景入情，層層遞進**：全詩從外部景物（綠水、暮色、細雨、歸鳥）寫起，逐步轉入內心的情感（珍惜離別、懷念酒宴）。自然景物不只是背景，更是主人恬淡心境的投射。
- **含蓄而深沉的情感**：與李白許多奔放熱烈的詩作不同，這首詩風格內斂、節制。末句「獨憶此傾樽」語氣平淡，卻承載了極大的情感分量——暗示這一夜的記憶將在漫長的旅途上持續迴響。
- **觀察精準，筆觸細膩**：詩中對場景的描寫極為精確：綠水直抵柴門、暮色從湖面升起的動態、細雨斜飛南軒的角度。這些細節共同構成一幅生動而親切的瞬間畫面。

**5. 這首詩的價值**  
此詩展現了李白不同於「飛流直下三千尺」的另一面——不是乘龍飛天的狂放詩仙，而是一個溫暖、細膩的摯友，靜靜品味著與友人共度的樸素時光。它提醒我們，李白的才華並不限於宏大的想像；他同樣能以精湛的筆觸捕捉平凡人情中的微妙之美。全詩是友誼、詩歌與美酒三者交織的見證——而這三樣，正是李白一生最珍視的事物。
