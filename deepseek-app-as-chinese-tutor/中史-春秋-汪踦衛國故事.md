# 汪踦衛國故事詳解

# The Story of Wang Ji Defending His Country: A Detailed Explanation

---

## 一、引言 | Introduction

**「汪踦衛國」** 是中國春秋時期一個著名的歷史典故，記載於《左傳·哀公十一年》與《禮記·檀弓下》。這個典故講述了一位名叫汪踦（亦作汪錡、汪锜）的魯國兒童，在國家危難之際挺身而出，參與抗擊齊國入侵的戰鬥並壯烈犧牲的故事。後世以「汪踦衛國」作為兒童英勇救國的典型事例。

**"Wang Ji Defending His Country"** is a famous historical allusion from China's Spring and Autumn Period, recorded in the Zuo Zhuan (Duke Ai, Year 11) and the Book of Rites (Tan Gong Xia). This allusion tells the story of a child of the State of Lu named Wang Ji (also written as Wang Qi) who stepped forward during a national crisis, participated in the battle against an invading army from the State of Qi, and died a heroic death. Later generations used "Wang Ji Defending His Country" as a typical example of children's heroic patriotism.

---

## 二、歷史背景 | Historical Background

此典故發生於**魯哀公十一年（公元前484年）** 。當時齊國攻打魯國，雙方戰於**郎**（今山東省境內）。齊國是大國、強國，魯國是弱國，齊國經常攻打和侵略魯國。

This allusion took place in **the 11th year of Duke Ai of Lu (484 BCE)** . At that time, the State of Qi attacked the State of Lu, and the two sides fought at **Lang** (in present-day Shandong Province). Qi was a large and powerful state, while Lu was a weak state, and Qi frequently attacked and invaded Lu.

---

## 三、原文與譯文 | Original Text and Translation

### 《禮記·檀弓下》原文 | Original Text from Book of Rites, Tan Gong Xia

> 戰于郎，公叔禺人遇負杖入保者息，曰：「使之雖病也，任之雖重也，君子不能為謀也，士弗能死也，不可！我則既言矣。」與其鄰童汪踦往，皆死焉。魯人欲勿殤童汪踦，問於仲尼。仲尼曰：「能執干戈以衛社稷，雖欲勿殤也，不亦可乎！」

### 白話譯文 | Vernacular Translation

在郎地作戰時，公叔禺人遇見扛著兵器進城避難的疲憊百姓，感嘆道：「雖然役使百姓使他們勞苦，雖然徵發賦稅使他們負擔沉重，但君子不能謀劃良策，士人不能為國捐軀，這是不行的！我既然說了這話，就要做到。」於是他與鄰居的童子汪踦一同前往作戰，兩人都戰死了。魯國人不想以殤禮（未成年之禮）來埋葬童子汪踦，便去請教孔子。孔子說：「能夠拿起武器保衛國家，即使不想用殤禮來對待他，不也是可以的嗎！」

During the battle at Lang, Gongshu Yuren encountered weary people carrying weapons into the city for refuge and sighed: "Although the people are made to work hard and bear heavy burdens, if the gentlemen cannot devise good strategies and the scholars cannot die for their country, that is unacceptable! Since I have said this, I must act on it." Then he went with his neighbor's child Wang Ji to join the battle, and both died. The people of Lu did not wish to bury the child Wang Ji with the funeral rites for minors, so they consulted Confucius. Confucius said: "If one can take up arms to defend the altars of the state, then even if one does not wish to treat him as a minor in death, is that not also acceptable!"

## 四、故事詳述 | Detailed Story

### 4\.1 國難當頭，少年立志 | In the Face of National Crisis, a Young Boy Resolves to Act

魯國軍隊抵擋不住齊國的進攻，迅速敗下陣來。魯國士人公叔禺人（亦稱公為）十分擔憂國家的安危。他身邊有一位十來歲的小書童，名叫汪踦。汪踦年紀雖小，卻頗有見識。他看到公叔禺人不住嘆氣，便問：「主人，您是否為魯軍戰敗而傷心？」公叔禺人嘆道：「齊國經常挑起戰爭，魯國人真感到羞愧啊！」

The Lu army could not withstand the Qi offensive and quickly suffered defeat. The Lu scholar Gongshu Yuren (also known as Gong Wei) was deeply worried about the nation's safety. By his side was a young book attendant of about ten years old named Wang Ji. Though young, Wang Ji was quite perceptive. Seeing Gongshu Yuren sighing repeatedly, he asked: "Master, are you grieving over the defeat of the Lu army?" Gongshu Yuren replied: "Qi frequently starts wars—how shameful it is for the people of Lu!"

### 4\.2 奔赴戰場，視死如歸 | Heading to the Battlefield, Facing Death with Equanimity

汪踦心中也如火烧般難受，便提議出城查看戰場形勢。公叔禺人備車出城，只見魯國士兵丟盔棄甲、傷痕累累地往後撤退。汪踦看到魯軍慘敗的景象，熱血沸騰。公叔禺人勸他：「孩子，你年紀還小，快回城去吧！」汪踦卻跳上一輛戰車，大喊道：「我也要上戰場殺敵！國家要滅亡了，我活著還有什麼意思！」

Wang Ji's heart burned with anguish, and he suggested going outside the city to survey the battlefield. Gongshu Yuren prepared a carriage and went out, only to see Lu soldiers retreating in disarray—some without armor, others wounded and bleeding. Seeing the miserable scene of defeat, Wang Ji's blood boiled with passion. Gongshu Yuren urged him: "Child, you are still young—hurry back to the city!" But Wang Ji leaped onto an abandoned war chariot and shouted: "I too will go to the battlefield to kill the enemy! If the nation is to perish, what meaning is there in living!"

### 4\.3 激勵士氣，壯烈犧牲 | Inspiring Morale and Dying a Heroic Death

公叔禺人深受鼓舞，也跳上戰車，高喊：「為了保衛國家，我們要和敵人決一死戰！」敗退的魯國將士看見一個成年人揮舞長槍、一個十來歲的孩子高舉大刀衝回敵陣，無不被震撼。士兵們紛紛掉頭，隨汪踦的戰車殺了回去。魯軍越戰越勇，最終齊軍全線崩潰，敗陣而逃。魯軍取得勝利後，將士們找到了汪踦——他手握帶血的戰刀，已經犧牲了。

Gongshu Yuren was deeply inspired and also leaped onto the war chariot, shouting: "To defend our country, we must fight the enemy to the death!"The retreating Lu soldiers were deeply moved when they saw an adult wielding a spear and a child of about ten raising a great sword as they charged back toward the enemy lines. The soldiers turned around one after another and followed Wang Ji's chariot back into battle. The Lu army fought with increasing valor, and eventually the Qi army collapsed completely and fled in defeat. After the Lu army achieved victory, the soldiers found Wang Ji—he was still gripping his bloodstained battle blade, already dead.

### 4\.4 破格葬禮，孔子嘉許 | An Exceptional Funeral and Confucius's Approval

少年英雄汪踦的事蹟迅速傳遍魯國。然而根據當時禮法，未成年人去世只能以殤禮（簡化的葬禮）安葬。魯國人不想以殤禮對待這位為國捐軀的少年，便去請教孔子。孔子嚴肅地說：「能執干戈以衛社稷，雖欲勿殤也，不亦可乎！」——如果一個小孩子能像大人一樣衝鋒陷陣、殺敵立功，做了大人要做的事，那麼為什麼不能像對待大人一樣對待我們的小英雄呢？於是魯國破除舊俗，為小英雄汪踦舉行了隆重的成人葬禮。

The deeds of the young hero Wang Ji quickly spread throughout the State of Lu. However, according to the rituals of the time, minors who died could only be buried with funeral rites for minors (simplified burial ceremonies). The people of Lu did not wish to bury this young martyr with minor's rites, so they consulted Confucius. Confucius said solemnly: "If one can take up arms to defend the altars of the state, then even if one does not wish to treat him as a minor in death, is that not also acceptable!"—If a child could charge into battle and perform meritorious deeds like an adult, doing what adults should do, then why should the little hero not be treated like an adult?Thus, the people of Lu broke with convention and held a grand adult funeral for the young hero Wang Ji.

## 五、孔子的評論解析 | Analysis of Confucius's Commentary

孔子對汪踦的評價是全文的關鍵：「**能執干戈以衛社稷，雖欲勿殤也，不亦可乎！**」

Confucius's assessment of Wang Ji is the key to understanding this story: "**If one can take up arms to defend the altars of the state, then even if one does not wish to treat him as a minor in death, is that not also acceptable!**"

這段話有兩層重要含義：

This statement carries two layers of significant meaning:

1\. 「**社稷」（國家）高於禮法形式**：孔子認為，一個兒童若能保家衛國，其貢獻已超越了年齡的界限。禮法的根本精神在於彰顯價值與美德，而非拘泥於形式。

2\. **「干戈」（武器）象徵責任與勇氣**：汪踦雖年幼，卻主動承擔了保衛國家的責任。孔子以此肯定了他的勇氣與愛國精神，也為破格以成人禮安葬提供了理論依據。

3\. **The "altars of the state" (the nation) transcend ritual formality**: Confucius believed that if a child could defend the nation, his contribution had already surpassed the boundaries of age. The fundamental spirit of ritual is to honor value and virtue, not to rigidly adhere to form.

4\. **"Arms" (weapons) symbolize responsibility and courage**: Though young, Wang Ji voluntarily assumed the responsibility of defending the nation. Confucius affirmed his courage and patriotic spirit, thereby providing the theoretical basis for burying him with adult rites.

六、文化影響與現代意義 | Cultural Influence and Modern Significance

### 6\.1 成語典故 | Idiomatic Allusion

「汪踦衛國」後來成為一個固定成語，用以**稱揚兒童英勇救國**的事蹟。其拼音為 **wāng jī wèi guó**（亦讀作 wāng yǐ wèi guó），英文可譯為 **"child's patriotism"** 。

"Wang Ji Defending His Country" later became a fixed idiom, used to **praise children's heroic patriotism**. Its pinyin is **wāng jī wèi guó** (also pronounced wāng yǐ wèi guó), and it can be translated into English as **"child's patriotism"**.

### 6\.2 文學引用 | Literary Reference

近代作家**魯迅**在《花邊文學·小童擋駕》中曾引用此典故：「即使到了緊要關頭，也還是什麼『木蘭從軍』、『汪踦衛國』，要推出『女子與小人』去搪塞的。」

The modern writer Lu Xun cited this allusion in his work _Flower-Edge Literature: Little Children Blocking the Way_: "Even at critical moments, it's still about 'Mulan Joining the Army' and 'Wang Ji Defending His Country'—pushing out 'women and children' to fend things off."

### 6\.3 現代啟示 | Modern Relevance

汪踦衛國的故事至今仍具有深刻的啟示意義：

&bull; **愛國不分年齡**：保家衛國是每一個公民的責任，不分長幼。

&bull; **勇氣與擔當**：在危難時刻，勇於承擔責任的精神值得永遠讚頌。

&bull; **禮儀的精神實質**：真正的禮儀在於對德行與貢獻的尊重，而非形式的束縛。

The story of Wang Ji Defending His Country still holds profound relevance today:

&bull; **Patriotism knows no age**: Defending one's country is the responsibility of every citizen, regardless of age.

&bull; **Courage and responsibility**: The spirit of stepping up to take responsibility in times of crisis is worthy of eternal praise.

&bull; **The essential spirit of ritual**: True ritual lies in respect for virtue and contribution, not in the constraints of form.

## 七、總結 | Summary

汪踦，一位春秋時期的魯國少年，在齊國入侵、國家危亡之際，毅然拿起武器奔赴戰場，與公叔禺人一同壯烈犧牲。魯國人本想以殤禮安葬他，但孔子一句「能執干戈以衛社稷」肯定了這位少年的功績，使其得以享受成人禮遇。這個故事不僅被載入《左傳》和《禮記》兩部經典，更成為中華文化中兒童愛國精神的典範，流傳千古。

Wang Ji, a young boy from the State of Lu during the Spring and Autumn Period, resolutely took up arms and rushed to the battlefield when Qi invaded and the nation was in peril, dying a heroic death alongside Gongshu Yuren. The people of Lu initially intended to bury him with minor's rites, but Confucius's words—"If one can take up arms to defend the altars of the state"—affirmed the young boy's achievement, allowing him to receive adult funeral honors. This story was not only recorded in two classics—the Zuo Zhuan and the Book of Rites—but also became a model of children's patriotic spirit in Chinese culture, passed down through the ages.
