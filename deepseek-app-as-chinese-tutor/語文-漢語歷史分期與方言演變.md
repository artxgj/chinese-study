## 第二部分：三大核心區別（語音・語法・詞彙）| Part 2: Three Core Differences

### 1\. 語音系統 | Phonology

&bull; **上古**：複輔音（kl-、pl-）；聲調未成熟；濁塞音韻尾（-b/-d/-g）。<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;_Old: consonant clusters; incipient tones; voiced stop codas (-b/-d/-g)._

&bull; 中古：複輔音消失；四聲（平上去入）確立；鼻音（-m/-n/-ŋ）與清塞音（-p/-t/-k）韻尾分明。<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;_Middle: clusters lost; four tones fixed; nasals (-m/-n/-ŋ) and voiceless stops (-p/-t/-k) distinct._

&bull; 近代：入聲韻尾弱化或消失；翹舌音（zh/ch/sh）分化；-m 開始併入 -n。<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;_Pre-modern: entering codas weaken or vanish; retroflexes emerge; -m merges into -n._

&bull; 現代：入聲完全消失（普通話）；僅存四聲；-m 全併入 -n。<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;_Modern: entering tone lost in standard; only 4 tones; -m fully merged into -n._

### 2\. 語法結構 | Grammar

&bull; 上古：單音節為主；虛詞（之、其、於）極發達；代詞有格位區別。<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;_Old: mainly monosyllabic; rich function words; pronominal case distinctions._

&bull; 中古：雙音節化加速；量詞（個、條）湧現；動態助詞（了、著）萌芽。<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;_Middle: disyllabification accelerates; measure words proliferate; aspect markers emerge._

&bull; 近代：動態助詞（了、著、過）成熟；被動式（被、叫）及補語結構成常態。<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;_Pre-modern: aspect markers mature; passives and complex complements standardize._

&bull; 現代：基本沿襲近代語法；書面語增加歐化長定語。<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;_Modern: largely follows Pre-modern; Europeanized long attributives in writing._

### 3\. 詞彙與構詞 | Lexicon and Word Formation

&bull; 上古：單純詞為主；派生靠清濁聲母交替（使動／自動）。<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;_Old: root words; derivation via voicing alternation (causative vs. intransitive)._

&bull; 中古：梵文佛教借詞（菩薩、涅槃）；雙音複合詞大增。<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;_Middle: Sanskrit Buddhist loans; disyllabic compounds surge._

&bull; 近代：白話口語詞爆發（怎麼樣）；蒙滿借詞（站、胡同）。<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;_Pre-modern: colloquial terms explode; Mongolian/Manchu loanwords appear._

&bull; 現代：日語借詞（社會、經濟）；英語外來詞（咖啡）；雙音節絕對優勢。<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;_Modern: Japanese and English loans dominate; disyllabic forms prevail._

## 第三部分：對現代普通話與各大方言的深層影響 | Part 3: Deep Influence on Mandarin and Dialects

### 對普通話 | On Mandarin

&bull; 語音：直接繼承近代漢語（元明官話） 音系。<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;_Phonology: directly inherits Pre-modern (Yuan-Ming Guanhua) sound system._

&bull; 語法：「把」字句、了著過，全來自近代白話。<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;_Grammar: 把-construction and aspects come from Pre-modern vernacular._

&bull; 詞彙：口語詞（吃／走）繼承近代；書面雅詞（食／行）繼承文言。<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;_Lexicon: colloquial terms from Pre-modern; formal synonyms from Wenyan._

### 對各大方言（中古視角深層剖析）| On Major Dialects (Middle Chinese Perspective)

&bull; 閩語 (Min) —— 上古「活化石」：漢代前已分化；保留輕唇讀重唇（飛 → p-）、「無」讀 m-；底層屬上古系統。<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;_Min – Old Chinese "living fossil": split pre-Han; labiodentals read as bilabials (飛 p-); 無 m-; core is Old Chinese._

&bull; 吳語 (Wu) —— 中古「博物館」：完整保留全濁聲母（/b/,/d/,/g/）；陰陽調類對立；聲調多達8個，直承南朝雅言。<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;_Wu – Middle Chinese "museum": fully preserves voiced initials; Yin-Yang tonal register; up to 8 tones; direct descendant of Southern Dynasties standard._

&bull; 粵語 (Yue) —— 中古「韻尾倉庫」：完整保留 -m/-n/-ŋ 與 -p/-t/-k；九聲六調；拒絕腭化（家 → gaa，非 jia）。<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;_Yue – Middle Chinese "coda warehouse": fully preserves nasals and stop codas; 9 tones/6 pitches; resists palatalization (家 = gaa)._

&bull; 客語 (Hakka) & 贛語 (Gan) —— 北朝移民後裔：全濁聲母今讀送氣清音；保留大量中古詞彙；屬中古北朝系統。<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;_Hakka & Gan – Northern Dynasties descendants: voiced initials → voiceless aspirated; retain Middle Chinese lexicon; belong to Northern Dynasties substrate._

&bull; 湘語 (Xiang) —— 南朝關聯：老湘語仍保留全濁聲母（如雙峰話）；新湘語受官話侵蝕。<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;_Xiang – linked to Southern Dynasties: Old Xiang still preserves voiced initials (e.g., Shuangfeng); New Xiang eroded by Mandarin._

&bull; 官話 (Mandarin) —— 變革先鋒：濁音清化；入聲丟失；-m→-n；腭化（k→j/q/x）；直接繼承近代漢語。<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;_Mandarin – radical innovator: devoicing; entering tone lost; -m→-n; palatalization (k→j/q/x); directly inherits Pre-modern Chinese._

## 第四部分：文言文與這三期漢語的關係 | Part 4: Relationship of Classical Chinese (Wenyan) to These Stages

**核心定論**：文言文 **不是** 口語，而是 **以上古漢語為藍本、經歷代模仿固化的跨時代書面語**。<br>
_Core: Wenyan is NOT speech. It is a trans-temporal written system modeled on Old Chinese and fossilized through imitation._

#### 1\. 時間錯位：文言語法漢代已定型；唐宋人寫文言時，口語已是近代漢語雛形（雙層語言）。
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;_Temporal mismatch: Wenyan grammar fixed by Han; Tang-Song writers spoke early Pre-modern (diglossia)._
#### 2\. 功能分工：文言用於科舉／公文；白話（記錄口語）用於小說／戲曲。近代漢語是白話文的直接源頭。
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;_Functional division: Wenyan for exams/edicts; Baihua for novels/dramas. Pre-modern is the direct source of written Baihua._
#### 3\. 現代滲透：成語、典故、四字格（功成名就）來自文言；書面語「之、其、於」是文言遺產。
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;_Modern infiltration: idioms and four-character phrases come from Wenyan; literary particles (之,其,於) are its legacy._
#### 4\. 方言「化石」：粵語「食／飲／行」比普通話「吃／喝／走」古老，但方言≠文言——文言是書面綜合體。
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;_Dialectal fossils: Cantonese 食/飲/行 are older than Mandarin 吃/喝/走, but dialects ≠ Wenyan – Wenyan is a written synthesis._

## 最終總結 | Final Summary

上古是漢語的根系（字源與古義）；中古是聲調與方言分化的核心濾鏡（多數方言為中古某一分支的直系後裔）；近代是現代普通話語音與白話語法的直接前身；現代在繼承近代基礎上，吸收日、英借詞與歐化句式。
文言文不是口語，而是以先秦語法為藍本的書面脊梁，至今透過成語、典故與雅詞持續影響現代書寫。
各地方言是「歷史活標本」：閩語指向上古，吳語鎖住中古濁音，粵語封存韻尾，客贛連結北朝移民，官話展現最激進的變革。

_Old Chinese is the root (etymology & ancient meanings). Middle Chinese is the key prism for tones and dialectal divergence (most dialects are direct offshoots of specific Middle Chinese branches). Pre-modern Chinese is the direct ancestor of Mandarin phonology and vernacular grammar. Modern Chinese inherits Pre-modern while adding Japanese/English loans and Europeanized syntax.
Wenyan is not speech but a written spine based on Old Chinese, still influencing modern writing via idioms, allusions, and formal registers.
Dialects are living fossils: Min points to Old Chinese, Wu locks in Middle Chinese voiced initials, Yue preserves codas, Hakka/Gan link to Northern migrants, and Mandarin shows the most radical evolution._
