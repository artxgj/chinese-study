# 二里头遗址 中英对照完整详解

# Erlitou Site — A Complete Bilingual Guide

## 一、概况 | I. Overview

### 中文
二里头遗址，又称“夏墟”，位于河南省洛阳市偃师区，是东亚大陆最早的广域王权国家遗存，迄今为止可确认的中国最早的王朝都城遗址，东亚地区最早进入青铜时代的庞大都邑。因其在中国文明史上的核心地位，被誉为“最早的中国”、“华夏第一王都”和“中华文明总进程的核心与引领者”。

### English
The Erlitou Site, also known as the “Xia Ruins” (Summer Capital Ruins), is located in Yanshi District, Luoyang City, Henan Province. It represents the earliest remains of a vast territorial state in East Asia, the earliest confirmed capital city of a Chinese dynasty to date, and the earliest large-scale urban settlement in East Asia to enter the Bronze Age. Due to its central role in the history of Chinese civilization, it has been hailed as the “Earliest China,” the “First Royal Capital of China,” and the “Core and Leader of the Overall Progress of Chinese Civilization”.

## 二、地理位置与规模 | II. Geographical Location & Scale

### 中文


&bull; **位置**：遗址位于河南省西部洛河下游，洛阳市偃师区二里头村周围。南临古洛河，北依邙山，背靠黄河。

&bull; **范围**：涵盖二里头、圪垱头和四角楼三个村。

&bull; **面积**：现存面积约300万平方米（3平方公里）。在公元前18至前16世纪，它是东亚地区最大的聚落。

### English


&bull; **Location**: The site is situated in the lower reaches of the Luo River in western Henan Province, surrounding Erlitou Village in Yanshi District, Luoyang City. It borders the ancient Luo River to the south, Mang Mountain to the north, and the Yellow River to the rear.

&bull; **Scope**: The site covers three villages: Erlitou, Gedangtou, and Sijiaolou.

&bull; **Area**: The extant area is approximately 3 million square meters (3 km²). From the 18th to the 16th century BCE, it was the largest settlement in East Asia.

## 三、发现与年代 | III. Discovery & Chronology

### 中文


&bull; **发现时间**：1959年，由历史学家徐旭生在豫西调查时发现。

&bull; **文化属性**：遗址上最为丰富的文化遗存属“二里头文化”。

&bull; **绝对年代**：约为公元前1750年至公元前1520年（距今约3800～3500年），相当于古代文献中的夏、商王朝时期。

&bull; **文化分期**：学术界一般将二里头文化分为四期。第一期约在夏朝中期；第二期约为夏朝中期晚段至晚期前段；第三期约为夏朝晚期后段；第四期部分已进入商代初年。

### English


&bull; **Discovery**: Discovered in 1959 by historian Xu Xusheng during a survey in western Henan.

&bull; **Cultural Attribution**: The most abundant cultural remains at the site belong to the “Erlitou Culture”.

&bull; **Absolute Date**: Approximately 1750–1520 BCE (roughly 3,800–3,500 years ago), corresponding to the Xia and Shang dynasties as recorded in ancient texts.

&bull; **Cultural Phases**: Scholars generally divide the Erlitou Culture into four phases. Phase I corresponds roughly to the middle Xia Dynasty; Phase II to the late middle through early late Xia; Phase III to the late Xia; and Phase IV partially extends into the early Shang Dynasty.

## 四、历史地位与文化意义 | IV. Historical Status & Cultural Significance

### 中文


&bull; **夏朝都邑**：被普遍认为是夏代中晚期的都城遗址，古称“斟鄩”。二里头遗址是打开夏王朝历史大门的一把“钥匙”。

&bull; **最早的中国**：作为东亚地区最早进入青铜时代的庞大都邑，二里头被称为“最早的中国”。

&bull; **文明核心**：二里头文化被认为是中华文明总进程的核心与引领者，开启了夏商周三代文明。它和之后的商周文明共同构成了早期华夏文明的主流，确立了华夏文明的基本特征。

&bull; **制度开创**：二里头遗址是一座精心规划、庞大有序、史无前例的王朝大都，多项中国古代都邑和政治制度都源于此。它是中国青铜礼制的创造者，更是夏商周王朝乃至整个古代中国核心政治制度、身份认同和文化思想的奠基者。

### English


&bull; **Capital of the Xia Dynasty**: Widely regarded as the capital site of the mid-to-late Xia Dynasty, historically known as “Zhenxun”. The Erlitou Site is the “key” to unlocking the history of the Xia Dynasty.

&bull; **Earliest China**: As the earliest large-scale urban settlement in East Asia to enter the Bronze Age, Erlitou is known as the “Earliest China”.

&bull; **Core of Civilization**: The Erlitou Culture is considered the core and leader of the overall progress of Chinese civilization, inaugurating the Xia-Shang-Zhou triadic civilization. Together with the subsequent Shang and Zhou civilizations, it formed the mainstream of early Chinese civilization and established its fundamental characteristics.

&bull; **Institutional Origin**: Erlitou was a meticulously planned, vast, and orderly dynastic capital of unprecedented scale. Many ancient Chinese capital city designs and political systems originated here. It was the creator of China’s bronze ritual system and the founder of the core political systems, identity, and cultural thought of the Xia-Shang-Zhou dynasties and all of ancient China.

## 五、都城布局 | V. Urban Layout

### 中文
二里头遗址是一座经缜密规划、布局严整的王国都城遗址。其布局开启了中国古代都城规划之先河，诸多形制被后世沿用。


&bull; **中心区与一般活动区**：遗址可分为中心区和一般活动区。

&bull; **多网格式布局**：发现了最早的多网格式都邑布局。

&bull; **城市道路系统**：拥有迄今所知中国最早的城市主干道路网络。呈“井”字形，最宽的道路达20米。

&bull; **宫城**：拥有最早的严整方正宫城格局。宫城面积超过10万平方米，四周有宫墙环绕。宫城的发现把中国宫城的历史由商代提早到了夏代。

&bull; **功能分区**：宫殿区、祭祀区、官营作坊区等分区明确。祭祀区位于宫殿区以北，作坊区位于宫殿区以南。

### English
The Erlitou Site is a royal capital that was meticulously planned and strictly organized. Its layout pioneered ancient Chinese capital city planning, with many features adopted by later dynasties.


&bull; **Central Zone & General Activity Zone**: The site can be divided into a central zone and a general activity zone.

&bull; **Multi-grid Layout**: The earliest multi-grid capital layout has been discovered here.

&bull; **Urban Road Network**: It features the earliest known urban road network in China. Arranged in a “well” (jing) shape, the widest roads measure 20 meters across.

&bull; **Palace City**: The site contains the earliest strict and square palace city layout. The palace city covers over 100,000 square meters, surrounded by palace walls. The discovery of the palace city pushed the history of Chinese palace cities back from the Shang Dynasty to the Xia Dynasty.

&bull; **Functional Zoning**: Distinct zones include the palace area, sacrificial area, and state-operated workshop area. The sacrificial area is located north of the palace area, while the workshop area lies to the south.

## 六、宫殿建筑 | VI. Palace Architecture

### 中文


&bull; **大型宫殿建筑群**：遗址发现多座大型宫殿建筑基址，包括1号、2号、3号、4号、5号、7号、8号等基址。

&bull; **中轴线规划**：大型宫殿建筑群具有明确的中轴线规划。

&bull; **四合院式建筑**：发现四合院式围墙夯土建筑。

&bull; **建造时期**：3号宫殿始建于二里头文化第二期；1号和2号宫殿建于第三期。

&bull; **最新发现（2025年）** ：在宫殿区中部新发现17号、18号和21号基址，其中17号基址南部发现排房F2。首次在宫殿区排房建筑中发现“墙槽埋础石”现象。

### English


&bull; **Large Palace Complexes**: Multiple large palace foundation sites have been discovered, including Nos. 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, and 8.

&bull; **Central Axis Planning**: The large palace complexes feature a clear central axis layout.

&bull; **Courtyard-style Architecture**: Rammed-earth buildings with enclosing walls in a courtyard (siheyuan) style have been found.

&bull; **Construction Period**: Palace No. 3 was first built in Phase II of the Erlitou Culture; Palaces Nos. 1 and 2 were built in Phase III.

&bull; **Latest Discoveries (2025)**: Newly discovered foundation sites Nos. 17, 18, and 21 in the central palace area, with Row House F2 found at the southern end of No. 17. The phenomenon of “placing foundation stones in wall trenches” was discovered for the first time in row houses within the palace area.

## 七、手工业作坊 | VII. Craft Workshops

### 中文


&bull; **铸铜作坊**：发现中国最早的青铜器铸造作坊，面积近2万平方米。统治者对青铜礼器铸造权保持着绝对的垄断。

&bull; **绿松石器作坊**：发现中国最早的绿松石器制造作坊。

&bull; **其他作坊**：发现制陶遗存、骨器加工作坊、角器加工作坊，以及疑似漆器加工作坊。

### English


&bull; **Bronze Casting Workshop**: The earliest bronze casting workshop in China has been discovered here, covering nearly 20,000 square meters. Rulers maintained an absolute monopoly over the casting of bronze ritual vessels.

&bull; **Turquoise Workshop**: The earliest turquoise artifact manufacturing workshop in China has been found.

&bull; **Other Workshops**: Pottery-making remains, bone tool workshops, horn tool workshops, and a suspected lacquerware workshop have also been discovered.

## 八、重要文物 | VIII. Important Artifacts

### 中文
出土文物包括青铜器、陶器、玉器、绿松石器、漆器、骨角牙器等，种类极为丰富。

### 1\. 青铜礼器群
中国最早的青铜礼器群。代表器物包括青铜酒器（如乳钉纹铜爵）、乐器、武器等，构成独具中国特色的青铜礼乐文明。

### 2\. 绿松石龙形器
一件长64.5厘米、由2000余片绿松石片镶嵌而成的龙形器物。因其精湛的工艺和巨大的体量，被学术界广泛认为是中国龙图腾崇拜的重要源头。

### 3\. 镶嵌绿松石铜牌饰
兽面纹铜牌饰，是二里头文化的代表性器物之一。此类兽面纹开了商周青铜器上兽面纹的先河。

### 4\. 玉礼器
出土玉璋、玉刀等玉礼器。

### 5\. 漆器
发现多件漆器，反映了当时手工业的发达水平。

### English
Excavated artifacts include bronzes, pottery, jade, turquoise objects, lacquerware, and bone, horn, and ivory artifacts—remarkably diverse in variety.

### 1\. Bronze Ritual Vessel Assemblage
The earliest assemblage of bronze ritual vessels in China. Representative objects include bronze wine vessels (such as the nail-pattern bronze jue), musical instruments, and weapons, constituting a uniquely Chinese bronze ritual-music civilization.

### 2\. Turquoise Dragon-shaped Artifact
A dragon-shaped object 64.5 cm in length, inlaid with over 2,000 pieces of turquoise. Due to its exquisite craftsmanship and massive size, it is widely regarded by the academic community as an important source of Chinese dragon totem worship.

### 3\. Turquoise-inlaid Bronze Plaque with Animal Face
The animal-face patterned bronze plaque inlaid with turquoise is one of the representative artifacts of the Erlitou Culture. This animal-face motif set a precedent for the animal-face designs found on Shang and Zhou bronze vessels.

### 4\. Jade Ritual Objects
Jade yazhang (ritual blade-shaped artifacts) and jade knives have been unearthed.

### 5\. Lacquerware
Multiple lacquerware items have been discovered, reflecting the advanced level of craftsmanship at the time.

## 九、墓葬与社会结构 | IX. Tombs & Social Structure

### 中文


&bull; **贵族墓葬**：宫殿区发现成组贵族墓，随葬有铜礼器、玉礼器、绿松石器等高规格器物。部分墓葬墓底铺有较厚的朱砂。

&bull; **工匠墓葬**：在铸铜遗址区域发现随葬铸铜工具的墓葬，墓主可能为工匠或铸铜管理者。

&bull; **社会分化**：墓葬的等级差异反映了明显的社会分层。贵族、平民和更低身份的人员同处一区但相距不远，表明社会结构复杂。二里头都邑的社会结构呈现出明显的等级分化和职业分工特征。

### English


&bull; **Elite Tombs**: Grouped elite tombs have been found in the palace area, buried with high-status objects including bronze ritual vessels, jade ritual objects, and turquoise artifacts. Some tomb floors were covered with a thick layer of cinnabar.

&bull; **Artisan Tombs**: Tombs containing bronze casting tools have been discovered in the bronze casting area; the occupants were likely artisans or bronze casting supervisors.

&bull; **Social Stratification**: The hierarchical differences among tombs reflect clear social stratification. Elites, commoners, and individuals of lower status lived in the same general area but not far apart, indicating a complex social structure. The social structure of the Erlitou capital shows pronounced hierarchical differentiation and occupational specialization.

## 十、学术争议 | X. Scholarly Debates

### 中文


&bull; **夏商分界**：二里头遗址是探索夏商王朝分界的关键遗址。关于二里头遗址究竟属于夏都还是商都，学术界曾有长期争论。

&bull; **学术共识**：目前，国内学界已基本形成共识，认为以二里头文化为核心的考古遗存代表了夏王朝的存在，二里头遗址是其晚期都邑。学术焦点已从“有无之争”转向更深入的研究。

&bull; **文字问题**：二里头文化或夏代是否有文字是学术界探讨已久的问题。部分学者认为二里头陶文是夏代文字，但尚未达成普遍共识。

### English


&bull; **Xia-Shang Boundary**: The Erlitou Site is a key site for exploring the boundary between the Xia and Shang dynasties. There has been long-standing academic debate over whether the Erlitou Site was the capital of the Xia or the Shang.

&bull; **Academic Consensus**: Currently, there is a basic consensus within the Chinese academic community that the archaeological remains centered on the Erlitou Culture represent the existence of the Xia Dynasty, and that the Erlitou Site was its late capital. The academic focus has shifted from the “existence debate” to more in-depth research.

&bull; **The Question of Writing**: Whether the Erlitou Culture or the Xia Dynasty had a writing system has long been a subject of academic inquiry. Some scholars believe that the pottery inscriptions at Erlitou represent Xia Dynasty writing, but no general consensus has been reached.

## 十一、保护与展示 | XI. Protection & Exhibition

### 中文


&bull; **保护单位**：1988年，二里头遗址被列为第三批全国重点文物保护单位。

&bull; **考古遗址公园**：2017年列入国家考古遗址公园立项名单，2022年入选第四批国家考古遗址公园名单。遗址公园占地面积1225亩。

&bull; **二里头夏都遗址博物馆**：2019年建成开放，是国家“十三五”重大文化工程项目。博物馆占地246亩，总建筑面积3.2万平方米。馆藏文物2089件，主要为青铜器、陶器、玉器、绿松石器、骨角牙器等。

&bull; **展陈内容**：基本陈列由“第一王朝”、“赫赫夏都”和“世纪探索”三部分组成，系统展示了夏代历史、夏文化探索历程、二里头遗址60多年考古成果以及中华文明探源工程的研究成果。

&bull; **保护立法**：2022年，《洛阳市二里头遗址保护条例》实施。

### English


&bull; **Protected Status**: In 1988, the Erlitou Site was listed among the third batch of Major Historical and Cultural Sites Protected at the National Level.

&bull; **Archaeological Site Park**: It was included in the National Archaeological Site Park project list in 2017 and selected for the fourth batch of National Archaeological Site Parks in 2022. The site park covers an area of 1,225 mu (approximately 81.7 hectares).

&bull; **Erlitou Site Museum of the Xia Capital**: Opened in 2019, it is a major national cultural project under the “13th Five-Year Plan”. The museum covers 246 mu (16.4 hectares) with a total floor area of 32,000 square meters. It houses 2,089 cultural artifacts, primarily bronzes, pottery, jade, turquoise objects, and bone, horn, and ivory artifacts.

&bull; **Exhibition Content**: The permanent exhibition consists of three parts: “The First Dynasty,” “The Majestic Xia Capital,” and “A Century of Exploration,” systematically presenting Xia Dynasty history, the journey of Xia culture exploration, over 60 years of archaeological achievements at the Erlitou Site, and the research outcomes of the Project to Trace the Origins of Chinese Civilization.

&bull; **Protection Legislation**: In 2022, the “Luoyang City Erlitou Site Protection Regulations” came into effect.

## 十二、最新考古进展（2025年） | XII. Latest Archaeological Progress (2025)

### 中文


&bull; **宫殿区发掘**：对宫东一区北部和宫殿区中部进行了发掘。

&bull; **宫东一区**：发现二里头文化的房址、水井、陶窑、夯土、墓葬、灰坑等遗迹，出土骨器、角器、蚌器、牙器及玉石器等。

&bull; **宫殿区中部**：发现17号、18号和21号基址。

&bull; **首次发现**：首次在宫殿区排房建筑中发现“墙槽埋础石”现象。

&bull; **最宽排房**：发现二里头都邑目前已知最宽排房。

### English


&bull; **Palace Area Excavation**: Excavations were conducted in the northern part of the East Palace Zone 1 and the central palace area.

&bull; **East Palace Zone 1**: Discoveries include house foundations, wells, pottery kilns, rammed earth, tombs, and ash pits of the Erlitou Culture, yielding bone, horn, clam, tooth, and jade/stone artifacts.

&bull; **Central Palace Area**: Foundation sites Nos. 17, 18, and 21 were discovered.

&bull; **First Discovery**: The phenomenon of “placing foundation stones in wall trenches” was discovered for the first time in row houses within the palace area.

&bull; **Widest Row House**: The widest row house currently known at the Erlitou capital was discovered.
