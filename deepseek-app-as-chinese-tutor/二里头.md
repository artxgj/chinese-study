# 二里头遗址 · 中英双语对照
## Erlitou Site – Bilingual Overview (Chinese & English)

---

### 📍 基本信息 | Basic Facts

· 位于中国河南省洛阳市偃师区，距今约3800–3500年（公元前18–16世纪），1959年由史学家徐旭生发现。遗址现存面积约300万平方米（3平方公里），1988年列为第三批全国重点文物保护单位。

· Located in Yanshi District, Luoyang, Henan, China. Dated to c. 18th–16th century BCE (approx. 3800–3500 years ago). Discovered in 1959 by historian Xu Xusheng. The site covers about 3 million m² (3 km²) and was designated a Major National Historical and Cultural Site in 1988 (third batch).

---

### 🏛️ 历史地位 | Historical Significance

· 学术界普遍认为是夏代中晚期都城，是二里头文化的命名地。它是东亚地区最早的青铜时代都邑，也是中国首个广域王权国家的遗存，其宫城规划（中轴线布局）开创了中国古代都城营建的传统。

· Widely regarded as the capital of the middle-to-late Xia Dynasty and the type site of the Erlitou culture. It is the earliest Bronze Age urban center in East Asia and represents China’s first state-level society with broad territorial control. Its palatial city layout, featuring a central axis, set the template for later Chinese capitals.

---

### 🏗️ 主要考古发现 | Key Discoveries

· 宫城与宫殿 / Palace complex

  发现中国最早的有中轴线布局的方正宫城。

  The earliest enclosed palace city in China, with a clear central‑axis layout.

· 城市道路网 / Road network

  拥有中国最早的城市主干道系统。

  The country’s earliest urban thoroughfare system.

· 官营作坊区 / Craft workshops

  发现中国最早的青铜铸造和绿松石加工官营作坊。

  Dedicated official workshops for bronze casting and turquoise working – the earliest such workshops found in China.

· 青铜礼器群 / Bronze ritual vessels

  出土了中国最早的青铜礼器组合。

  The oldest assemblage of bronze ritual objects in China.

· 绿松石龙形器 / Turquoise dragon artifact

  长约64.5厘米，由绿松石片拼成，被认为是龙图腾崇拜的重要源头。
  A 64.5‑cm‑long dragon-shaped object made of turquoise pieces, believed to be a key precursor of Chinese dragon worship.

· 多网格布局 / Grid layout

  近年发现城市由道路和墙垣划分成多个网格状区域，反映出高度社会组织和等级制度。

  Recent excavations reveal a city divided into multiple walled blocks by roads and walls, indicating advanced social organization.

---

### 🏛️ 二里头夏都遗址博物馆 | Erlitou Site Museum of the Xia Capital

· 2019年10月开放，占地246亩（16.4公顷），建筑面积3.2万平方米。馆藏文物3.5万余件，包括青铜器、玉器等。

· Opened in October 2019. Covers 16.4 hectares (246 mu) with a floor area of 32,000 m². Houses over 35,000 cultural relics, including bronzes and jades.

---

### ✨ 总结 | Summary

· 二里头遗址是实证中华五千年文明的关键坐标，为我们理解中国国家的起源、礼制形成和早期都城规划提供了珍贵的实物证据。

· Erlitou is a crucial archaeological site that substantiates the 5,000‑year history of Chinese civilization, offering tangible evidence for the origins of statehood, ritual culture, and urban planning in ancient China.
