# 三家分晉 中英雙語完整詳解

# The Partition of Jin – A Complete Bilingual Analysis

## 一、定義與概述

## I. Definition and Overview

#### 中文：
三家分晉，是指春秋末期，晉國被韓、趙、魏三個卿大夫家族瓜分、導致晉國滅亡的歷史事件。此事件被史學界視為春秋時代與戰國時代的分水嶺。周威烈王二十三年(公元前403年)，周天子正式冊封韓虔、趙籍、魏斯三人為諸侯，標誌著三家分晉的正式完成。

#### English:
The Partition of Jin (三家分晉) refers to the historical event at the end of the Spring and Autumn period, in which the State of Jin was divided among three ministerial families—Han, Zhao, and Wei—leading to the eventual destruction of Jin.This event is regarded by historians as the watershed dividing the Spring and Autumn period from the Warring States period.In 403 BCE, King Weilie of Zhou formally enfeoffed Han Qian, Zhao Ji, and Wei Si as feudal lords, marking the formal completion of the Partition of Jin.

## 二、歷史背景

## II. Historical Background

### (一) 晉無公族

### (1) The “No Royal Relatives” Policy of Jin

#### 中文：
周代各諸侯國通常將公室子孫分封為大夫，以血緣關係作為公室的屏衛。然而晉國在驪姬之亂時，晉獻公逐殺諸公子，從此不再分封公子、公孫為大夫，史稱「晉無公族」。這一政策導致公室力量薄弱，無法拱衛國君，異姓卿大夫勢力趁機崛起。

#### English:
During the Zhou dynasty, feudal lords typically enfeoffed their royal descendants as ministers to serve as a protective screen for the royal house through blood ties. However, during the Li Ji Unrest in Jin, Duke Xian of Jin expelled and killed many princes, and thereafter Jin ceased to enfeoff princes and grand-princes as ministers—a policy historically known as “Jin had no royal relatives” (晉無公族).This policy weakened the power of the royal house, leaving the duke without adequate protection, and allowed the rise of powerful ministerial families of non-royal surnames.

### (二) 六卿專權

### (2) The Six Ministers’ Seizure of Power

#### 中文：
春秋初至晉文公稱霸時，趙、魏、韓、范、中行、智(知)氏六個異姓貴族成為舉足輕重的新興勢力，即「六卿」。春秋末年，晉國公室卑弱，大權旁落，分屬六卿，史稱「六卿專權」。六卿的權位逐漸世襲化，勢力日益穩固，導致「晉公室卑而六卿強，欲內相攻」。

#### English:
From the early Spring and Autumn period to the time when Duke Wen of Jin achieved hegemony, six noble families of non-royal surnames—Zhao, Wei, Han, Fan, Zhonghang, and Zhi—rose to become influential new powers, known as the “Six Ministers” (六卿).By the late Spring and Autumn period, the Jin royal house had grown weak, and state power had fallen into the hands of the six ministerial families—a situation known as the “Six Ministers’ Seizure of Power” (六卿專權).The positions of the Six Ministers gradually became hereditary, and their power grew increasingly entrenched, leading to a situation where “the Jin royal house was weak while the six ministers were strong, each coveting the others’ territory.”

### (三) 經濟基礎的變革

### (3) Transformation of the Economic Foundation

#### 中文：
春秋中後期，晉國的土地制度正經歷從奴隸制向封建制的深刻轉化。卿大夫家族通過兼併、賞賜等方式掌握大量封邑和依附人口，經濟實力急劇增強。這種經濟基礎的變革，使卿族具備了與公室分庭抗禮甚至取而代之的物質條件。

#### English:
During the mid-to-late Spring and Autumn period, the land system of Jin was undergoing a profound transformation from slavery to feudalism. The ministerial families, through annexation and grants, came to control vast estates and dependent populations, greatly enhancing their economic power. This transformation of the economic foundation gave the ministerial families the material conditions to contend with—or even replace—the royal house.

## 三、發展過程

## III. The Process of Events

#### 第一階段：六卿兼併(公元前514年—公元前497年)

#### Phase One: The Six Ministers’ Annexation Wars (514 BCE – 497 BCE)

#### 中文：
晉頃公十二年(公元前514年)，六卿藉故消滅公室支脈祁氏、羊舌氏，瓜分其土地。此後六卿之間內鬥不斷。公元前497年，六卿內戰爆發，范氏和中行氏被趙、魏、韓、智四家聯合擊敗並驅逐，其封地被四家瓜分。至此，晉國進入趙、魏、韓、智四卿專政的時代。

#### English:
In 514 BCE (the 12th year of Duke Qing of Jin), the six ministers used various pretexts to eliminate the royal branch families of Qi and Yangshe, dividing their lands among themselves.Thereafter, infighting among the six ministers continued unabated. In 497 BCE, civil war broke out among the six ministers; the Fan and Zhonghang families were defeated and expelled by a coalition of the Zhao, Wei, Han, and Zhi families, and their territories were divided among the four.At this point, Jin entered an era dominated by the four ministers: Zhao, Wei, Han, and Zhi.

#### 第二階段：晉陽之戰(公元前455年—公元前453年)

#### Phase Two: The Battle of Jinyang (455 BCE – 453 BCE)

#### 中文：
四卿之中，以智氏最為強大。擔任晉國正卿的智伯(荀瑤)恃強向韓康子、魏桓子索取土地，韓、魏兩家畏懼智氏而被迫割地。當智伯向趙襄子索地時遭到拒絕，遂於周貞定王十四年(公元前455年)聯合韓、魏攻打趙氏。

趙襄子退守晉陽(今山西太原)。智伯引汾水灌城，晉陽城「城不浸者三版，沈灶產蛙」，但城中軍民「民無叛意」，堅守不屈。戰事相持至第三年(公元前453年)，趙襄子派人暗中說服韓康子與魏桓子——韓、魏深知智氏滅趙後必將輪到自己，遂與趙氏結盟，反攻智氏。三家聯合消滅智氏宗族，瓜分其土地，是為「晉陽之戰」。

#### English:
Among the four families, the Zhi were the most powerful.Zhi Yao (or Zhi Bo Yao), who served as the chief minister of Jin, arrogantly demanded territory from Han Kangzi and Wei Huanzi. Fearing Zhi’s power, the Han and Wei reluctantly complied.When Zhi Yao made the same demand of Zhao Xiangzi and was refused, he allied with Han and Wei to attack Zhao in 455 BCE.

Zhao Xiangzi retreated to the city of Jinyang (modern-day Taiyuan, Shanxi). Zhi Yao diverted the Fen River to flood the city—the water rose to within “three wooden planks of the top of the city wall, and cooking stoves were submerged with frogs spawning in them”—yet the people of Jinyang “showed no intention of surrendering” and held firm.The siege lasted until the third year (453 BCE), when Zhao Xiangzi secretly persuaded Han Kangzi and Wei Huanzi—both realized that once Zhi destroyed Zhao, they would be next. They formed an alliance with Zhao and launched a counterattack against Zhi.The three families jointly annihilated the Zhi clan and divided its lands—this was the Battle of Jinyang.

#### 第三階段：三家分晉(公元前453年—公元前403年)

#### Phase Three: The Partition of Jin (453 BCE – 403 BCE)

#### 中文：
消滅智氏後，晉國僅剩韓、趙、魏三家卿族。此後晉國國君名存實亡，大權完全落入三家之手。晉出公試圖藉助齊、魯軍隊討伐三家，反被三家擊敗，出公被迫出逃，死於途中。

周威烈王二十三年(公元前403年)，周天子正式任命晉國大夫魏斯、趙籍、韓虔為諸侯。從此，魏、趙、韓三國與晉國國君平起平坐，晉國名存實亡。司馬光《資治通鑑》以此事為開篇：「周威烈王二十三年，初命晉大夫魏斯、趙籍、韓虔為諸侯……」

#### English:
After the elimination of the Zhi, only the Han, Zhao, and Wei ministerial families remained in Jin.Thereafter, the duke of Jin existed in name only, with all real power falling into the hands of the three families.Duke Chu of Jin attempted to use troops from Qi and Lu to attack the three families, but was defeated and forced into exile, dying en route.

In 403 BCE (the 23rd year of King Weilie of Zhou), the Zhou king formally enfeoffed the Jin ministers Wei Si, Zhao Ji, and Han Qian as feudal lords.From this point, the states of Wei, Zhao, and Han stood on equal footing with the duke of Jin, and Jin existed in name only.Sima Guang’s Zizhi Tongjian (Comprehensive Mirror for Aid in Government) opens with this very event: “In the 23rd year of King Weilie of Zhou, for the first time, the Jin ministers Wei Si, Zhao Ji, and Han Qian were enfeoffed as feudal lords…”

## 四、最終結果

## IV. The Final Outcome

#### 中文：
公元前403年三家受封為諸侯後，晉國並未立即滅亡，尚餘絳城與曲沃兩城為晉君保留，晉國與韓、趙、魏三國共同存在了27年。公元前376年，韓、趙、魏三家廢黜末代晉君晉靜公，將其貶為庶人，瓜分了晉國殘餘土地，晉國徹底滅亡，宗廟祭祀斷絕。公元前349年，韓、趙兩國進一步瓜分了殘餘的晉國領土。

#### English:
After the three families were enfeoffed as feudal lords in 403 BCE, Jin did not immediately cease to exist. The two cities of Jiang and Quwo were left to the duke of Jin, and Jin coexisted alongside Han, Zhao, and Wei for 27 years.In 376 BCE, the three states of Han, Zhao, and Wei deposed the last duke of Jin, Duke Jing, reduced him to commoner status, and divided the remaining Jin territories among themselves—bringing the complete end of the State of Jin and the extinction of its ancestral sacrifices.In 349 BCE, Han and Zhao further divided the remaining Jin territories.

## 五、歷史影響

## V. Historical Significance

### (一) 政治格局的巨變

### (1) Transformation of the Political Landscape

#### 中文：
三家分晉標誌著「春秋五霸」之一的晉國滅亡，而「戰國七雄」中的韓、趙、魏三國由此誕生。晉國作為春秋時期最強大的諸侯國之一，其分裂徹底改變了中原的政治格局，揭開了諸侯兼併戰爭的新序幕。

#### English:
The Partition of Jin marked the end of Jin—one of the “Five Hegemons of the Spring and Autumn Period”—and gave rise to three of the “Seven Great Powers of the Warring States”: Han, Zhao, and Wei.As one of the most powerful states of the Spring and Autumn period, the disintegration of Jin fundamentally altered the political landscape of the Central Plains and ushered in a new era of interstate annexation warfare.

### (二) 春秋與戰國的分界線

### (2) The Dividing Line Between Spring and Autumn and Warring States

#### 中文：
史學界普遍以三家分晉作為春秋時代與戰國時代的分界線。宋代司馬光編纂《資治通鑑》時，特意以此事作為全書的開篇，強調其劃時代的意義。司馬光在「臣光曰」中深刻指出，周天子承認三家僭越、將卿大夫升格為諸侯，導致了禮制綱紀的根本崩壞。

#### English:
Historians generally regard the Partition of Jin as the dividing line between the Spring and Autumn period and the Warring States period.When Sima Guang compiled the Zizhi Tongjian during the Song dynasty, he deliberately chose this event as the opening of the entire work, emphasizing its epoch-making significance.In his commentary, Sima Guang pointed out that the Zhou king’s recognition of the three ministers’ usurpation—elevating them from ministers to feudal lords—led to the fundamental collapse of the ritual order and social hierarchy.

### (三) 禮崩樂壞的標誌

### (3) The Symbol of “Ritual Collapse and Musical Decay”

#### 中文：
三家分晉之前，即使如晉文公般強勢的諸侯也不敢僭越周天子的禮制。然而三家分晉之後，周天子不但不申斥三家，反而正式承認其諸侯地位，周王室親手破壞了維繫天下秩序的禮制。此後天下人不再以禮制名分定高下，而以實力爭雄——宗法制度被實力取代，分封制轉向郡縣制，禮樂制度徹底崩壞，天命觀被「強者為王」所打破。

#### English:
Before the Partition of Jin, even a powerful ruler like Duke Wen of Jin dared not overstep the ritual system of the Zhou king.However, after the Partition of Jin, the Zhou king not only failed to condemn the three families but formally recognized them as feudal lords—the Zhou royal house itself destroyed the ritual order that had maintained the world’s hierarchy.Thereafter, the world no longer judged status by ritual rank but by actual power—patriarchal clan systems gave way to rule by strength, enfeoffment gave way to commanderies and counties, the ritual-music system completely collapsed, and the concept of the Mandate of Heaven was replaced by the principle that “the strong are kings.”

## 六、《資治通鑑》的歷史評價

## VI. Sima Guang’s Historical Judgment

#### 中文：
司馬光在《資治通鑑》開篇以「臣光曰」發表長篇議論，強調禮制與名分的重要性。他認為天子統轄三公，三公督率諸侯，諸侯控制卿大夫，卿大夫治理士庶人——整個社會自上而下各守本分，國家才能長治久安。三家分晉事件中，周天子將本是臣下的卿大夫升為諸侯，是「名不正則言不順」的典型，從此「禮樂征伐自諸侯出」的局面徹底惡化為「自大夫出」。

司馬光以此事為《資治通鑑》開篇，意在告誡後世統治者：維護禮制名分是維繫國家秩序的根基，一旦禮崩樂壞，天下必將陷入無休止的戰亂。

#### English:
In his commentary at the opening of the Zizhi Tongjian, Sima Guang delivered an extended discourse emphasizing the importance of ritual order and proper titles.He argued that the Son of Heaven commands the Three Dukes, the Three Dukes oversee the feudal lords, the feudal lords control their ministers, and the ministers govern the common people—only when each level of society fulfills its proper role can the state enjoy lasting peace.In the Partition of Jin, the Zhou king elevated ministers—who were by rights subjects—to the status of feudal lords; this was a classic case of “if names are not correct, words do not ring true.” From this point, the situation where “rituals, music, and military campaigns emanated from the feudal lords” deteriorated completely into one where they emanated from mere ministers.

By choosing this event as the opening of the Zizhi Tongjian, Sima Guang sought to warn later rulers that preserving ritual order and proper titles is the foundation of state stability—once the ritual order collapses, the world will inevitably descend into endless chaos.

## 七、總結

## VII. Summary

#### 中文：
三家分晉是中國歷史上具有劃時代意義的重大事件。它不僅終結了稱霸中原數百年的晉國，更標誌著整個時代的根本轉折——從講究宗法禮制的春秋時代，進入以實力與兼併為主題的戰國時代。這一事件深刻影響了此後兩千餘年中國歷史的發展進程，是理解中國古代政治制度變遷與社會轉型的關鍵節點。

#### English:
The Partition of Jin is a landmark event of epochal significance in Chinese history. It not only brought an end to the State of Jin—which had dominated the Central Plains for centuries—but also marked a fundamental turning point of the entire era: from the Spring and Autumn period, characterized by patriarchal clan rituals and hierarchical order, to the Warring States period, defined by raw power and annexation warfare.This event profoundly influenced the course of Chinese history for over two millennia and remains a crucial juncture for understanding the transformation of ancient China’s political institutions and social structures.
