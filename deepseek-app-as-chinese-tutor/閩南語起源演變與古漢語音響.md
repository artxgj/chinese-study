# 閩南語的起源演變與古漢語、文言文及中古漢語的影響

# The Origin and Evolution of Southern Min and the Influence of Old Chinese, Literary Chinese, and Middle Chinese

---

## 一、緒論 / Introduction

閩南語（Southern Min / Hokkien）是漢語閩語支中最具影響力的一支，主要分布於福建南部、臺灣、廣東潮汕、海南及東南亞等地。語言學界普遍認為，閩南語是漢語方言中保存最多古代漢語特徵的語言，因此被譽為古漢語的「活化石」。

Southern Min (also known as Hokkien) is the most influential branch of the Min Chinese language family. It is primarily spoken in southern Fujian, Taiwan, the Chaoshan region of Guangdong, Hainan, and Southeast Asia. Linguists generally regard Southern Min as the Chinese dialect that preserves the most features of ancient Chinese, earning it the title of a "living fossil" of Old Chinese.

---

## 二、閩南語的起源與形成 / The Origin and Formation of Southern Min

### 2\.1 上古時期的底層 / Old Chinese Substratum

閩南語的根源可追溯至上古漢語（Old Chinese）。學術研究指出，閩方言擁有西漢以前的上古漢語語音特徵，最初可能形成於福建以外的地區，後來隨著移民進入福建。美國漢學家羅杰瑞（Jerry Norman）透過重構原始閩語（Proto-Min），發現其音韻系統保留了比中古漢語更古老的六套塞音/塞擦音對立（中古漢語僅有三套）。

The roots of Southern Min can be traced back to Old Chinese. Scholarly research indicates that Min dialects possess phonological features of Old Chinese from before the Western Han dynasty and likely formed outside of Fujian before being brought into the region by migrants. American Sinologist Jerry Norman, through his reconstruction of Proto-Min, discovered that its phonological system preserves six sets of stop/affricate contrasts—more ancient than the three sets found in Middle Chinese.

### 2\.2 歷次移民浪潮與語言形成 / Migration Waves and Language Formation

閩南語的形成主要源於歷史上多次大規模的北人南遷：

The formation of Southern Min resulted primarily from several large-scale southward migrations of northern Chinese populations:

#### (1) 兩漢時期（公元前202年－公元220年）/ Han Dynasty (202 BC – 220 AD)

有學者認為閩南方言最早形成於東漢末年。兩漢時期閩越人的北遷與漢人的南移，為閩語的形成奠定了基礎。

Some scholars argue that Southern Min was first formed in the late Eastern Han dynasty. The northward migration of the Minyue people and the southward movement of Han Chinese during the Han dynasty laid the foundation for the formation of Min languages.

#### (2) 西晉永嘉之亂（四世紀）/ Yongjia Rebellion (4th Century)

西晉永嘉二年（308年），五胡亂華引發大規模避難潮。中原「衣冠八族」（林、黃、陳、鄭、詹、丘、何、胡等氏族）南遷至福建泉州晉江流域一帶，帶來了當時的古漢語——「河洛語」。這次移民雖數量不多，但奠定了泉州話的雛形。

In 308 AD, the "Uprising of the Five Barbarians" triggered a massive wave of refugees. The "Eight Noble Clans" of the Central Plains (Lin, Huang, Chen, Zheng, Zhan, Qiu, He, Hu, etc.) migrated south to the Jinjiang River basin in Quanzhou, Fujian, bringing with them the ancient Chinese of the time—"Heluo Language". Although this migration was small in scale, it established the prototype of the Quanzhou dialect.

#### (3) 唐代陳政、陳元光父子（七世紀）/ Chen Family Migration (7th Century)

唐高宗總章二年（669年），朝廷派陳政、陳元光父子率軍入閩平定「蠻獠嘯亂」，後屯兵於漳州一帶，帶來了七世紀的中古漢語。這次移民對漳州話的形成具有關鍵作用。

In 669 AD, the Tang court dispatched General Chen Zheng and his son Chen Yuanguang with troops to Fujian to quell a local rebellion. They later settled in the Zhangzhou area, bringing 7th-century Middle Chinese with them. This migration played a crucial role in the formation of the Zhangzhou dialect.

#### (4) 唐代王潮兄弟（九世紀）/ Wang Brothers Migration (9th Century)

唐代末年黃巢之亂（878年）後，河南光州固始人王潮、王審邽、王審知三兄弟南下統治福建，帶來了九世紀的中原漢語。這兩批唐代移民以固始方言教導當地人誦讀四書五經，形成了漳州話的「讀書音」系統。

After the Huang Chao Rebellion in 878 AD, the three Wang brothers from Gushi, Guangzhou, Henan, moved south to govern Fujian, bringing 9th-century Central Plains Chinese with them. These two waves of Tang immigrants used the Gushi dialect to teach locals to read the Four Books and Five Classics, forming the "literary reading" (pronunciation) system of the Zhangzhou dialect.

### 2\.3 語言融合 / Linguistic Fusion

閩南語並非單純的中原漢語移植，而是多種語言融合的產物。在其詞彙與語法構成中，既有上古、中古和近代漢語的遺存，也有古越語（閩越語）和吳語、楚語的成分，甚至包含來自東南亞的外來語。

Southern Min is not merely a transplant of Central Plains Chinese but a product of multiple linguistic fusions. Its vocabulary and grammatical structure contain remnants of Old Chinese, Middle Chinese, and Modern Chinese, as well as elements of Old Yue (Minyue), Wu, and Chu languages, and even loanwords from Southeast Asia.

## 三、上古漢語對閩南語的影響 / The Influence of Old Chinese on Southern Min

### 3\.1 語音層面：上古音遺存 / Phonology: Old Chinese Sound Retentions

閩南語保留了多項重要的上古漢語語音特徵：

Southern Min preserves several major phonological features of Old Chinese:

#### (1) 古無輕唇音 / Lack of Labiodental Initials

上古漢語沒有「f」這個聲母，當時屬於「b」或「p」的音，在現代普通話中讀為「f」聲母的字，閩南語仍保留了上古的發音方式。例如：「飛」、「肥」、「夫」、「浮」等字，閩南語讀作「p」或「ph」、「b」聲母，而非普通話的「f」。

Old Chinese lacked the "f" initial; sounds that were pronounced as "b" or "p" in ancient times later became "f" in Modern Standard Mandarin, but Southern Min retains the Old Chinese pronunciation. For example, characters like 飛 (fly), 肥 (fat), 夫 (husband), and 浮 (float) are pronounced with "p," "ph," or "b" initials in Southern Min, rather than the "f" of Mandarin.

#### (2) 古無舌上音 / Lack of Retroflex Initials

上古漢語沒有「zh」、「ch」、「sh」等舌上音（卷舌音），這些特徵在閩南語中仍然保留。

Old Chinese lacked retroflex initials such as "zh," "ch," and "sh"—a feature still preserved in Southern Min.

#### (3) 古多舌音 / Abundance of Dental Initials

「娘日二紐歸泥」、「喻三歸匣」、「照二歸精」、「照三歸端」等上古音現象，在閩南語中均有保留。

Old Chinese phonological phenomena—such as the merging of certain initial categories—are all preserved in Southern Min.

### 3\.2 詞彙層面：上古詞彙的沿用 / Vocabulary: Old Chinese Lexical Retentions

閩南語口語中保留了大量的上古漢語詞彙，這些詞彙在現代普通話中已經消失或僅存於書面語。例如：

Southern Min colloquial speech preserves a large number of Old Chinese vocabulary items that have disappeared from Modern Mandarin or survive only in written language. For example:

| 閩南語 / Southern Min | 古漢語 / Old Chinese | 普通話 / Mandarin |
| ------------------ | ----------------- | -------------- |
| 鼎 (tiaⁿ)           | 古代炊具              | 鍋              |
| 箸 (tī)             | 筷子                | 筷子             |
| 囝 (kiáⁿ)           | 兒子、孩子             | 兒子、小孩          |
| 斟酌 (chim-chiok)    | 考慮、思量             | 考慮             |
| 新婦 (sin-pū)        | 媳婦                | 媳婦             |

### 3\.3 語法層面：上古語法結構 / Grammar: Old Chinese Syntactic Structures

閩南語在語法上保留了多項上古漢語的特徵，包括賓語前置、介詞省略、比較句中形容詞謂語前置、形容詞和名詞活用為動詞，以及無繫詞的判斷句等。

Southern Min preserves several syntactic features of Old Chinese, including object-fronting, preposition omission, adjective-predicate fronting in comparative sentences, flexible use of adjectives and nouns as verbs, and copula-less copulative sentences.

## 四、中古漢語對閩南語的影響 / The Influence of Middle Chinese on Southern Min

### 4\.1 語音層面：中古音系的保留 / Phonology: Middle Chinese Sound System Retention

閩南語的韻母和聲調系統基本上保留了中古漢語音系的特徵。具體表現包括：

Southern Min's rhyme and tone systems basically preserve the features of the Middle Chinese phonological system. Specific manifestations include:

#### (1) 入聲的完整保留 / Complete Retention of Entering Tone (Ru Tone)

中古漢語的入聲（以 -p、-t、-k 收尾的促音）在普通話中已完全消失，但閩南語完整地保留了所有入聲韻尾。這是閩南語作為古漢語「活化石」的重要證據之一。

The entering tone (ru tone) of Middle Chinese—checked syllables ending in -p, -t, or -k—has completely disappeared in Mandarin, but Southern Min has fully preserved all entering tone codas. This is one of the key pieces of evidence for Southern Min being a "living fossil" of ancient Chinese.

#### (2) 聲調系統的傳承 / Inheritance of the Tone System

中古漢語的「平、上、去、入」四聲，依聲母清濁分化為陰陽兩調，形成「陰平、陽平、陰上、陽上、陰去、陽去、陰入、陽入」八個聲調。閩南語大體保留了這一聲調格局。

The four tones of Middle Chinese—level (平), rising (上), departing (去), and entering (入)—each split into yin (陰) and yang (陽) registers based on the voicing of the initial consonant, forming eight tones. Southern Min largely preserves this tonal framework.

#### (3) 文白異讀系統 / Literary-Colloquial Reading System

閩南語最具特色的語言現象之一是「文白異讀」——同一個漢字有「讀書音」（文讀，源自中古漢語）和「說話音」（白讀，源自上古漢語或更早的層次）兩種讀音。文讀音主要來自唐代以後通過科舉、經典誦讀傳入的中古漢語，而白讀音則保留了更古老的上古層次。

One of the most distinctive features of Southern Min is the "literary-colloquial reading" system—the same Chinese character has two pronunciations: the "literary reading" (文讀, derived from Middle Chinese) and the "colloquial reading" (白讀, derived from Old Chinese or earlier strata). The literary readings mainly came from Middle Chinese introduced through the imperial examination system and classic reading after the Tang dynasty, while colloquial readings preserve older Old Chinese strata.

### 4\.2 詞彙層面：中古漢語詞彙的傳承 / Vocabulary: Middle Chinese Lexical Inheritance

閩南語的詞彙系統中，有大量詞彙可直接對應中古漢語的文獻記載。《閩南方言與古漢語同源詞典》系統考證了1235條方言詞條與古漢語的音義對應關係，其中大量詞條可追溯至隋唐時期的中古漢語特徵。

The vocabulary system of Southern Min contains a large number of words that can be directly mapped to Middle Chinese documentary records. The Dictionary of Cognates between Southern Min Dialect and Old Chinese systematically verifies the phonological and semantic correspondences of 1,235 dialect entries with ancient Chinese, a significant portion of which can be traced to Middle Chinese features from the Sui and Tang dynasties.

## 五、文言文對閩南語的影響 / The Influence of Literary Chinese on Southern Min

### 5\.1 書面語傳統與文言詞彙 / Written Tradition and Literary Vocabulary

文言文作為中國兩千多年來的書面標準語，對閩南語的書面表達和詞彙系統產生了深遠影響。閩南語的文讀音系統正是通過文言經典的誦讀而得以傳承和鞏固的。

Literary Chinese, as the written standard of China for over two millennia, has had a profound influence on the written expression and vocabulary system of Southern Min. The literary reading system of Southern Min was transmitted and consolidated precisely through the recitation of classical texts in Literary Chinese.

### 5\.2 語法層面的文言影響 / Grammatical Influence of Literary Chinese

閩南語在語法上與文言文有高度的相似性，包括：

Southern Min exhibits a high degree of grammatical similarity to Literary Chinese, including:


&bull; 賓語前置（Object-fronting）

&bull; 介詞省略（Preposition omission）

&bull; 形容詞謂語前置於比較句中（Adjective-predicate fronting in comparative sentences）

&bull; 形容詞與名詞活用為動詞（Flexible use of adjectives and nouns as verbs）

&bull; 無繫詞的判斷句（Copula-less copulative sentences）

這些語法特徵並非閩南語獨立發展的結果，而是源於其與古漢語（包括文言文所代表的書面傳統）在歷史上的淵源關係。

These grammatical features are not the result of independent development in Southern Min but stem from its historical relationship with ancient Chinese—including the written tradition represented by Literary Chinese.

### 5\.3 明清戲文與文言傳統 / Ming-Qing Drama Texts and the Literary Tradition

明清時期的閩南語戲文中，大量使用文言表達方式。研究指出，閩南語中一些較為文言的句式，可能是在官話（Mandarin）系統影響下借入的。這說明文言文通過戲曲、經典教育等管道，持續影響著閩南語的書面與口語表達。

Ming-Qing dynasty Southern Min drama texts extensively employed Literary Chinese expressions. Research indicates that some of the more literary syntactic patterns in Southern Min may have been borrowed under the influence of the Mandarin system. This demonstrates that Literary Chinese, through channels such as opera and classical education, continuously influenced both the written and spoken expression of Southern Min.

## 六、總結 / Conclusion

閩南語是漢語發展史上極具價值的語言標本。其形成歷經了從上古漢語到中古漢語的漫長演變過程，先後受到兩漢、魏晉、隋唐等多個時期北方移民所帶來的漢語的深刻影響。

Southern Min is an extremely valuable linguistic specimen in the history of Chinese language development. Its formation underwent a long evolutionary process from Old Chinese to Middle Chinese, receiving profound influence from Chinese brought by northern migrants during multiple periods including the Han, Wei-Jin, and Sui-Tang dynasties.

從語音上看，閩南語既保留了上古漢語的聲母特徵（古無輕唇音、古無舌上音等），也完整保留了中古漢語的入聲韻尾和聲調系統。從詞彙上看，閩南語口語中保存了大量上古和中古漢語的詞彙。從語法上看，閩南語保留了文言文式的句式結構。

Phonologically, Southern Min preserves both the initial consonant features of Old Chinese (lack of labiodental and retroflex initials, etc.) and the complete entering tone codas and tone system of Middle Chinese. Lexically, Southern Min colloquial speech preserves a large number of Old and Middle Chinese vocabulary items. Grammatically, Southern Min retains syntactic structures characteristic of Literary Chinese.

正因為閩南語在不同語言層面上多層次、多維度地保留了古漢語的特徵，語言學界將其譽為「古漢語的活化石」。這一獨特的語言地位，使閩南語成為研究漢語語音史、詞彙史和語法史不可或缺的重要材料。

It is precisely because Southern Min preserves features of ancient Chinese at multiple linguistic levels and dimensions that linguists have hailed it as a "living fossil of Old Chinese". This unique linguistic status makes Southern Min an indispensable and important resource for the study of Chinese phonological history, lexical history, and grammatical history.
