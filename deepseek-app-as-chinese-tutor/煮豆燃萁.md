# 煮豆燃萁 (Zhǔ Dòu Rán Qí) — 中英雙語成語故事詳解

---

## 一、成語簡介 | Basic Information

**煮豆燃萁**（拼音：zhǔ dòu rán qí）

| 項目   | 內容             |
| ---- | -------------- |
| **字面意思** | 用豆萁（豆莖）作燃料來煮豆子 |
| **比喻義**  | 比喻兄弟間不顧親情，自相殘殺 |
| **感情色彩** | 貶義             |
| **語法功能** | 可作主語、謂語、定語     |
| **近義詞**  | 同室操戈、相煎太急、兄弟鬩牆 |
| **反義詞**  | 情同手足、輔車相依、親密無間 |

### English:

| Item                 | Content                                                 |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Literal Meaning**     | To boil beans using bean stalks as fuel                 |
| **Figurative Meaning**  | Brothers or close kin attacking and harming one another |
| **Tone**                | Derogatory                                              |
| **Grammatical Function** | Can serve as subject, predicate, or attributive         |
| **Synonyms**            | internecine strife, fratricidal conflict                |
| **Antonyms**            | brotherly affection, close as hands and feet            |

## 二、故事背景 | Historical Background

成語出自**南朝宋·劉義慶《世說新語·文學》**。

三國時期，曹操去世後，長子**曹丕**繼位成為魏國皇帝（魏文帝）。曹丕嫉妒弟弟**曹植**的才華，擔心曹植威脅自己的皇位，便想藉口除掉他。

This idiom originates from **A New Account of the Tales of the World 
&bull; Literature** _(Shishuo Xinyu), compiled by Liu Yiqing of the Southern Dynasties_.

During the Three Kingdoms period, after **Cao Cao** passed away, his eldest son **Cao Pi** ascended the throne as Emperor Wen of Wei. Jealous of his younger brother **Cao Zhi**’s extraordinary talent and fearing he might threaten his throne, Cao Pi sought an excuse to eliminate him.

## 三、故事原文（文言文）| Original Text (Classical Chinese)

文帝嘗令東阿王七步作詩，不成者行大法。應聲便為詩曰：「**煮豆持作羹，漉菽以為汁。萁在釜下燃，豆在釜中泣。本是同根生，相煎何太急？** 」帝深有慚色。

---

## 四、白話文翻譯 | Vernacular Chinese Translation

有一天，曹丕把曹植叫到面前，命令他在**七步之內**作出一首詩，如果寫不出來，就要處死他。

曹植知道哥哥存心要害死他，又傷心又憤怒。他強忍悲痛，在七步之內當場吟出這首詩：

> 煮豆子來做豆羹，<br>
> 過濾豆渣留下汁。<br>
> 豆萁在鍋下燃燒，<br>
> 豆子在鍋中哭泣。<br>
> 我們本是同根生，<br>
> 為何煎熬這麼急？

曹丕聽了這首詩，感到羞愧，便放過了曹植。

_One day, Cao Pi summoned Cao Zhi and ordered him to compose a poem **within seven steps** — or face execution._

_Knowing his brother intended to kill him, Cao Zhi was both heartbroken and furious. Suppressing his grief, he composed and recited this poem within seven steps:_

> _Boil beans to make a bean stew, <br>
> Strain the dregs to get the juice. <br>
> Bean stalks burn beneath the pot, <br>
> Beans weep inside, in bitter thought. <br>
> We sprouted from one single root— <br>
> Why boil me with such furious fruit?_ 

_Hearing this poem, Cao Pi was deeply ashamed and spared his brother’s life._

## 五、英文翻譯 | English Translation of the Poem

以下為幾個常見的英文譯本：

### Version 1 (Literal):

> _Beans are boiled to make a soup, <br>
> Strained to get the juice anew. <br>
> Stalks burn beneath the pot so hot, <br>
> Beans weep inside, in bitter thought. <br>
> We sprouted from one single root, <br>
> Why burn me with such furious fruit?_

> _Beans are boiled by burning bean-stalks; bean-seeds inside the pot sob: “We are born of one same root.”_

## 六、成語寓意與用法 | Meaning and Usage

### 寓意 | Moral

「煮豆燃萁」用豆與豆萁本為同根所生的自然現象，比喻**兄弟骨肉之間不應互相迫害**。這個故事提醒人們：手足之間應當和諧相處、團結一心，不應因權力、名利而反目成仇。

_“Boiling beans with bean stalks” uses the natural phenomenon that_ **beans and bean stalks grow from the same root to symbolize that brothers and close kin should not harm one another**. _This story reminds us that siblings should live in harmony and unity, and not become enemies over power, fame, or profit_.

### 用法 | Usage


&bull; **例句 | Example**: _「公司內部若出現煮豆燃萁的局面，只會讓競爭對手有機可乘。」（If internal strife like “boiling beans with bean stalks” arises within a company, it will only give competitors an opportunity.）_

&bull; **現代詩例 | Modern Poetic Example**: 陳毅《過臨洮》詩：「煮豆燃萁傷往昔，而今團結樂陶陶。」

## 八、歷史影響 | Historical Impact

曹植雖然靠機智逃過一劫，但此後一再被貶，最終在四十一歲時憂鬱而死。「煮豆燃萁」與「七步詩」的故事流傳至今，成為中華文化中**警示骨肉相殘的經典典故**。

_Although Cao Zhi escaped death through his wit, he was repeatedly demoted afterward and ultimately died of depression at the age of forty-one. The story of “boiling beans with bean stalks” and the “Seven-Step Poem” has been passed down to this day as a classic Chinese tale_ **warning against fraternal strife**.
