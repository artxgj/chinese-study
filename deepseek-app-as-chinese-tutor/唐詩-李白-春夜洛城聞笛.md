**三、詩意詳細解析**

**（一）寫作背景**

〈春夜洛城聞笛〉是李白在洛陽（唐代東都）所作的一首七言絕句，具體創作時間不詳，約在開元年間（713-741年）李白寓居洛陽時 [1]。洛陽是唐代的東都，也是當時的音樂文化中心之一。詩人於春夜聞笛聲，觸動了深沉的鄉愁，揮筆寫下這首膾炙人口的思鄉之作 [2]。

**（二）字詞釋義**

- **洛城**：洛陽城，唐代東都。
- **玉笛**：精美的笛子，這裡指笛聲。
- **暗飛聲**：笛聲在暗中傳播飛揚。
- **散入春風**：笛聲隨著春風飄散。
- **滿洛城**：充滿了整個洛陽城。
- **折柳**：即〈折楊柳〉，漢代橫吹曲名，多寫離別之情。
- **故園**：故鄉、家園。

**（三）逐句解析**

- **首句「誰家玉笛暗飛聲」**：詩人於春夜中忽然聽到笛聲，不知是誰在吹奏。「暗飛聲」三字寫出笛聲在夜色中悄然而至、四處飄飛的情景，營造出一種神秘而悠遠的氛圍 [1]。

- **次句「散入春風滿洛城」**：笛聲隨春風飄散，充滿了整個洛陽城。春風不僅傳送了笛聲，也渲染了春天夜晚的溫馨與生機，但這美好的春景卻更凸顯了詩人的孤寂與鄉愁。

- **第三句「此夜曲中聞折柳」**：詩人聽出笛曲是〈折楊柳〉，這是漢代以來的離別曲調，以楊柳為題材，多寫送別之情。聽見此曲，詩人立刻被喚起了對故鄉的深切思念。

- **末句「何人不起故園情」**：詩人以反問作結，強烈地表達了鄉愁的普遍性——聽了這樣的笛聲，誰能不興起對故園的思念之情呢？這既是詩人自我的感慨，也是對所有離鄉遊子共通情感的深刻體現。

**（四）整體意境與主題**

這首詩以春夜聞笛為線索，將聽覺感受與視覺意象融為一體，表達了詩人深沉的思鄉之情 [1]。全詩由「聞」到「感」，由笛聲到春風，由客觀景物到主觀情感，層層遞進，自然流暢。

詩中的「折柳」一曲，既是具象的音樂，也是離別的象徵。春夜的洛陽城，春風沉醉，笛聲悠揚，但詩人卻在這美好的景色中感受到了孤獨與思念。

**（五）藝術特色**

- **以聲寫情**：通過笛聲、春風等聲音意象，將抽象的鄉愁具體化、形象化。
- **以小見大**：從偶然聽到的一曲笛聲，昇華到對所有離鄉遊子共通的鄉愁的深刻體悟。
- **語言流暢**：全詩語言自然流暢，一氣呵成，毫無雕琢之感。
- **反問收束**：末句以反問作結，語氣強烈，富有感染力。

**III. Detailed Analysis**

**(1) Historical Background**

"Chun Ye Luo Cheng Wen Di" is a seven-character quatrain written by Li Bai during his stay in Luoyang (the Eastern Capital of the Tang Dynasty), likely during the Kaiyuan era (713-741 AD) [1]. Luoyang was a major cultural and musical center at the time. On a spring night, the poet heard a flute melody that stirred deep feelings of homesickness, prompting this memorable poem [2].

**(2) Vocabulary Explanations**

- **洛城 (Luò Chéng)**: Luoyang city, the Eastern Capital of the Tang Dynasty.
- **玉笛 (Yù Dí)**: A beautiful jade flute; here it refers to the sound of the flute.
- **暗飛聲 (Àn Fēi Shēng)**: The sound of the flute flying secretly through the night.
- **散入春風 (Sàn Rù Chūn Fēng)**: The sound scatters into the spring breeze.
- **滿洛城 (Mǎn Luò Chéng)**: Filling the whole city of Luoyang.
- **折柳 (Zhé Liǔ)**: "Breaking Willows," a Han dynasty tune often associated with parting and farewell.
- **故園 (Gù Yuán)**: Hometown, homeland.

**(3) Line-by-Line Interpretation**

- **First Line: "From whose house does the jade flute send its hidden sound?"**  
  The poet suddenly hears a flute melody on the spring night, but does not know who is playing. The phrase "hidden sound" suggests the music drifting softly through the night, creating an atmosphere of mystery and distance [1].

- **Second Line: "It scatters into the spring wind, filling all Luoyang."**  
  The flute sound, carried by the spring breeze, fills the entire city. The spring breeze conveys both the sound and the warmth of the season, yet this beautiful spring scene only highlights the poet's loneliness and longing for home.

- **Third Line: "Tonight, in this tune, I hear 'Breaking Willows.'"**  
  The poet recognizes the tune as "Breaking Willows," a Han dynasty farewell song associated with separation. Hearing this, he is immediately overcome with intense homesickness.

- **Fourth Line: "Who could hear it and not be moved by thoughts of home?"**  
  The poem ends with a rhetorical question, powerfully expressing the universality of homesickness. This is both the poet's personal lament and a profound reflection on the shared feelings of all travelers.

**(4) Overall Mood and Themes**

The poem uses the experience of hearing a flute on a spring night to blend auditory sensations with visual imagery, expressing the poet's deep homesickness [1]. The poem moves from "hearing" to "feeling," from the flute's sound to the spring breeze, from objective scenery to subjective emotion, in a natural and flowing progression.

The "Breaking Willows" tune is both concrete music and a symbol of parting. On a beautiful spring night in Luoyang, amidst the gentle breeze and the sound of the flute, the poet feels a deep sense of loneliness and longing.

**(5) Artistic Features**

- **Expressing Emotion Through Sound**: Using the flute and the breeze, the poet makes abstract homesickness concrete and vivid.
- **From the Particular to the Universal**: A single flute melody leads to a profound insight into the universal experience of nostalgia.
- **Flowing Language**: The poem's language is natural and fluent, with no trace of artificiality.
- **Rhetorical Question**: The final rhetorical question is powerful and deeply moving.
