# 中國傳說生物清單 / List of Chinese Legendary Creatures

## 四靈 / Four Auspicious Beasts (Four Spirits)

源自遠古圖騰崇拜，最早見於戰國時期（公元前475-221年）。《禮記·禮運》明確將麟、鳳、龜、龍並稱四靈。  
Originated from ancient totem worship, first appeared in the Warring States period (475–221 BCE). The *Book of Rites* explicitly lists the Unicorn, Phoenix, Turtle, and Dragon as the Four Spirits.

| 繁體 / 簡體 (Name) | English | 起源 / Origin | 功能 / Function |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **龍 / 龙** (Lóng) | Dragon | 最早可追溯至戰國時期 / Earliest records trace back to the Warring States period. | 掌管降雨與豐收；帝王權威象徵；鱗蟲之長 / Controls rain and harvest; symbol of imperial authority; leader of scaled creatures. |
| **鳳凰 / 凤凰** (Fènghuáng) | Phoenix | 先秦典籍《爾雅》已有記載 / Recorded in the pre-Qin classic *Erya*. | 象徵后妃尊貴與吉祥和諧；羽蟲之長 / Symbol of empress dignity, auspiciousness, and harmony; leader of feathered creatures. |
| **麒麟** (Qílín) | Qilin / Chinese Unicorn | 明代定型為龍頭、牛蹄、鱗身 / Finalized in Ming dynasty as dragon head, ox hooves, scaled body. | 盛世祥兆；象徵智慧、高貴、和平與賜福子嗣；毛蟲之長 / Omen of prosperity; symbolizes wisdom, nobility, peace, and blessing offspring; leader of hairy creatures. |
| **龜 / 龟** (Guī) | Turtle | 上古時期即被視為靈物 / Considered a spiritual being since ancient times. | 長壽與不朽的象徵；甲蟲之長 / Symbol of longevity and immortality; leader of shelled creatures. |

## 四象（天之四靈） / Four Symbols

源自遠古星宿崇拜，殷商甲骨文已有四象形象的記載；戰國曾侯乙墓漆箱完整記錄了二十八宿與四象名稱。對應四方、四季與五行。  
Originated from ancient star worship; oracle bones of Shang dynasty already recorded their images. The lacquer box from Marquis Yi of Zeng (Warring States) fully records the 28 lunar mansions and the Four Symbols. They correspond to the four directions, four seasons, and five elements.

| 繁體 / 簡體 (Name) | English | 起源 / Origin | 功能 / Function |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **青龍 / 青龙** (Qīnglóng) | Azure Dragon | 東方七宿想像為龍形；東方屬木，色青 / Eastern seven lunar mansions imagined as a dragon; East belongs to Wood, color blue. | 守護東方；代表春季；五行屬木 / Guards the East; represents Spring; Wood element. |
| **朱雀** (Zhūquè) | Vermilion Bird | 南方七宿的總稱 / Collective name for the southern seven mansions. | 守護南方；代表夏季；五行屬火 / Guards the South; represents Summer; Fire element. |
| **白虎** (Báihǔ) | White Tiger | 西方七宿化成的神獸 / Divine beast formed from the western seven mansions. | 守護西方；代表秋季；五行屬金；辟邪、禳災 / Guards the West; represents Autumn; Metal element; wards off evil and disasters. |
| **玄武** (Xuánwǔ) | Black Tortoise | 北方七宿的總稱 / Collective name for the northern seven mansions. | 守護北方；代表冬季；五行屬水 / Guards the North; represents Winter; Water element. |

> 四象加上**黃龍 / 黄龙** (Huánglóng) 即為「天官五獸」。黃龍被視為中央之靈與皇權象徵。  
> Adding **Yellow Dragon** to the Four Symbols makes the "Five Celestial Officials". The Yellow Dragon is seen as the central spirit and symbol of imperial power.

## 上古十大神獸 / Ten Ancient Divine Beasts

記載於《山海經》、《淮南子》等上古神話典籍中。至漢代融入五行學說形成完整的象徵系統。  
Recorded in ancient mythological texts such as the *Classic of Mountains and Seas* and *Huainanzi*. By the Han dynasty, they were integrated with the Five Elements theory into a complete symbolic system.

| 繁體 / 簡體 (Name) | English | 起源 / Origin | 功能 / Function |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **青龍 / 青龙** | Azure Dragon | 見「四象」 / See "Four Symbols" above. | 東方守護神 / Eastern guardian deity. |
| **白虎** | White Tiger | 見「四象」 / See "Four Symbols" above. | 西方守護神 / Western guardian deity. |
| **朱雀** | Vermilion Bird | 見「四象」 / See "Four Symbols" above. | 南方守護神 / Southern guardian deity. |
| **玄武** | Black Tortoise | 見「四象」 / See "Four Symbols" above. | 北方守護神 / Northern guardian deity. |
| **黃龍 / 黄龙** | Yellow Dragon | 中央之靈，常見於九龍壁 / Central spirit, commonly seen on Nine-Dragon Walls. | 皇權象徵 / Symbol of imperial authority. |
| **應龍 / 应龙** (Yìnglóng) | Yinglong | 有翼之龍 / A winged dragon. | 曾助黃帝誅殺蚩尤，助大禹治水；與麒麟存在血脈關聯 / Helped the Yellow Emperor slay Chiyou and helped Yu control the floods; has a blood connection with the Qilin. |
| **螣蛇** (Téngshé) | Flying Snake | 能飛的蛇類 / A snake that can fly. | 上古六神獸之一 / One of the six ancient divine beasts. |
| **勾陳 / 勾陈** (Gōuchén) | Gouchen | 與麒麟相關 / Associated with the Qilin. | 主掌戊日權柄 / Governs the power of the Wu (earth) days. |
| **畢方 / 毕方** (Bìfāng) | Bifang | 《山海經》記載的獨足鳥 / A one-legged bird recorded in the *Classic of Mountains and Seas*. | 預警火災；傳說見到可長壽 / Warns of fires; legends say seeing it brings longevity. |
| **九尾狐** (Jiǔwěihú) | Nine-tailed Fox | 《山海經》出現三次 / Appears three times in the *Classic of Mountains and Seas*. | 早期為祥瑞與子孫繁榮的象徵；後被妖魔化 / Early symbol of good fortune and prosperity; later demonized as a man-eating spirit. |

## 四凶 / Four Perils

起源於《左傳》記載：舜帝將四個凶民族群流放到四方以抵禦魑魅。漢魏以後逐漸轉化為驅惡逐邪的神明。  
Originated from the *Zuo Commentary*: Emperor Shun exiled four凶 tribes to the four corners to ward off demons. After Han-Wei, they gradually transformed into deities that expel evil.

| 繁體 / 簡體 (Name) | English | 起源 / Origin | 功能 / Function |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **混沌** (Hùndùn) | Hundun | 帝鴻氏之不才子 / The unworthy son of Emperor Dihong. | 原為凶神；後轉化為驅邪神祇 / Originally an evil spirit; later transformed into an exorcism deity. |
| **窮奇 / 穷奇** (Qióngqí) | Qiongqi | 少皞氏之不才子 / The unworthy son of Emperor Shaohao. | 原為凶神，比喻背信棄義之人；後轉為瑞獸 / Originally an evil spirit, metaphor for treacherous people; later became an auspicious beast. |
| **檮杌 / 梼杌** (Táowù) | Taowu | 顓頊氏之不才子 / The unworthy son of Emperor Zhuanxu. | 比喻頑固不化、態度凶惡之人 / Metaphor for stubborn, fierce, and unyielding people. |
| **饕餮** (Tāotiè) | Taotie | 縉雲氏之不才子 / The unworthy son of Jinyun. | 以「能吃」為最大特點；後轉化為驅邪瑞獸 / Famous for its enormous appetite; later became an exorcism beast. |

## 龍生九子 / Nine Sons of the Dragon

「九」在傳統文化中常作虛數表示極多。最廣為流傳的版本出自明代李東陽《懷麓堂集》。  
In traditional culture, "nine" is often a number meaning "many". The most popular version comes from Li Dongyang's *Huailutang Collection* (Ming dynasty).

| 繁體 / 簡體 (Name) | Pinyin | 特長/愛好 / Specialty | 常見用途 / Common Use |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **囚牛** | Qiúniú | 愛好音樂 / Loves music. | 裝飾胡琴等樂器，稱「龍頭胡琴」 / Decorates string instruments like the huqin, called "dragon-head huqin". |
| **睚眥 / 睚眦** | Yázì | 性好殺 / Fierce and bloodthirsty. | 裝飾刀劍柄首 / Adorns sword and dagger hilts. |
| **嘲風 / 嘲风** | Cháofēng | 喜好冒險 / Loves adventure. | 殿角走獸 / Palace rooftop corner beast. |
| **蒲牢** | Púláo | 喜好鳴叫 / Likes to roar. | 鐘上獸鈕 / Bell knob decoration. |
| **狻猊** | Suānní | 喜好坐著 / Likes to sit still. | 佛座與香爐裝飾 / Buddha seat and incense burner decorations. |
| **霸下 / 贔屭 / 赑屃** | Bàxià / Bìxì | 喜愛負重 / Loves carrying heavy loads. | 駝碑石龜；象徵長壽與不朽 / Stone tortoise carrying steles; symbol of longevity. |
| **狴犴** | Bì'àn | 喜好訴訟 / Loves lawsuits and justice. | 裝飾監獄門首 / Adorns prison gates. |
| **負屭 / 负屃** | Fùxì | 喜愛文字 / Loves literature and calligraphy. | 裝飾碑文兩側 / Adorns the sides of stele inscriptions. |
| **螭吻 / 蚩吻** | Chīwěn | 喜好吞食 / Loves to swallow. | 殿脊獸頭，防火 / Palace ridge beast, symbolically prevents fire. |

> 不同版本的「龍生九子」名單有所差異，麒麟、饕餮、鰲魚等也曾被列入其中。  
> Different versions of the "Nine Sons" list vary; Qilin, Taotie, and Ao (giant turtle) are sometimes included.

## 十二獸（大儺十二獸） / Twelve Beasts (Nuo Exorcism Beasts)

起源於漢代儺儀，見於《後漢書·禮儀志》。十二位神獸各司吞食不同鬼疫。晉代《續漢書·禮儀志》記載了「十二獸吃鬼歌」。  
Originated from Han dynasty Nuo exorcism rituals, recorded in the *Book of Later Han*. Each of the twelve beasts eats specific ghosts and plagues. The *Book of Later Han (Sequel)* records the "Song of the Twelve Beasts Eating Ghosts".

| **雄伯** | Xióngbó | 食魅 / Eats demons. |
| **騰簡 / 腾简** | Téngjiǎn | 食不祥 / Eats inauspicious things. |
| **攬諸 / 揽诸** | Lǎnzhū | 食咎 / Eats misfortunes. |
| **伯奇** | Bóqí | 食夢（消除惡夢） / Eats dreams (dispels nightmares). |
| **強梁 / 强梁** | Qiángliáng | 與祖明共食磔死、寄生 / Eats corpses and parasites along with Zuming. |
| **祖明** | Zǔmíng | 與強梁共食磔死、寄生 / Eats corpses and parasites along with Qiangliang. |
