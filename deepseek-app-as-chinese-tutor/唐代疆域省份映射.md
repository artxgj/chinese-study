### 唐代“道”與現代省份的映射 / Mapping Tang Dynasty "Circuits" to Modern Provinces

唐代的行政區劃以“道”為最高層級，類似於今天的“省”。唐太宗時全國分為**十道**，唐玄宗時增為**十五道**。以下是十道與現代中國省份的對應關係：

The Tang Dynasty's highest administrative division was the **"Dao" (Circuit)** — analogous to today's **province**. Under Emperor Taizong, the empire was divided into **Ten Circuits**; under Emperor Xuanzong, this expanded to **Fifteen**. Below is the mapping of the Ten Circuits to modern Chinese provinces:

---

| 唐代十道 (Tang Ten Circuits) | 對應現代省份 (Corresponding Modern Provinces)                                                                                          |
| ------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 關內道 (Guannei Circuit)    | 陝西省全境、甘肅省東部、寧夏回族自治區、青海省部分地區 / Entire Shaanxi, eastern Gansu, Ningxia, and parts of Qinghai                                       |
| 河南道 (Henan Circuit)      | 河南省大部、山東省、江蘇省、安徽省部分地區 / Most of Henan, plus parts of Shandong, Jiangsu, and Anhui                                                |
| 河東道 (Hedong Circuit)     | 山西省全境、河南省部分地區 / Entire Shanxi, plus parts of Henan                                                                               |
| 河北道 (Hebei Circuit)      | 河北省全境、河南省部分地區 / Entire Hebei, plus parts of Henan                                                                                |
| 隴右道 (Longyou Circuit)    | 甘肅省全境、青海省部分地區、新疆維吾爾自治區東部 / Entire Gansu, parts of Qinghai, and eastern Xinjiang                                                  |
| 淮南道 (Huainan Circuit)    | 安徽省、江蘇省大部分地區 / Most of Anhui and Jiangsu                                                                                         |
| 江南道 (Jiangnan Circuit)   | 江蘇省南部、浙江省、安徽省南部、江西省、福建省、湖南省、廣東省大部分地區 / Southern Jiangsu, Zhejiang, southern Anhui, Jiangxi, Fujian, Hunan, and most of Guangdong |
| 山南道 (Shannan Circuit)    | 湖北省西部、湖南省西部、四川省東部、重慶市全境 / Western Hubei, western Hunan, eastern Sichuan, and entire Chongqing                                    |

### 福建泉州市在唐代的名稱 / The Name of Quanzhou, Fujian During the Tang Dynasty

**現代泉州市**在唐代的名稱經歷了多次演變：

The name of **modern-day Quanzhou** went through several changes during the Tang Dynasty:

**· 唐久視元年（700年）**：於今泉州市區置武榮州 (Wurongzhou)

**· 唐景雲二年（711年）**：武榮州改稱泉州 (Quanzhou)，治所即今泉州市址

**· 唐天寶元年（742年）**：泉州改為清源郡 (Qingyuan Commandery)

**· 唐乾元元年（758年）**：復名泉州 (Quanzhou)

**重要辨析 / Important Clarification**: 唐代“泉州”之名曾先後指代兩個不同地方。**711年之前**，“泉州”治所在今福州；**711年之後**，原福州之“泉州”改稱“閩州”（後改福州），而**武榮州**則更名為泉州，即今日之泉州市。因此，**今日泉州市在唐代中後期正式名稱為“泉州”**（亦曾短暫稱“清源郡”）。

Before **711 AD**, the name "Quanzhou" referred to the area of present-day **Fuzhou**. After **711 AD**, the original Quanzhou (in Fuzhou) was renamed "Minzhou" (later Fuzhou), while **Wurongzhou** was renamed Quanzhou — which is the city known as Quanzhou today. Thus, **the official name of present-day Quanzhou for most of the Tang Dynasty was "Quanzhou"** (briefly renamed "Qingyuan Commandery" from 742–758 AD).
