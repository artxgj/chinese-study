### Part 3: Bilingual Response

#### CHINESE SECTION

**摘要**

中國歷代數學與工程技術的發展，是一部從經驗積累到理論體系構建，並在世界範圍內長期保持領先的輝煌歷史。其發展軌跡既遵循自身內在的學術邏輯，也與不同時期的社會經濟需求緊密相連。數學方面，從先秦的籌算、漢唐的《九章算術》奠基，到宋元的賈憲三角、四元術等達到全盛，再到明清的珠算普及與西學融合，形成了一套獨特的以算法為核心的體系。工程技術方面，則在水利（都江堰、大运河）、建築（長城、故宮、木結構）、冶鐵、造紙、火藥、指南針等領域取得了舉世矚目的成就。

---

**細節**

**一、數學：從實用算學到理論高峰**

中國古代數學以獨特的**算法體系**為核心，與古希臘的公理化數學迥然不同。其發展大致可分為四個階段：

- **先秦至漢唐（奠基與體系形成）**：數學知識源於仰韶文化的陶器幾何圖案與商代甲骨文的十進制記數。至春秋戰國，十進位值制籌算已普遍使用。秦漢時期，《九章算術》的成書標誌著以算法為核心的數學體系正式確立，涵蓋分數運算、開平方、線性方程組解法、正負數運算等。同時期的《周髀算經》則記載了勾股定理的應用。

- **魏晉南北朝（理論奠基與突破）**：三國劉徽為《九章算術》作注，以演繹邏輯證明公式與解法，奠定理論基礎。他創立的「割圓術」用極限思想求圓周率。南北朝祖沖之將圓周率精確到小數點後第七位（3.1415926至3.1415927之間），並給出密率355/113，領先世界近一千年。

- **宋元（全盛時期）**：這是中國古代數學的巔峰。賈憲在開方術中引入「賈憲三角」（即楊輝三角）；秦九韶創立「大衍總數術」（即中國剩餘定理）；朱世傑發展出「四元術」求解多元高次方程組。印刷術的普及也極大地推動了數學知識的傳播。

- **明清（珠算普及與西學東漸）**：明代珠算完全取代籌算。16世紀末，西方數學通過耶穌會傳教士傳入，徐光啟與利瑪竇合譯《幾何原本》前六卷，開啟了中西數學融合的進程。

**二、工程技術：從經驗智慧到系統科學**

中國古代工程技術的發展與農業生產、國家治理、軍事防禦等需求息息相關：

- **先秦時期（技術奠基與水利先驅）**：商周青銅冶鑄技術精湛；春秋戰國鐵器出現推動生產力發展，槓桿、滑輪等簡單機械得到應用；誕生了世界年代最久且仍在使用的都江堰，以及最早的灌溉系統坎兒井；戰國《考工記》是手工業技術的重要總結。

- **秦漢時期（體系成熟與標準建立）**：秦代靈渠溝通長江與珠江水系；秦陵銅車馬展現卓越的金屬加工技術；漢代冶鐵技術高度發達，造紙術經蔡倫改進後成為主要書寫材料；張衡創製候風地動儀與渾天儀。

- **隋唐時期（宏偉工程與建築成熟）**：隋代開鑿大運河，貫通南北交通；宇文愷設計規劃嚴整的大興城（唐長安城）；唐代木結構建築的梁柱與斗拱技術趨於標準化；蒲津橋是大型冶鐵工程的典範。

- **宋元明清（技術高峰與全面總結）**：宋代指南針用於航海，火藥用於戰爭，活字印刷術得到應用；蘇頌製造水運儀象台；元明清時期，元大都、明清故宮是木結構建築的頂峰；明代宋應星的《天工開物》全面總結了農業和手工業生產技術。


#### ENGLISH SECTION

**Abstract**

The development of mathematics and engineering across China's dynastic periods represents a glorious history of empirical accumulation, theoretical systematization, and sustained world-leading achievement. Its trajectory was shaped by both internal academic logic and the socioeconomic needs of different eras. In mathematics, the tradition progressed from the counting rods of the pre-Qin period and the foundational *Nine Chapters on the Mathematical Art* in the Han and Tang dynasties, to the golden age of the Song and Yuan dynasties with achievements like Jia Xian's Triangle and the Four Unknowns method, and finally to the popularization of the abacus and the integration of Western knowledge during the Ming and Qing dynasties, forming a unique algorithm-centered system. Engineering achievements were equally remarkable, spanning hydraulic engineering (Dujiangyan, Grand Canal), architecture (Great Wall, Forbidden City, timber structures), metallurgy, papermaking, gunpowder, and the compass.

---

**Details**

**I. Mathematics: From Practical Computation to Theoretical Heights**

Ancient Chinese mathematics was centered on a unique **algorithmic system**, distinct from the axiomatic mathematics of ancient Greece. Its development can be roughly divided into four stages:

- **Pre-Qin to Han-Tang (Foundation and Systematization)**:
  - Mathematical knowledge originated from geometric patterns on pottery of the Yangshao culture and decimal notation on Shang dynasty oracle bones. By the Spring and Autumn and Warring States periods, rod calculus based on the decimal place-value system was widely used.
  - The **Han dynasty** saw the completion of the **Nine Chapters on the Mathematical Art**, establishing the algorithm-centered system. It covered operations with fractions, square roots, linear equations, and positive and negative numbers. The **Zhoubi Suanjing** also recorded the application of the Pythagorean theorem.

- **Wei-Jin and Northern-Southern Dynasties (Theoretical Foundation and Breakthroughs)**:
  - In the **Three Kingdoms** period, **Liu Hui** provided commentary on the *Nine Chapters*, proving its formulas with deductive logic. His "**method of exhaustion**" (割圆术) used limit concepts to calculate pi.
  - During the **Northern and Southern Dynasties**, **Zu Chongzhi** calculated pi to the **seventh decimal place (3.1415926 – 3.1415927)** and provided the **milü (355/113)**, a level of precision that was not surpassed for nearly a millennium.

- **Song and Yuan Dynasties (The Golden Age)**:
  - This was the zenith of ancient Chinese mathematics. **Jia Xian** introduced the "**Jia Xian Triangle**" (later known as Yanghui's Triangle). **Qin Jiushao** formulated the "**Dayan General Method**" (Chinese Remainder Theorem). **Zhu Shijie** developed the "**Four Unknowns**" method for solving multivariate higher-order equations. The advent of printing also greatly facilitated the spread of mathematical knowledge.

- **Ming and Qing Dynasties (Abacus and East-West Synthesis)**:
  - In the **Ming dynasty**, the **abacus** completely replaced counting rods. From the late 16th century, Western mathematics was introduced by Jesuit missionaries. **Xu Guangqi** and **Matteo Ricci** translated the first six books of Euclid's *Elements*, initiating a process of integrating Chinese and Western mathematics.

**II. Engineering: From Empirical Wisdom to Systematic Science**

The development of engineering in ancient China was closely tied to the needs of agriculture, state governance, and military defense:

- **Pre-Qin Period (Foundation and Hydraulic Pioneering)**:
  - Bronze casting was exquisite in the **Shang and Zhou** dynasties.
  - The advent of **iron tools** during the **Spring and Autumn and Warring States** periods boosted productivity, and simple machines like levers and pulleys were applied.
  - The world's oldest still-operational irrigation system, **Dujiangyan**, and the earliest irrigation canals, **Karez**, were built. The **Kaogong Ji** (Records of Examination of Craftsmen) of the Warring States period was an important summary of handicraft technology.

- **Qin and Han Periods (System Maturity and Standardization)**:
  - The **Lingqu Canal** of the **Qin dynasty** connected the Yangtze and Pearl River systems.
  - The bronze chariots and horses from the **Qin Shihuang Mausoleum** showcase exceptional metalworking skill.
  - The **Han dynasty** saw highly developed **iron smelting** and the improvement of **papermaking** by **Cai Lun**. **Zhang Heng** created the **seismograph** and **armillary sphere**.

- **Sui and Tang Dynasties (Grand Hydraulics and Architectural Maturity)**:
  - The **Grand Canal**, constructed in the **Sui dynasty**, was a major north-south transportation artery.
  - **Yuwen Kai** designed the **Daxingcheng** (Tang Chang'an), the most well-planned city of its time.
  - **Tang** architecture matured, with standardization of **beam-column** and **bracket-set** (dougong) techniques for timber structures. The **Pujin Bridge** exemplified large-scale iron casting.

- **Song, Yuan, Ming, and Qing Dynasties (Technological Peaks and Comprehensive Summaries)**:
  - In the **Song dynasty**, the compass was used in navigation, gunpowder in warfare, and movable-type printing was applied. **Su Song** constructed the monumental **Water-Powered Armillary Sphere and Celestial Globe Tower**.
  - During the **Yuan, Ming, and Qing** dynasties, engineering continued to develop. The **Yuan Dadu** (Khanbaliq) and the **Forbidden City** represent the pinnacle of timber construction.
  - **Song Yingxing's** *Tiangong Kaiwu* (Exploitation of the Works of Nature) in the **Ming dynasty** was an encyclopedic work summarizing agricultural and industrial techniques.
