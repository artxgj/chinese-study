## 三、成語出處 / Origin of the Idiom

### （一）最早出處 / Earliest Origin

#### 唐代·柳宗元《王氏伯仲唱和詩序》

> **原文**：「操斧于班、郢之門，斯強顏耳。」

> **白話翻譯**：拿著斧頭在魯班和郢都匠石（另一位著名巧匠）的門前耍弄，這真是厚著臉皮、不自量力啊。

> **English translation**: "To wield an axe at the gates of Ban and Ying — that is nothing but brazen-faced audacity."

### （二）演變過程 / Evolution of the Idiom

#### 明代·梅之煥《題李白墓》

> **原文**：「采石江邊一堆土，李白之名高千古；來來往往一首詩，魯班門前弄大斧。」

> **白話翻譯**：採石江邊那一堆黃土（指李白之墓），李白的大名流芳千古；來來往往的人都在此題詩一首，這就好比在魯班門前耍弄大斧一樣可笑。

> **English translation**: "By the Caishiji River lies a mound of earth, Li Bai's name echoes through the ages; here and there, visitors leave their poems — wielding a great axe before Lu Ban's gate."

由此詩句簡化演變成今天的成語「班門弄斧」。

From this poem, the idiom "Ban Men Nong Fu" (wielding an axe at Lu Ban's gate) was derived.

## 四、完整成語故事（一）：柳宗元與匠石 / Complete Story (Part 1): Liu Zongyuan and the Craftsman Shi

### 故事背景 / Background

唐代大文學家柳宗元的朋友王緯兄弟，出身於書香世家，富有文采。兩人來到京師創作詩文，柳宗元為他們的唱和詩作寫了一篇序文。

Liu Zongyuan, a great Tang Dynasty writer, had friends named Wang Wei and his brother, who came from a scholarly family and were highly talented in literature. When they came to the capital to write poetry, Liu Zongyuan wrote a preface for their responsive poems.

### 匠石的故事 / The Story of Craftsman Shi

魯班是春秋戰國時期的能工巧匠，被後世尊為木匠的祖師爺。當時還有一位工匠名叫「石」（匠石），是楚國京城郢都人，他的本事據說和魯班不相上下。

Lu Ban was a master craftsman from the Spring and Autumn and Warring States period, revered by later generations as the patron saint of carpenters. At that time, there was another craftsman named Shi (Craftsman Shi) from Ying, the capital of Chu, whose skill was said to be on par with Lu Ban's.

匠石的本領高到什麼程度呢？據說他有一個好朋友，兩人常常一起配合表演。這個朋友在鼻子尖上薄薄地塗一層白灰，匠石手持斧頭，瞄準鼻子尖，「唰」的一下，就用斧刃把鼻子尖上的白灰掃掉了，而鼻子尖的皮連蹭都沒有蹭到！

How skilled was Craftsman Shi? It is said he had a good friend, and the two often performed together. The friend would apply a thin layer of white powder on the tip of his nose. Craftsman Shi would then take his axe, aim at the tip of the nose, and with a "swish," sweep the powder off with the axe blade — without even grazing the skin of the nose![reference:34]

### 柳宗元的感嘆 / Liu Zongyuan's Remark

柳宗元因此感嘆道：假如有人拿著斧頭，敢在魯班和郢都的匠石這樣的宗師面前耍弄，那就太不自量力了。

Liu Zongyuan thus remarked: If someone dares to wield an axe in front of masters like Lu Ban and Craftsman Shi of Ying, that person is truly overestimating their own abilities.

> **這就是「班門弄斧」一語最早的雛形。**

> **This was the earliest prototype of the idiom "Ban Men Nong Fu."**

## 五、完整成語故事（二）：明代梅之煥與李白墓 / Complete Story (Part 2): Mei Zhihuan and Li Bai's Tomb

### 故事背景 / Background

李白是唐代偉大的詩人，被譽為「詩仙」，死後名傳千古。他的墓位於安徽當塗的採石磯（採石江邊）。

Li Bai was a great poet of the Tang Dynasty, known as the "Immortal Poet." His fame endured for millennia after his death. His tomb is located at Caishiji (by the Caishi River) in Dangtu, Anhui.

### 梅之煥的所見所感 / What Mei Zhihuan Saw and Felt

明朝有一位文人叫梅之煥。有一天，他來到採石磯遊覽李白的墓地，只見四周的牆壁上，到處塗塗寫寫，許多遊人都在李白的墓前留下了詩句。

During the Ming Dynasty, there was a scholar named Mei Zhihuan. One day, he visited Li Bai's tomb at Caishiji. He saw that the surrounding walls were covered with scribbled poems — many visitors had left their verses in front of Li Bai's grave.

梅之煥讀了這些詩後，發現其中不少寫得水平很低、十分拙劣。他覺得這些人在李白這位大詩人面前題詩獻醜，實在太可笑、太不自量力了。

After reading these poems, Mei Zhihuan found that many of them were of very poor quality. He thought it was utterly ridiculous and presumptuous for these people to show off their mediocre poems in front of the great poet Li Bai.

### 梅之煥的諷刺詩 / Mei Zhihuan's Satirical Poem

於是梅之煥也提筆寫了一首詩諷刺道：

So Mei Zhihuan picked up his brush and wrote a satirical poem:

> **「采石江邊一堆土，李白之名高千古；**<br>
> **來來往往一首詩，魯班門前弄大斧。」**

> "By the Caishi River lies a mound of earth, <br>
> Li Bai's name echoes through the ages; <br>
> Here and there, visitors leave their poems — <br>
> Wielding a great axe before Lu Ban's gate."

意思是說：那麼多人來來往往在李白的墓前題詩，也不想想，在李白的墓前賣弄詩文，那不就等於在魯班（木匠祖師）面前耍弄斧頭一樣，不知天高地厚嗎？

The meaning: So many people come and go, leaving poems at Li Bai's tomb — without realizing that showing off their poetry in front of Li Bai is just like wielding an axe in front of Lu Ban (the master carpenter). How utterly clueless![reference:59]

## 六、成語寓意 / Moral of the Idiom

### 核心寓意 / Core Meaning

「班門弄斧」告訴人們一個重要的道理：**要學會謙虛，不要隨便在行家面前賣弄本事，否則只會讓別人笑話**。

"Ban Men Nong Fu" teaches people an important lesson: **One must learn humility and not show off one's skills in front of experts, or else one will only become a laughingstock**.

一個人學了一點東西、有了一點本事，切不可妄自尊大、目空一切。這樣只會一葉障目、止步不前，停留在「半桶水」的水平上。

When a person has learned a little and acquired some skill, they must not become arrogant and look down on everyone else. Doing so will only blind them to their own limitations, halt their progress, and keep them at a "half-full bucket" level.

### 正確態度 / The Right Attitude

該成語也告訴人們：只有既謙虛謹慎，又敢於探索、勇於攀登，才能真正取得進步和成就。

The idiom also teaches that only by combining humility and caution with the courage to explore and strive can one truly make progress and achieve success.

> **相關俗語**：這句成語和俗語所說的 「關公面前耍大刀」 意思差不多。

> **Related saying**: This idiom has a similar meaning to the Chinese saying **"wielding a broadsword in front of Guan Yu"** (showing off in front of a true master).

## 七、用法說明 / Usage Notes

### 語義 / Meaning

比喻在行家面前賣弄本事，不自量力。

Metaphor for showing off one's meager skills in front of an expert — overestimating oneself.

### 使用類別 / Category

用在「愚昧無知」的表述上，含貶義。

Used in expressions of "foolish ignorance," with a derogatory connotation.

### 例句 / Example Sentences

| 中文例句 / Chinese             | English Translation                                                                                      |
| -------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 他可是大名家，你別班門弄斧了。            | He is a true master — don't show off in front of him.                                                    |
| 在您面前，我怎敢班門弄斧，隨便發言呢？        | How dare I show off in front of you and speak carelessly?                                                |
| 今天在各位名家面前談創作，我真是班門弄斧了。     | Discussing creative writing in front of all these masters today — I am truly showing off before experts. |
| 就憑你這點本事，就敢在專家面前班門弄斧，真是可笑。  | With just that little skill, you dare to show off in front of experts — how ridiculous!                  |
| 我又不是書法家，不敢在這裡班門弄斧地談什麼運筆技巧。 | I am not a calligrapher, so I dare not talk about brushwork techniques here in front of experts.         |

### 近義詞 / Synonyms

· 布鼓雷門 — beating a drum at Lei's gate (showing off in front of an expert)
· 貽笑大方 — to make a fool of oneself before experts

### 也可用作自謙 / Also Used as Self-deprecation

這句成語有時也用作自謙之詞，表示自己不敢在行家面前賣弄自己的小本領。

This idiom is sometimes used as a self-deprecating expression, indicating that one dare not show off one's meager skills in front of experts.
