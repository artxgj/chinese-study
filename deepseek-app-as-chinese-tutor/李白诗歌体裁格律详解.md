# 李白诗歌体裁与格律详解

# A Detailed Guide to the Poetic Forms and Metrics of Li Bai’s Poetry

## 一、总论 / Part I: General Introduction

李白（701–762）是中国唐代最伟大的诗人之一，其诗歌创作各体皆工。据学者统计，李白传世诗作中包括：五言律诗约88–127首、七言律诗约8–10首、五言绝句约73–83首、七言绝句约80–85首，以及大量古体诗、乐府诗和歌行体作品。

Li Bai (701–762) was one of the greatest poets of the Tang Dynasty, and he excelled in virtually every poetic form. According to scholarly statistics, his extant works include approximately 88–127 five-character regulated verses (wulü), 8–10 seven-character regulated verses (qilü), 73–83 five-character quatrains (wujue), 80–85 seven-character quatrains (qijue), along with a substantial number of ancient-style poems (gushi), yuefu ballads, and gexing songs.

李白虽精通格律，但更偏爱形式自由的古体诗，以其抒发奔放不羁的豪情。闻一多先生评价李白的律诗有着“古诗的灵魂”。

Although Li Bai was thoroughly versed in prosodic rules, he preferred the formally free ancient-style poetry to express his untrammeled spirit. The scholar Wen Yiduo remarked that Li Bai’s regulated verses possess “the soul of ancient poetry”.

## 二、古体诗 / Part II: Ancient-Style Poetry (Gushi)

古体诗又称古风，是相对近体诗（格律诗）而言的非格律诗体。古体诗不讲究平仄、黏连，对仗自由，押韵也较为宽松，可用邻韵或换韵。

Ancient-style poetry (_gushi_), also called _gufeng_, refers to non-regulated verse forms as opposed to regulated verse (_jintishi_). Ancient-style poetry does not require strict attention to level and oblique tones (_pingze_), tonal linkage (_nian_), or parallelism (_duizhang_); its rhyme schemes are also more flexible, allowing adjacent rhymes or multiple rhyme changes.

### (一) 四言古诗 / Four-Character Ancient Verse

四言古诗每句四字，继承《诗经》传统。李白四言诗数量较少，格律自由，不限平仄，可押韵可不押韵。

Four-character ancient verse features four characters per line, inheriting the tradition of the _Book of Songs_. Li Bai wrote relatively few such poems. The form has no fixed tonal requirements and may or may not rhyme.

### (二) 五言古诗（五古）/ Five-Character Ancient Verse (Wugu)

五古每句五字，句数不限。李白《古风》五十九首是其五古的代表作。五古的格律特点包括：

Five-character ancient verse features five characters per line with no fixed number of lines. Li Bai’s _Fifty-Nine Old-Style Poems (Gufeng)_ are the representative works of his five-character ancient verse. Its prosodic features include:


&bull; **字数**：每句五字 / **Line length**: five characters per line

&bull; **句数**：不限 / **Number of lines**: variable

&bull; **平仄**：不拘 / **Tonal pattern**: no fixed requirements

&bull; **对仗**：可用可不用 / **Parallelism**: optional

&bull; **押韵**：可押平韵或仄韵，可换韵 / **Rhyme**: may use level or oblique rhymes, may change rhymes

诗例 / _Example_: 

《古风·其一》“大雅久不作，吾衰竟谁陈……”（Gufeng No. 1: “The great tradition has long not been practiced…”）

### (三) 七言古诗（七古）/ Seven-Character Ancient Verse (Qigu)

七古每句七字，句数不限。李白七古较五古更具创造性，其中包含杂言体。格律特点：

Seven-character ancient verse features seven characters per line with no fixed number of lines. Li Bai’s seven-character ancient verse is even more innovative than his five-character works and includes mixed-line forms (_zayan ti_). Prosodic features:


&bull; **字数**：每句七字 / **Line length**: seven characters per line

&bull; **句数**：不限 / **Number of lines**: variable

&bull; **平仄**：不拘 / **Tonal pattern**: no fixed requirements

&bull; **对仗**：可用可不用 / **Parallelism**: optional

&bull; **押韵**：可押平韵或仄韵，可换韵 / **Rhyme**: may use level or oblique rhymes, may change rhymes

### (四) 杂言古诗（杂言体）/ Mixed-Line Ancient Verse (Zayan Ti)

杂言古诗每句字数长短不齐，是李白最具创造性的体裁之一。如《蜀道难》运用三言、四言、五言、七言乃至十一言句式参差错落。

Mixed-line ancient verse features lines of uneven length, and is one of Li Bai’s most creative forms. For example, _The Difficult Road_ to Shu uses lines ranging from three to eleven characters in irregular alternation.


&bull; **字数**：每句字数不定，长短错落 / **Line length**: variable, alternating between short and long lines

&bull; **句数**：不限 / **Number of lines**: variable

&bull; **平仄**：不拘 / **Tonal pattern**: no fixed requirements

&bull; **押韵**：频繁换韵，韵脚灵活 / **Rhyme**: frequent rhyme changes, flexible rhyme scheme

## 三、乐府诗 / Part III: Yuefu Poetry

乐府诗原为汉代乐府机关采集的歌谣，唐代诗人多沿用乐府古题进行创作。李白乐府诗约230首（含乐府130首、歌行100首），大部分采用古题而自出机杼。

Yuefu poetry originally referred to ballads collected by the Music Bureau (_Yuefu_) of the Han Dynasty. Tang poets often wrote using old _yuefu_ titles. Li Bai wrote about 230 _yuefu_ poems (including 130 yuefu and 100 _gexing_), mostly using old titles but with original innovation.

&bull; **题材**：沿用汉魏六朝乐府古题 / **Subject**: uses old yuefu titles from Han, Wei, and Six Dynasties

&bull; **形式**：多为古体，五言、七言、杂言皆有 / **Form**: mostly ancient-style, including five-, seven-, and mixed-line forms

&bull; **格律**：总体自由，部分作品可具格律诗特征 / **Metrics**: generally free; some works may exhibit regulated verse characteristics

&bull; **押韵**：灵活多变，可频繁换韵 / **Rhyme**: flexible, with frequent rhyme changes

诗例 / _Examples_: 

《将进酒》（_Invitation to Wine_）、《蜀道难》（_The Difficult Road to Shu_）、《关山月》（_Moon over Mountain Pass_）

## 四、歌行体 / Part IV: Gexing (Song-Form Poetry)

歌行体是乐府诗的变体，以“歌”“行”“吟”“谣”等为题，是李白最擅长的体裁。歌行体音节、格律较为自由，形式富于变化。

_Gexing_ is a variant of _yuefu_ poetry, titled with words such as “song” (_ge_), “ballad” (_xing_), “chant” (_yin_), or “folk song” (_yao_). It is the genre in which Li Bai excelled most. The prosody of gexing is relatively free and formally varied.

&bull; **句式**：以七言为主，杂言为辅 / **Line structure**: predominantly seven-character, supplemented by mixed lines

&bull; **句数**：不限 / **Number of lines**: variable

&bull; **平仄**：不拘 / **Tonal pattern**: no fixed requirements

&bull; **对仗**：不强求 / **Parallelism**: not required

&bull; **押韵**：自由换韵，节奏感强 / **Rhyme**: free rhyme changes with strong rhythmic flow

诗例 / _Examples_: 

《将进酒》（_Invitation to Wine_）用三言、五言、七言错杂而成，全诗转换六个韵；《梦游天姥吟留别》（_Dreaming of Tianmu Mountain_）

## 五、近体诗（格律诗）/ Part V: Regulated Verse (Jintishi)

近体诗是唐代成熟的有严格格律规范的诗歌体裁。李白虽以古体诗闻名，但也创作了大量近体诗。研究表明李白对各种格律规律了然于胸，大醉之后格律更见峻整细密。

_Jintishi_ (regulated verse) is a poetic form with strict prosodic rules that matured in the Tang Dynasty. Although Li Bai is famous for his ancient-style poetry, he also wrote a significant number of regulated verses. Research shows that Li Bai was thoroughly familiar with all prosodic rules, and his metrics became even more precise when he was drunk.

### (一) 五言律诗（五律）/ Five-Character Regulated Verse (Wulü)

五律每句五字，全诗八句，共四十字。李白有五律88首（一说127首）。

Five-character regulated verse has five characters per line and eight lines per poem, totaling forty characters. Li Bai wrote 88 such poems（some sources say 127）.

格律规则 / _Prosodic Rules_:

| 项目 / _Item_              | 规则 / _Rule_                                                                                   |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 字数 / _Line length_       | 每句五字 / five characters per line                                                             |
| 句数 / _Number of lines_   | 八句 / eight lines                                                                            |
| 总字数 / _Total characters_ | 四十字 / forty characters                                                                      |
| 平仄 / _Tonal pattern_     | 严格依照平仄谱 / strict tonal patterns                                                             |
| 对仗 / _Parallelism_       | 中间两联（颔联、颈联）必须对仗 / the middle two couplets must have parallelism                             |
| 押韵 / _Rhyme_             | 一般押平声韵，偶句押韵，一韵到底 / usually level-tone rhyme, rhyming on even lines, single rhyme throughout |

诗例 / _Example_: 

《渡荆门送别》（Farewell at the Ferry of Jingmen）——“渡远荆门外，来从楚国游。山随平野尽，江入大荒流。月下飞天镜，云生结海楼。仍怜故乡水，万里送行舟。”此诗严格合律，仅第七句“仍怜故乡水”为“平平仄平仄”，是“平平平仄仄”的变格。

### (二) 七言律诗（七律）/ Seven-Character Regulated Verse (Qilü)

七律每句七字，全诗八句，共五十六字。李白七律较少，仅8–10首。

Seven-character regulated verse has seven characters per line and eight lines per poem, totaling fifty-six characters. Li Bai wrote relatively few—only 8 to 10.

格律规则 / Prosodic Rules:

| 项目 / _Item_              | 规则 / _Rule_                                                                                   |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 字数 / _Line length_       | 每句七字 / seven characters per line                                                            |
| 句数 / _Number of lines_   | 八句 / eight lines                                                                            |
| 总字数 / _Total characters_ | 五十六字 / fifty-six characters                                                                 |
| 平仄 / _Tonal pattern_     | 严格依照平仄谱 / strict tonal patterns                                                             |
| 对仗 / _Parallelism_       | 中间两联必须对仗 / the middle two couplets must have parallelism                                    |
| 押韵 / _Rhyme_             | 一般押平声韵，偶句押韵，一韵到底 / usually level-tone rhyme, rhyming on even lines, single rhyme throughout |

诗例 / _Example_: 

《登金陵凤凰台》（_On Climbing the Phoenix Terrace in Jinling_）

### (三) 五言绝句（五绝）/ Five-Character Quatrain (Wujue)

五绝每句五字，全诗四句，共二十字。李白有五绝73–83首。

Five-character quatrain has five characters per line and four lines per poem, totaling twenty characters. Li Bai wrote 73 to 83 such poems.

五绝分两类 / _Two Subtypes_:

#### 1\. 律绝（近体绝句）/ Regulated Quatrain (Lüjue)

&bull; 严格依照平仄谱 / Strict tonal patterns

&bull; 押平声韵，一韵到底 / Level-tone rhyme, single rhyme throughout

&bull; 可对仗可不对仗 / Parallelism optional

#### 2\. 古绝（古体绝句）/ Ancient Quatrain (Gujue)

&bull; 不讲究平仄 / No fixed tonal requirements

&bull; 可押平韵或仄韵 / May use level or oblique rhymes

&bull; 格律自由 / Prosodically free

诗例 / Examples:

&bull; **律绝** / _Regulated_: 《独坐敬亭山》（_Sitting Alone on Jingting Mountain_）

&bull; **古绝** / _Ancient_: 《静夜思》（_Thoughts on a Tranquil Night_）——不讲究格律，是古体五言绝句

### (四) 七言绝句（七绝）/` `Seven-Character Quatrain (Qijue)

七绝每句七字，全诗四句，共二十八字。李白有七绝80–85首，是初盛唐诗人中七绝数量最多的之一。

Seven-character quatrain has seven characters per line and four lines per poem, totaling twenty-eight characters. Li Bai wrote 80 to 85 such poems, making him one of the most prolific seven-character quatrain writers among early and High Tang poets.

格律规则（以平起平收首句入韵式为例）/ _Prosodic Rules (using the level-flat, level-ending, first-line rhyming pattern as example)_:

```
平平仄仄仄平平（韵）
仄仄平平仄仄平（韵）
仄仄平平平仄仄
平平仄仄仄平平（韵）
```

诗例 / _Example_: 《早发白帝城》（_Leaving the White Emperor City at Dawn_）——

朝辞白帝彩云间（平），千里江陵一日还（平）。<br>
两岸猿声啼不住，轻舟已过万重山（平）。

## 六、总结 / Part VI: Conclusion

李白诗歌体裁涵盖了中国古典诗歌的几乎所有主要形式。兹将各体裁格律要点汇总如下：

Li Bai’s poetry encompasses virtually all major forms of classical Chinese poetry. The key prosodic features of each genre are summarized below:

| 体裁 / _Genre_            | 每句字数 / _Chars/line_     | 句数 / _Lines_    | 平仄 / _Tonal pattern_        | 对仗 / _Parallelism_            | 押韵 / _Rhyme_                |
| --------------------- | --------------------- | ------------- | ------------------------- | --------------------------- | ------------------------- |
| 四言古诗 / _4-char ancient_ | 4                     | 不限 / variable | 不拘 / free                 | 不拘 / free                   | 自由 / free                 |
| 五言古诗 / _5-char ancient_ | 5                     | 不限 / variable | 不拘 / free                 | 不拘 / free                   | 自由 / free                 |
| 七言古诗 / _7-char ancient_ | 7                     | 不限 / variable | 不拘 / free                 | 不拘 / free                   | 自由 / free                 |
| 杂言古诗 / _mixed-line_     | 不定 / variable         | 不限 / variable | 不拘 / free                 | 不拘 / free                   | 自由 / free                 |
| 乐府诗 / _Yuefu_           | 不定 / variable         | 不限 / variable | 多不拘 / mostly free         | 不拘 / free                   | 自由 / free                 |
| 歌行体 / _Gexing_          | 以七言为主 / mostly 7-char | 不限 / variable | 不拘 / free                 | 不强求 / optional              | 自由换韵 / free changes       |
| 五言律诗 / _Wulü_           | 5                     | 8             | 严格 / strict               | 中二联必须 / middle two required | 平韵一韵到底 / level, single    |
| 七言律诗 / _Qilü_           | 7                     | 8             | 严格 / strict               | 中二联必须 / middle two required | 平韵一韵到底 / level, single    |
| 五言绝句 / _Wujue_          | 5                     | 4             | 律绝严/古绝自由 / strict or free | 不拘 / optional               | 律绝平韵/古绝自由 / level or free |
| 七言绝句 / _Qijue_          | 7                     | 4             | 律绝严/古绝自由 / strict or free | 不拘 / optional               | 律绝平韵/古绝自由 / level or free |

总体而言，李白“各体皆工”，他既能写出格律严谨的律诗绝句，更能以形式自由的古体诗、乐府和歌行体驰骋才情。正如赵翼所评：“青莲集中古诗多，律诗少……盖才气豪迈，全以神运，自不屑束缚于格律、对偶。”

In sum, Li Bai “excelled in all forms”. He could produce strictly regulated _lüshi_ and _jueju_, but even more so, he used the formally free ancient-style poetry, _yuefu_, and _gexing_ to give full play to his genius. As Zhao Yi remarked: “In the collected works of Li Bai, ancient-style poems are many, regulated verses few… This is because his talent was so bold and untrammeled that he disdained to be bound by prosodic rules and parallelism”.
