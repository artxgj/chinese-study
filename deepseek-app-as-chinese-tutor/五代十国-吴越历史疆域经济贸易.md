# 吴越国（907年—978年）完整详解

## 一、概述 / Overview

#### 中文:
吴越国是五代十国时期的十国之一，由杭州临安人钱镠于907年所建，定都杭州（称西府），以越州（今绍兴）为陪都（称东府）。吴越国历三代五王，至978年末代国王钱弘俶（钱俶）“纳土归宋”，立国72年。若从893年钱镠就任镇海节度使算起，则存续85年，是五代十国中存续时间最长的政权。吴越国以“保境安民”和“善事中原”为国策，是十国中政局最稳定、经济最繁荣的政权之一。

#### English:
The Wuyue Kingdom (907–978) was one of the Ten Kingdoms during the Five Dynasties and Ten Kingdoms period. It was founded by Qian Liu, a native of Lin'an, Hangzhou, in 907, with its capital at Hangzhou (known as the Western Capital) and Shaoxing (known as the Eastern Capital) as the secondary capital. The kingdom lasted for 72 years through three generations and five kings, until the final king Qian Hongchu (Qian Chu) surrendered his territory to the Song Dynasty in 978. Counting from 893 when Qian Liu assumed the position of Zhenhai Military Commissioner, the kingdom endured for 85 years, making it the longest-lasting regime among the Ten Kingdoms. The Wuyue Kingdom pursued the state policies of "protecting the borders and comforting the people" and "serving the Central Plains", and was one of the most politically stable and economically prosperous regimes among the Ten Kingdoms.

## 二、历史 / History

### 2.1 建国背景 / Foundation Background

#### 中文:
吴越国的基业始于唐末的动荡。钱镠（852-932）早年以贩盐为业，后成为董昌部将。886年，钱镠奉命攻破越州，开始占据杭州。893年，钱镠被唐朝廷封为镇海军节度使。895年，董昌在越州称帝，钱镠奉命讨伐并平定之，至此基本控制了两浙地区。896年，钱镠平定浙东，奠定了吴越国的基本疆域。

#### English:
The foundation of the Wuyue Kingdom was laid amid the turmoil of the late Tang Dynasty. Qian Liu (852–932) initially worked as a salt trader before becoming a general under Dong Chang. In 886, Qian Liu captured Yuezhou (present-day Shaoxing) on orders and began to occupy Hangzhou. In 893, he was appointed Zhenhai Military Commissioner by the Tang court. In 895, when Dong Chang declared himself emperor in Yuezhou, Qian Liu was ordered to suppress the rebellion, after which he essentially gained control over the entire Liangzhe (two Zhejiang) region. In 896, Qian Liu pacified eastern Zhejiang, establishing the foundational territory of the Wuyue Kingdom.

### 2.2 立国与延续 / Establishment and Continuation

#### 中文:
907年，朱温篡唐建立后梁，钱镠审时度势，接受后梁册封为吴越王，正式开启了钱氏对两浙地区长达七十二年的统治。923年，钱镠被加封为吴越国王，建朝廷设百官，吴越国正式建立。此后吴越国历代君主均继续臣服于中原政权（后梁、后唐、后晋、后汉、后周及北宋），向其进贡并接受册封。

#### English:
In 907, when Zhu Wen usurped the Tang throne and established the Later Liang, Qian Liu assessed the situation and accepted the Later Liang's enfeoffment as King of Wuyue, officially beginning the Qian family's 72-year rule over the Liangzhe region. In 923, Qian Liu was further enfeoffed as King of Wuyue, establishing a royal court and official bureaucracy, marking the formal establishment of the Wuyue Kingdom. Subsequently, successive rulers of Wuyue continued to pledge allegiance to the Central Plains regimes (Later Liang, Later Tang, Later Jin, Later Han, Later Zhou, and the Northern Song), paying tribute and accepting investiture.

### 2.3 纳土归宋 / Surrender to the Song

#### 中文:
978年（北宋太平兴国三年），末代国王钱弘俶为避免战乱，主动献土并入北宋。吴越国是北宋开国战争期间唯一没有被宋朝使用武力攻下的国家。这一和平统一之举使两浙地区实现了平稳过渡，成为中华文明统一性思想的集中彰显。

#### English:
In 978 (the third year of the Taiping Xingguo era of the Northern Song), the final king Qian Hongchu voluntarily surrendered his territory to the Northern Song to avoid warfare. The Wuyue Kingdom was the only state during the Northern Song's founding wars that was not conquered by military force. This peaceful reunification enabled a smooth transition for the Liangzhe regionand exemplified the ideal of Chinese cultural unity.

## 三、疆域 / Territory

### 3.1 疆域范围 / Territorial Extent

#### 中文:
吴越国全盛时期疆域涵盖“十三州一军八十六县”，约为现今浙江省全境、上海市全境、江苏省东南部（苏州市）以及福建省东北部（福州市）。

具体辖州包括：苏州、湖州、杭州、越州（绍兴）、明州（宁波）、台州、温州、婺州（金华）、衢州、处州（丽水）、睦州（杭州西部地区），以及秀州（嘉兴，后析置）和福州（947年获得）等。吴越国还对润州（镇江）、常州拥有声索的法理依据。人口约55万户。

#### English:
At its peak, the Wuyue Kingdom's territory encompassed "thirteen prefectures, one military command, and eighty-six counties", roughly corresponding to the entirety of present-day Zhejiang Province, all of Shanghai Municipality, southeastern Jiangsu Province (Suzhou), and northeastern Fujian Province (Fuzhou).

The specific prefectures included: Suzhou, Huzhou, Hangzhou, Yuezhou (Shaoxing), Mingzhou (Ningbo), Taizhou, Wenzhou, Wuzhou (Jinhua), Quzhou, Chuzhou (Lishui), Muzhou (western Hangzhou area), as well as Xiuzhou (Jiaxing, later established) and Fuzhou (acquired in 947). The kingdom also had legal claims to Runzhou (Zhenjiang) and Changzhou. The population was approximately 550,000 households.

### 3.2 都城 / Capitals

#### 中文:


&bull; 西府（西都）： 杭州（钱塘），为正式都城

&bull; 东府（东都）： 越州（今绍兴），为陪都

#### English:


&bull; Western Capital: Hangzhou (Qiantang), the official capital

&bull; Eastern Capital: Yuezhou (present-day Shaoxing), the secondary capital

## 四、经济 / Economy

### 4.1 农业与水利 / Agriculture and Water Conservancy

#### 中文:
吴越国高度重视农业生产，钱镠采取“保境安民”方针，重农桑、兴水利。最著名的水利工程是修筑钱塘江海塘，钱镠亲自规划，以“石囤木桩法”在钱塘江岸筑起蜿蜒数百公里的海塘。此外还疏浚湖泊，垦荒兴农。这些措施使两浙之地获得了一个较长的稳定发展时期，为“上有天堂，下有苏杭”的盛景奠定了基础。

#### English:
The Wuyue Kingdom placed great emphasis on agricultural production. Qian Liu pursued the policy of "protecting the borders and comforting the people," promoting agriculture, sericulture, and water conservancy. The most famous water conservancy project was the construction of the Qiantang River seawall, personally planned by Qian Liu using the "stone-bag and wooden-pile method," building hundreds of kilometers of seawall along the Qiantang River. The kingdom also dredged lakes and reclaimed wasteland for farming. These measures provided the Liangzhe region with a prolonged period of stable development, laying the foundation for the renowned scenery of "above there is paradise, below there are Suzhou and Hangzhou".

### 4.2 工商业繁荣 / Commercial and Industrial Prosperity

#### 中文:
吴越国经济繁荣盛景，北宋文学家苏轼描绘为“铸山煮海，象犀珠玉之富，甲于天下”。杭州城“灯火家家市，笙歌处处楼”，户口从唐末的万户激增至十万户，成为“东南第一州”。欧阳修记载吴越国“习俗工巧，邑屋华丽，盖十余万家”。

两浙地区在吴越国时期发生了显著的经济重心转移——唐代处于第三梯队的沿海明州（宁波）、台州、温州，到宋代已升至第二梯队，完成了从沿海“不发达”地区到沿海发达地区的转变。

#### English:
The economic prosperity of the Wuyue Kingdom was captured by Northern Song literary giant Su Shi, who wrote: "Mining mountains and boiling the sea, the wealth of elephant tusks, rhino horns, pearls, and jade is unmatched under heaven". Hangzhou was described as "every household a market with lanterns, every building resounding with song and dance," its households surging from 10,000 in the late Tang to 100,000, becoming the "foremost prefecture in the southeast". Ouyang Xiu recorded that the Wuyue Kingdom had "refined customs and craftsmanship, magnificent urban dwellings, with over 100,000 households".

During the Wuyue period, the Liangzhe region experienced a significant economic shift—coastal prefectures like Mingzhou (Ningbo), Taizhou, and Wenzhou, which ranked in the third tier during the Tang Dynasty, rose to the second tier by the Song Dynasty, completing a transformation from "underdeveloped" coastal areas to developed coastal regions.

## 五、贸易 / Trade

### 5.1 海上贸易的动因 / Motivation for Maritime Trade

#### 中文:
吴越国因受到北方邻国吴国和南唐的陆上军事威胁，陆路交通受阻，遂大力发展航海贸易。钱镠及历代统治者清醒地认识到，以“一军十三州”的有限疆域和国力，逐鹿中原无异于自取灭亡，因此选择坚定不移地“保境安民”，将经略重心投向海洋，“大兴舟楫商贾之利”。

#### English:
Facing military threats from the northern neighboring states of Wu and Southern Tang, which blocked overland routes, the Wuyue Kingdom vigorously developed maritime trade. Qian Liu and subsequent rulers clearly recognized that with the limited territory and national strength of "one military command and thirteen prefectures," competing for supremacy in the Central Plains would be self-destructive. They therefore steadfastly pursued the policy of "protecting the borders and comforting the people," directing their strategic focus toward the ocean and "greatly promoting the profits of shipping and commerce".

### 5.2 港口与贸易网络 / Ports and Trade Networks

#### 中文:
杭州和明州（宁波）是吴越国最重要的对外贸易港口。杭州港因海塘修建而吞吐能力大幅提升，海船可安全停泊装卸；明州港商船云集。此外台州、温州的港口也陆续投入运转。

吴越国在北方沿海地区遍设“回易务”（又称“博易务”），任命回易官吏进行贸易。这些机构负责管理海外贸易、征收关税、规范交易、培养水手和翻译、规划航线、修缮港口、接待外商等。

#### English:
Hangzhou and Mingzhou (Ningbo) were the most important foreign trade ports of the Wuyue Kingdom. Hangzhou's port saw a qualitative leap in throughput capacity after the construction of the seawall, allowing ships to dock and unload safely; Mingzhou port was crowded with merchant ships. The ports of Taizhou and Wenzhou were also gradually put into operation.

The Wuyue Kingdom established "Exchange Affairs Offices" (also known as "Trade Affairs Offices") throughout the northern coastal areas, appointing officials to conduct trade. These institutions were responsible for managing overseas trade, collecting customs duties, regulating transactions, training sailors and interpreters, planning routes, repairing ports, and receiving foreign merchants.

### 5.3 贸易路线 / Trade Routes

#### 中文:
吴越国的海上贸易路线极为广阔：


&bull; 向东： 经黄海、东海到达朝鲜半岛（新罗、后百济、高丽）和日本

&bull; 向南： 经南海抵达东南亚、南亚诸古国

&bull; 向西： 过印度洋，驶向西亚、北非，航程最远到达波斯湾，贸易版图遍及半个地球

&bull; 向北： 与契丹（辽国）建立海上贸易联系

《五代史补》记载当时吴越国都城杭州“舟楫辐辏，望之不见其首尾”。

#### English:
The maritime trade routes of the Wuyue Kingdom were extraordinarily extensive:


&bull; Eastward: Across the Yellow Sea and East China Sea to the Korean Peninsula (Silla, Later Baekje, Goryeo) and Japan

&bull; Southward: Through the South China Sea to Southeast Asia and the ancient kingdoms of South Asia

&bull; Westward: Across the Indian Ocean to West Asia and North Africa, with the farthest voyages reaching the Persian Gulf—a trade network spanning half the globe

&bull; Northward: Maritime trade connections with the Khitan (Liao Dynasty)

The _Supplement to the History of the Five Dynasties_ records that at the Wuyue capital Hangzhou, "ships converged like the spokes of a wheel, their prows and sterns invisible from end to end".

### 5.4 贸易商品 / Trade Goods

#### 中文:
出口商品： 丝织品（吴绫杭绸）、瓷器（越窑秘色瓷）、茶叶、铜器等

进口商品： 朝鲜半岛的马匹、药材；日本的沙金、木材、香药；来自西亚的象牙、犀角、珠玉等奇珍异宝

苏轼称吴越国“象犀珠玉之富，甲于天下”。

#### English:
Exports: Silk textiles (Wu silk and Hangzhou brocade), porcelain (Yue ware secret-color celadon), tea, and copper goods

Imports: Horses and medicinal herbs from the Korean Peninsula; gold dust, timber, and aromatic medicines from Japan; and exotic treasures such as ivory, rhino horn, pearls, and jade from West Asia

Su Shi remarked that the Wuyue Kingdom's "wealth of elephant tusks, rhino horns, pearls, and jade is unmatched under heaven".

### 5.5 贸易模式与制度 / Trade Model and Institutions

#### 中文:
吴越国施行的是古代罕见的“官民协同出海”模式——官方负责后勤和武装护航，远洋交易的基本是民间商船。这种分工既让官方免于承担商业风险，又防止民间商人坐大成为武装集团，实现了近百年高效合作。

吴越国还专门设立“博易务”等机构管理贸易。在北方沿海州城，博易务甚至可以“自置刑狱”，拥有执法权，处理吴越国商人与当地的贸易纠纷。史书记载其“航海所入，岁贡百万”，足见海上贸易极大地推动了吴越国的经济发展。

#### English:
The Wuyue Kingdom implemented a rare "official-civilian coordinated maritime enterprise" model in ancient times—the government provided logistics and armed escort, while private merchant ships conducted the actual long-distance trading. This division of labor freed the government from commercial risks while preventing private merchants from growing into armed groups that could threaten state stability, enabling nearly a century of highly effective cooperation.

The Wuyue Kingdom also established specialized institutions such as the "Trade Affairs Office" to manage trade. In northern coastal prefectures, these offices could even "establish their own courts" with enforcement powers to handle trade disputes between Wuyue merchants and local traders. Historical records state that "maritime trade revenue contributed millions annually to the tribute", demonstrating how maritime trade tremendously propelled the Wuyue Kingdom's economic development.

## 六、总结 / Summary

#### 中文:
吴越国作为五代十国时期存续时间最长的政权，以“保境安民”为国策，在近百年间为两浙地区创造了和平稳定的发展环境。其疆域以浙江为中心，全盛时覆盖今浙江、上海、苏州及福州等地。在经济上，吴越国重农桑、兴水利，使江南地区成为富庶之地；在贸易上，面对陆上威胁，吴越国转而向海而生，发展出覆盖半个地球的远洋贸易网络，杭州、明州等港口成为海上丝绸之路的重要节点。吴越国的海洋精神与商业智慧，不仅积累了“甲于天下”的财富，更为后世留下了宝贵的文化遗产。

#### English:
As the longest-lasting regime among the Ten Kingdoms during the Five Dynasties and Ten Kingdoms period, the Wuyue Kingdom pursued the state policy of "protecting the borders and comforting the people," creating a peaceful and stable environment for development in the Liangzhe region for nearly a century. Its territory centered on Zhejiang, reaching its peak to encompass present-day Zhejiang, Shanghai, Suzhou, and Fuzhou. Economically, the kingdom promoted agriculture, sericulture, and water conservancy, transforming the Jiangnan region into a land of prosperity. In trade, facing land-based threats, the Wuyue Kingdom turned toward the sea, developing a far-reaching maritime trade network that spanned half the globe, with ports like Hangzhou and Mingzhou becoming key nodes on the Maritime Silk Road. The maritime spirit and commercial wisdom of the Wuyue Kingdom not only amassed wealth "unmatched under heaven" but also left a precious cultural legacy for future generations.
