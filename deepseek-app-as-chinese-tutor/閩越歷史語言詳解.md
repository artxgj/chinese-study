# 閩越（Minyue）完整详解

## 一、概述 / Overview

閩越（Mǐn Yuè），又稱閩越國、閩粵、東越，是中國福建省歷史上的一個古國，存在於約公元前334年至公元前110年。閩越是由南遷的越國王族與福建當地的古閩人（七閩）融合而形成的百越一支。閩越國是福建歷史上最早、最強盛的地方割據政權，在近一個世紀的歲月中創造了燦爛的閩越古國文化。

**Minyue** (also known as the Minyue Kingdom, Minyue State, or Dongyue) was an ancient kingdom in what is now Fujian Province, China. It existed from approximately 334 BC to 110 BC. Minyue was formed by the fusion of the royal family of the Yue State who migrated southward with the local ancient Min people (known as "Seven Min") in Fujian, becoming a branch of the Baiyue peoples. The Minyue Kingdom was the earliest and most powerful regional regime in Fujian's history, creating a brilliant Minyue ancient culture over nearly a century.

---

## 二、創立與滅亡 / Rise and Fall

### 創立（Establishment）

&bull; **約公元前334年（戰國中期）** ：越國被楚國打敗後分崩離析，越王族分散在東南沿海，其中一支遷居福建地區，逐漸與當地閩族土著相融合，形成「閩越族」。越王勾踐的後裔成為閩越的統治者。

&bull; **戰國晚期**：閩越族首領**無諸**自立為閩越王。

&bull; **公元前222年（秦王政二十五年）** ：秦國消滅楚國後進軍閩越，在閩地設置**閩中郡**，廢去無諸的王號，降為「君長」。

&bull; **公元前206年**：秦朝滅亡，楚漢戰爭爆發。無諸北上中原，幫助劉邦擊敗項羽。

&bull; **公元前202年（漢高祖五年）** ：劉邦重新封無諸為**閩越王**，建都**東冶**（今福州市鼓樓區屏山）。閩越國正式確立。

---

&bull; **~334 BC (Mid-Warring States period)** : The Yue State was defeated and collapsed after being conquered by Chu. The royal family scattered along the southeastern coast. One branch migrated to the Fujian region and gradually merged with the local Min indigenous people, forming the "Minyue people". The descendants of King Goujian of Yue became the rulers of Minyue.

&bull; **Late Warring States period** : The Minyue leader **Wuzhu** declared himself King of Minyue.

&bull; **222 BC (25th year of Qin King Zheng)** : After destroying Chu, the Qin army advanced into Minyue and established **Minzhong Commandery** in the region, deposing Wuzhu's kingship and demoting him to "Lord" (Junzhang).

&bull; **206 BC** : The Qin Dynasty fell, and the Chu-Han Contention broke out. Wuzhu went north to the Central Plains and assisted Liu Bang in defeating Xiang Yu.

&bull; **202 BC (5th year of Emperor Gaozu of Han)** : Liu Bang re-enfeoffed Wuzhu as **King of Minyue**, with the capital at **Dongye** (present-day Pingshan, Huada Street, Gulou District, Fuzhou). The Minyue Kingdom was formally established.

---

### 滅亡（Fall）

&bull; **無諸死後**，其後代多次發動對周邊小國的戰爭，最終導致閩越國走向滅亡。

&bull; **公元前135年（漢武帝建元年間）** ：閩越王**郢**先發兵攻打浙江南部的東甌國，後又進攻南越國。南越向漢朝求救，漢朝派出兩路大軍準備合擊閩越。在大軍壓境下，郢的弟弟余善趁機殺死郢，向漢軍投降。漢朝改封無諸的孫子**丑**為**越繇王**，又封余善為東越王，從此閩中出現「一國兩王」的局面。

&bull; **公元前112年（元鼎五年）** ：南越國發生內亂，漢朝出兵平亂。余善起初申請派遣閩越水軍援助，但中途以風浪為由滯留不進。漢朝認為他反覆無常，準備討伐。余善聞訊後先發制人，起兵攻入漢境，自稱「東越武帝」。

&bull; **公元前110年（元封元年）** ：漢武帝調遣四路大軍進攻閩越。在漢軍合圍下，閩越內部發生政變，余善被族人殺死。漢武帝以「閩越悍，數反覆」為由，下令將閩越王族和民眾**遷往江淮地區**。閩越國92年的統治至此結束。王城被漢軍焚毀。

---

&bull; **After Wuzhu's death** , his descendants launched multiple wars against neighboring small states, ultimately leading to the downfall of the Minyue Kingdom.

&bull; **135 BC (Jianyuan era of Emperor Wu of Han)** : King **Ying** of Minyue first attacked Dong'ou in southern Zhejiang, then invaded Nanyue. Nanyue sought help from the Han dynasty, which dispatched two armies to attack Minyue. Under military pressure, Ying's younger brother Yushan killed Ying and surrendered to the Han forces. The Han court enfeoffed Wuzhu's grandson Chou as King of Yueyao and Yushan as King of Dongyue, creating a situation of "one kingdom, two kings" in the Min region.

&bull; **112 BC (5th year of Yuanding era)** : Civil unrest broke out in Nanyue, and the Han army was dispatched to suppress it. Yushan initially offered to send Minyue naval forces but halted midway, citing storms. The Han court deemed him unreliable and prepared to attack. Yushan struck first, invading Han territory and proclaiming himself "Emperor of Dongyue".

&bull; **110 BC (1st year of Yuanfeng era)** : Emperor Wu of Han dispatched four armies to attack Minyue. Under the Han siege, a coup occurred within Minyue, and Yushan was killed by his own people. Emperor Wu, citing that "the Minyue are fierce and have repeatedly rebelled," ordered the relocation of the Minyue royal family and populace to the **Jianghuai region**. The 92-year rule of the Minyue Kingdom came to an end. The royal city was burned down by Han troops.

---

## 三、疆域 / Territory

閩越國的疆域以今福建省為核心。鼎盛時期，其勢力範圍包括：

&bull; **主體**：今福建省全境（包括馬祖群島和金門縣等地）
 
&bull; **北部**：浙江東南部，包括靈江、甌江、飛雲江、鰲江諸流域

&bull; **西部**：江西東部鉛山、余干一帶

&bull; **南部**：廣東東部潮州、梅州地區

**都城**：前期定都**東冶**（今福州）；後期東越王余善在閩北建立王城（今武夷山市興田鎮城村，即**城村漢城遺址**）。城村漢城遺址是江南地區迄今發現的最大西漢城址，面積48萬平方米。

公元前192年（漢惠帝三年），漢朝析置閩越北部為**東甌國**。

---

The territory of the Minyue Kingdom was centered on present-day Fujian Province. At its peak, its sphere of influence included:


&bull; **Core area** : The entire territory of present-day Fujian Province (including the Matsu Islands and Kinmen)

&bull; **North** : Southeastern Zhejiang, including the Lingjiang, Oujiang, Feiyunjiang, and Aojiang river basins

&bull; **West** : Yanshan and Yugan areas in eastern Jiangxi

&bull; **South** : Chaozhou and Meizhou areas in eastern Guangdong

**Capital** : Initially at **Dongye** (present-day Fuzhou); later, King Yushan of Dongyue established a royal city in northern Fujian (present-day Chengcun, Xingtian Town, Wuyishan City — the **Chengcun Han Castle Site**). The Chengcun Han Castle Site is the largest Western Han city site discovered south of the Yangtze River, covering 480,000 square meters.

In 192 BC (3rd year of Emperor Hui of Han), the Han court separated the northern part of Minyue to establish the **Dong'ou Kingdom**.

---

## 四、經濟與貿易 / Economy and Trade

### 農業 / Agriculture

閩越國以**稻作農業**為主，兼以畜牧、採集和漁獵。考古發掘出土了大量鐵製農具，包括犁、鋤、鍤、鐮、五齒耙等，表明當時農業生產力大幅提高，精耕細作的農業技術已被閩越人掌握。閩越人還採用「火耕水耨」的生產技術。

The Minyue Kingdom's agriculture was **rice-based**, supplemented by animal husbandry, gathering, and fishing/hunting. Archaeological excavations have unearthed a large number of iron agricultural tools, including plows, hoes, spades, sickles, and five-tooth rakes, indicating that agricultural productivity had greatly improved and intensive farming techniques had been mastered. The Minyue people also employed "slash-and-burn and water-weeding" farming techniques.

---

### 手工業 / Handicraft Industry

閩越國的手工業相當發達，主要包括：


&bull; **冶鐵業**：閩越立國後大興冶煉業，推廣鐵器。鐵製農具、工具和兵器種類齊全，使用相當廣泛。

&bull; 製陶業：閩越創造了輝煌的印紋硬陶文化，工藝獨特。

&bull; **紡織業**：紡織技術是閩越國三大經濟之一。

&bull; **造船業**：閩越人「以船為車」，擅長造船，水運業發達。

&bull; **建築業**：城村漢城遺址出土的長達2米的空心花紋磚等建築構件，反映了高超的建築技術。

---

The handicraft industry of the Minyue Kingdom was quite developed, mainly including:


&bull; **Iron smelting** : After the establishment of the kingdom, iron smelting flourished and iron implements were widely promoted. Iron agricultural tools, tools, and weapons were produced in complete varieties and widely used.

&bull; **Pottery** : The Minyue created a brilliant stamped hard pottery culture with unique craftsmanship.

&bull; **Textiles** : Textile technology was one of the three major economic pillars of the Minyue Kingdom.

&bull; **Shipbuilding** : The Minyue people "used boats as carriages" and excelled in shipbuilding, with developed water transportation.

&bull; **Construction** : Architectural components such as 2-meter-long hollow patterned bricks unearthed at the Chengcun Han Castle site reflect advanced construction techniques.

---

### 貿易 / Trade

閩越國的海外貿易相當活躍：


&bull; **東冶港**（今福州）是閩越國對外海上交通和航運的重要港口。

&bull; 從西漢時期起，東冶與中南半島已開闢定期航線，成為東南海運的樞紐和對外貿易的主要港口。

&bull; 來自中南半島的海外商品皆在東冶集散轉運。考古發現的海外奇珍證明了當時閩越國曾從東南亞等地通過海外貿易輸入相當數量的商品。

&bull; 東冶港是海上絲綢之路的早期起點之一。

---

The Minyue Kingdom's overseas trade was quite active:


&bull; **Dongye Port** (present-day Fuzhou) was an important port for Minyue's overseas maritime transportation and shipping.

&bull; From the Western Han period onward, Dongye had established regular shipping routes with the Indochina Peninsula, becoming a hub of southeastern maritime transport and a major foreign trade port.

&bull; Overseas goods from the Indochina Peninsula were collected and transshipped at Dongye. Archaeological discoveries of exotic treasures prove that the Minyue Kingdom imported considerable quantities of goods from Southeast Asia and other regions through overseas trade.

&bull; Dongye Port was one of the early starting points of the Maritime Silk Road.

---

## 五、閩越出名的人物 / Famous Figures of Minyue

### 1\. 無諸（Wuzhu）

越王勾踐的後裔，姓騶氏。戰國晚期自立為閩越王。秦朝統一後被廢去王號，降為君長。秦末楚漢戰爭中，無諸幫助劉邦擊敗項羽。公元前202年，劉邦封無諸為閩越王，建都東冶。無諸頗有雄才大略，積極吸收中原的生產技術和文化，促進閩越文化與中原文化的融合，使閩越國的經濟社會得到較大的發展。

Wuzhu : A descendant of King Goujian of Yue, with the surname Zou. He declared himself King of Minyue in the late Warring States period. After the Qin unification, his kingship was revoked and he was demoted to Lord. During the Chu-Han Contention at the end of Qin, Wuzhu assisted Liu Bang in defeating Xiang Yu. In 202 BC, Liu Bang enfeoffed Wuzhu as King of Minyue, with the capital at Dongye. Wuzhu was a man of great talent and strategy, actively absorbing Central Plains production technology and culture, promoting the integration of Minyue and Central Plains cultures, and significantly developing Minyue's economy and society.

---

### 2\. 郢（Ying）

無諸的後裔，閩越王位的繼承人。在位期間多次發動對周邊小國的戰爭，先攻東甌，後攻南越，最終導致漢朝出兵干預。公元前135年，被其弟余善殺死。

Ying : A descendant of Wuzhu and heir to the Minyue throne. During his reign, he launched multiple wars against neighboring small states, first attacking Dong'ou and then Nanyue, ultimately leading to Han military intervention. In 135 BC, he was killed by his younger brother Yushan.

---

### 3\. 余善（Yushan）

郢的弟弟，姓騶氏。公元前135年殺兄郢後向漢軍投降。漢朝封他為東越王，從此閩中出現「一國兩王」的局面。余善統治閩越約20年，與漢廷相安無事。後因叛漢自稱「東越武帝」，引發漢武帝派四路大軍征討。公元前110年，余善被族人殺死，閩越國亡。相傳余善死後葬在侯官縣（今閩侯縣）寧棋里。

Yushan : Ying's younger brother, with the surname Zou. In 135 BC, he killed his brother Ying and surrendered to the Han forces. The Han court enfeoffed him as King of Dongyue, creating a situation of "one kingdom, two kings" in the Min region. Yushan ruled Minyue for about 20 years, maintaining peace with the Han court. Later, he rebelled against Han and proclaimed himself "Emperor of Dongyue," prompting Emperor Wu of Han to dispatch four armies against him. In 110 BC, Yushan was killed by his own people, and the Minyue Kingdom fell. According to legend, Yushan was buried in Ningqili, Houguan County (present-day Minhou County).

---

### 4\. 丑（Chou）

無諸的孫子。公元前135年余善殺郢後，漢朝封丑為越繇王（又稱繇王），奉祀閩越先王。

**Chou** : Wuzhu's grandson. After Yushan killed Ying in 135 BC, the Han court enfeoffed Chou as King of Yueyao (also known as Yao King) to continue the sacrifices to the former Minyue kings.

---

## 六、閩越語與閩南語的關係 / Relationship between Minyue Language and Minnan Dialect

### 閩越語概述 / Overview of Minyue Language

閩越語是古代閩越人所使用的語言，屬於古越語的一支。戰國後期，越國被楚國滅亡後，越國王族退到福建境內建立閩越國，與當地原住民融合形成了**閩越語**。閩越語是今日漢語族閩語支各語言的**底層組成部分**，其聲韻特色仍保留於今日閩語諸語言中。

The Minyue language was the language spoken by the ancient Minyue people, belonging to the branch of Old Yue languages. After the Yue State was destroyed by Chu in the late Warring States period, the Yue royal family retreated to Fujian and established the Minyue Kingdom, merging with the local indigenous people to form the **Minyue language**. The Minyue language is a **substrate component** of the various languages of the Min branch of the Sinitic language family, and its phonological characteristics are still preserved in today's Min languages.

---

### 閩越語對閩南語的影響 / Influence of Minyue Language on Minnan

#### 1\. 語言融合的歷史過程 / Historical Process of Language Integration

公元前110年，漢武帝滅閩越後，漢朝駐軍多為江東的**吳人**和江西的**楚人**，他們將自己的母語——**古吳語**和**古楚語**（古湘語）帶入了福建地區，融入了閩越語元素，最後形成了**原始閩語**。閩南語為漢藏語系的一支，是古代閩越人所使用的閩越語與漢人入閩帶來漢語的結合。

#### 1\. Historical Process of Language Integration

---

After Emperor Wu of Han destroyed Minyue in 110 BC, the Han garrison troops were mostly **Wu people** from Jiangdong and **Chu people** from Jiangxi. They brought their native languages — **Old Wu** and **Old Chu** (Old Xiang) — into the Fujian region, which integrated with Minyue language elements and ultimately formed **Proto-Min**. Minnan is a branch of the Sino-Tibetan language family, formed by the combination of the Minyue language spoken by the ancient Minyue people and the Chinese language brought by Han Chinese migrants entering Fujian.

#### 2\. 底層影響 / Substrate Influence

閩南語是以「多源聚合」的語言發展模式形成的。學者們在考察閩南語字源時，應多方面考慮**古閩越語在閩南語底層中的反映**。閩南語中保留了非漢語底層特徵的詞語。例如，泉州方言中一些動植物名詞的構詞成分和構詞方式保留著古閩越語的特徵。這些非漢語底層詞彙與壯侗語族有著明顯且密切的聯繫。

#### 2\. Substrate Influence

---

Minnan was formed through a "multi-source convergence" language development model. When examining the etymologies of Minnan words, scholars should consider in multiple ways **the reflection of the ancient Minyue language in the Minnan substrate**. Minnan retains words with non-Chinese substrate features. For example, some animal and plant noun formation components and patterns in the Quanzhou dialect preserve characteristics of the ancient Minyue language. These non-Chinese substrate vocabulary items have obvious and close connections with the Kam-Tai language family.

#### 3\. 閩南語與古閩越語的聯繫 / Connection between Minnan and Ancient Minyue

福建是閩越族居住的地方，百姓說閩越語。泉州方言實際上是**漢語與閩越語交融的結果**。早期福建地區的「閩方言」是一個統一體。後來，由於福建省多山的地形，再經過漫長時間的變化與地域分化，才從閩方言中明顯地分化出福州話、閩南話等次方言。上古閩越語融入吳楚語成分後，在宋代前分化為建州、福州、泉州三地方言。

現代閩南話中，**閩**、**蠻**仍同音 (閩南發音: 閩 bân, 蠻 bân)，這是古閩越語底層在現代語言中的一個遺存。中原南遷漢人與閩越土著交融，最終形成了今天的閩南人和閩南文化。


#### 3\. Connection between Minnan and Ancient Minyue

Fujian was the land of the Minyue people, who spoke the Minyue language. The Quanzhou dialect is actually **the result of the fusion of Chinese and Minyue**. In the early period, the "Min dialects" of Fujian formed a unified whole. Later, due to Fujian's mountainous terrain and prolonged historical changes and regional differentiation, the Min dialects clearly differentiated into sub-dialects such as Fuzhou dialect and Minnan dialect. After the ancient Minyue language integrated Wu-Chu elements, it differentiated before the Song dynasty into the three dialects of Jianzhou, Fuzhou, and Quanzhou.

In modern Minnan, the words **"Min"  and "Man" (barbarian) are still homophonous** (Minnan pronunciations: 閩 bân, 蠻 bân) — a relic of the ancient Minyue substrate in modern language. The Han Chinese who migrated south from the Central Plains intermingled with the Minyue indigenous people, ultimately forming today's Minnan people and Minnan culture.
