**三、詩意詳細解析**

**（一）寫作背景**

〈旅夜書懷〉是杜甫於唐代宗永泰元年（765年）離開成都草堂，乘舟東下時所作 [1]。此前，杜甫在成都依靠好友嚴武的接濟，生活相對安定。嚴武去世後，杜甫失去依靠，被迫攜家帶眷離開成都，開始了漂泊西南的人生歷程。這首詩即寫於他沿長江東下、途經渝州（今重慶）至忠州（今重慶忠縣）的旅途之中，時年約五十三歲 [2]。

**（二）字詞釋義**

- **旅夜**：旅途中的夜晚。
- **書懷**：抒寫胸懷、感慨。
- **細草**：纖細的小草。
- **危檣**：高高的桅杆。危，高。
- **獨夜舟**：孤獨的夜泊之舟。
- **星垂**：星星低垂，彷彿要墜落地面。
- **平野闊**：平曠的原野顯得格外遼闊。
- **月湧**：月光在江面上湧動。
- **大江**：長江。
- **名豈文章著**：名聲豈能靠文章來顯著。
- **官應老病休**：官職應當因年老多病而退休。
- **飄飄**：漂泊無依的樣子。
- **沙鷗**：一種水鳥，常棲息於沙洲。

**（三）逐句解析**

- **首聯「細草微風岸，危檣獨夜舟」**：詩人從細微處著筆，描寫夜泊之景。微風吹拂著岸邊的細草，高高的桅杆下，孤舟獨泊於夜色之中。這既是寫實，也暗喻詩人漂泊無依、孤獨落寞的處境。細草與危檣，一低一高，一靜一動，形成鮮明對比。

- **頷聯「星垂平野闊，月湧大江流」**：這是千古傳誦的名句。星星低垂，原野顯得格外遼闊；月光湧動，大江奔流不息。詩人以雄渾的筆墨，勾勒出天地之間的壯闊景象。然而，在這宏大的自然面前，詩人卻感到自身的渺小與孤獨，為下文的感慨埋下伏筆。

- **頸聯「名豈文章著，官應老病休」**：詩人轉入直抒胸臆。他感嘆：我的名聲難道真的是靠文章才顯著的嗎？我的官職，應該也是因為年老多病才退休的吧？這兩句表面上是謙虛，實際上是對自己一生懷才不遇的深沉感慨。杜甫有經世濟民的抱負，卻因政治黑暗而不得施展，如今只能以文章聞名，以老病休官，其中暗含無限辛酸。

- **尾聯「飄飄何所似，天地一沙鷗」**：詩人將自己漂泊無依的形象，比作天地間一隻孤獨的沙鷗。沙鷗在廣闊天地中翱翔，卻無所歸依，正如詩人自己，雖有才華與抱負，卻只能四處漂泊，無處安身。這一比喻生動而深刻，將全詩的孤獨與悲涼推向高潮。

**（四）整體意境與主題**

這首詩以旅夜泊舟為背景，通過對自然景物的描寫與個人感慨的抒發，展現了杜甫晚年漂泊無依的孤獨心境與壯志難酬的深沉憂憤。全詩情景交融，結構謹嚴，被譽為杜甫五言律詩的典範之作。

- **景與情的交織**：前兩聯寫景，後兩聯抒情，但景中有情，情中有景。壯闊的自然景象與詩人的孤獨渺小形成強烈對比。
- **人生的感慨**：頸聯的「名豈文章著，官應老病休」是杜甫對自己一生的總結與感慨，暗含懷才不遇的悲涼。
- **象徵的運用**：尾聯的「天地一沙鷗」是全詩的靈魂，以沙鷗的孤獨與漂泊，象徵詩人自身的處境，意境深遠，令人回味。

**（五）藝術特色**

- **對仗工整**：全詩對仗極為工整，尤以「星垂平野闊，月湧大江流」為最，是唐詩中寫景的千古名句。
- **動靜結合**：細草、危檣是靜景，微風、星垂、月湧、江流是動景，動靜相宜，畫面豐富。
- **情景交融**：壯闊的自然景象與詩人孤獨的心境融為一體，達到「一切景語皆情語」的境界。
- **比喻深刻**：以沙鷗自喻，形象生動，寓意深遠。

**III. Detailed Analysis**

**(1) Historical Background**

"Lü Ye Shu Huai" was written by Du Fu in 765 AD (the first year of the Yongtai era), when he left his thatched cottage in Chengdu and sailed eastward [1]. Previously, Du Fu had relied on his friend Yan Wu for support in Chengdu, living a relatively stable life. After Yan Wu's death, Du Fu lost his patron and was forced to leave Chengdu with his family, beginning his wandering life in the southwest. This poem was written during his journey down the Yangtze River, passing through Yuzhou (modern Chongqing) to Zhongzhou (modern Zhongxian, Chongqing), at about the age of fifty-three [2].

**(2) Vocabulary Explanations**

- **旅夜 (Lǚ Yè)**: A night on a journey.
- **書懷 (Shū Huái)**: Writing out one's feelings or thoughts.
- **細草 (Xì Cǎo)**: Fine, slender grass.
- **危檣 (Wēi Qiáng)**: A tall mast. 危 (wēi) means "high."
- **獨夜舟 (Dú Yè Zhōu)**: A lonely boat moored at night.
- **星垂 (Xīng Chuí)**: The stars hang low, as if about to fall.
- **平野闊 (Píng Yě Kuò)**: The vast, open plains.
- **月湧 (Yuè Yǒng)**: The moonlight surging on the river.
- **大江 (Dà Jiāng)**: The Yangtze River.
- **名豈文章著 (Míng Qǐ Wén Zhāng Zhù)**: How can fame be achieved through writing alone?
- **官應老病休 (Guān Yīng Lǎo Bìng Xiū)**: One should retire from office due to old age and illness.
- **飄飄 (Piāo Piāo)**: Drifting, wandering without a fixed place.
- **沙鷗 (Shā Ōu)**: A sand gull, a water bird often found on sandbars.

**(3) Line-by-Line Interpretation**

- **First Couplet: "Fine grass, a light breeze on the bank; / A tall mast, a lonely boat at night."**  
  The poet begins with a delicate scene of a night mooring. The fine grass and light breeze create a serene image, while the tall mast and lonely boat convey a sense of isolation. This is both a realistic description and a metaphor for the poet's own solitary, wandering state.

- **Second Couplet: "The stars hang low, the plains stretch wide; / The moon surges, the great river flows."**  
  This is the most famous couplet of the poem. The poet paints a magnificent scene of the vast natural world: the stars seem to hang low over the wide plains, and the moonlight surges on the flowing Yangtze. Yet, amidst this grandeur, the poet feels the insignificance of his own existence, setting the stage for the following lines of personal reflection.

- **Third Couplet: "Is fame to be achieved through writing? / Office should be retired from due to age and illness."**  
  The poet turns to direct personal expression. These lines seem modest, but they are filled with deep regret for a life of unfulfilled ambitions. Du Fu had always hoped to serve the state, but political obstacles prevented him. Now, he is known only for his poetry and has retired due to old age and illness, a fate he accepts with sorrow.

- **Fourth Couplet: "What am I like, adrift and wandering? / A single gull between heaven and earth."**  
  The poet compares himself to a solitary gull drifting between heaven and earth. The gull is free but homeless, just as Du Fu, despite his talent and aspirations, is left wandering with no place to call home. This metaphor is both vivid and profound, bringing the poem's themes of loneliness and desolation to a powerful conclusion.

**(4) Overall Mood and Themes**

This poem, set against the backdrop of a night journey, blends natural description with personal reflection to express Du Fu's profound loneliness and his sorrow at unrealized ambitions. It is a masterpiece of Du Fu's regulated verse.

- **Interweaving of Scene and Emotion**: The first two couplets describe the scenery, while the last two express the poet's feelings. Yet, the scene is infused with emotion, and the emotions are conveyed through the scene.
- **Reflection on Life**: The third couplet is a poignant summary of Du Fu's life, expressing his regret that his name is not known for his political achievements but for his poetry.
- **Powerful Symbolism**: The final image of the "solitary gull" is the soul of the poem. It symbolizes the poet's loneliness and wandering, creating a lasting, resonant image.

**(5) Artistic Features**

- **Balanced Parallelism**: The poem features highly balanced parallel lines, especially the famous second couplet, which is considered a masterpiece of landscape description in Tang poetry.
- **Dynamic and Static Contrast**: The poem combines static elements (fine grass, tall mast) with dynamic ones (breeze, stars, moon, river), creating a rich and varied scene.
- **Fusion of Scene and Emotion**: The vast natural landscape is seamlessly integrated with the poet's lonely heart.
- **Profound Metaphor**: The comparison of the poet to a solitary gull is vivid, concise, and deeply meaningful.
