# 五代王朝后梁为何名称“后梁” —— 中英对照完整详解

## 一、总述 / Overview

#### 中文：
后梁（907年—923年）是五代十国时期五代的第一个朝代，由朱温（后梁太祖）所建，定都开封（今河南开封），后迁都洛阳。其国号“梁”及史称“后梁”，背后包含两层含义：一是国号“梁”的由来，二是史家为何在其前加一“后”字。

#### English:
The Later Liang (907–923) was the first of the Five Dynasties during the Five Dynasties and Ten Kingdoms period. It was founded by Zhu Wen (Emperor Taizu of Later Liang), with its capital at Kaifeng (modern-day Kaifeng, Henan) and later moved to Luoyang. The name “Liang” and the historiographical designation “Later Liang” contain two layers of meaning: first, the origin of the dynastic name “Liang”; second, why historians added the prefix “Later” (後/后).

---

## 二、国号“梁”的来源 / The Origin of the Dynastic Name “Liang”

### 2.1 源自封爵“梁王” / From the Title “Prince of Liang”

#### 中文：
朱温原为唐末宣武军节度使，因镇压黄巢起义有功，逐渐成为唐朝最大的藩镇势力。唐昭宗于天复元年（901年）进封朱温为**梁王**。907年，朱温逼迫唐哀帝禅位，**以其封爵“梁王”改国号为梁**。以王爵定国号，是符合中古以来传统的做法。

#### English:
Zhu Wen was originally the military governor (Jiedushi) of Xuanwu Circuit in the late Tang dynasty. Due to his contributions in suppressing the Huang Chao Rebellion, he gradually became the most powerful warlord of the Tang dynasty. In 901, Emperor Zhaozong of Tang conferred upon Zhu Wen the title **Prince of Liang**. In 907, Zhu Wen forced Emperor Ai of Tang to abdicate and **adopted “Liang” as his dynastic name based on his princely title “Prince of Liang”**. Adopting a dynastic name from a princely title was a tradition consistent with medieval Chinese practice.

### 2.2 源自地名“大梁” / From the Place Name “Daliang”

#### 中文：
“梁”字更深层的地理来源，是朱温的根据地——**汴州（今河南开封）** 。汴州在战国时期是魏国的都城**大梁**所在地。战国时魏惠王迁都大梁后，魏国也被称为梁国（《孟子》中“梁惠王”即源于此）。汉初彭越的梁国、西汉梁孝王刘武的封国，皆为此地。因此，“梁”逐渐成为**故魏之地（汴州一带）的代称**。朱温受封梁王并以“梁”为国号，皆因其势力根基在此地。此外，朱温祖居**砀山**（今安徽砀山），战国时亦属梁地。

#### English:
The deeper geographical origin of the name “Liang” lies in Zhu Wen’s power base — **Bianzhou (modern-day Kaifeng, Henan)** . During the Warring States period, Bianzhou was the location of Daliang, the capital of the State of Wei. After King Hui of Wei moved his capital to Daliang, the State of Wei was also referred to as the State of Liang (hence “King Hui of Liang” in the Mencius). The early Han dynasty fief of Peng Yue and the principality of Liu Wu (Prince Xiao of Liang) were both located in this same region. Thus, “Liang” gradually became **a geographical designation for the former Wei territory (the Bianzhou area)** . Zhu Wen’s title “Prince of Liang” and his adoption of “Liang” as his dynastic name both stem from his power base being rooted in this region. Additionally, Zhu Wen’s ancestral home was Dangshan (modern-day Dangshan, Anhui), which in the Warring States period also belonged to the Liang region.

## 三、史称“后梁”的原因 / The Reason for the Historiographical Name “Later Liang”

### 3.1 为与南朝的萧梁相区分 / To Distinguish from the Southern Dynasties’ Xiao Liang

#### 中文：
朱温建立的王朝虽然自称为“梁”或“大梁”，**但中国历史上在此之前已经有一个以“梁”为国号的著名朝代——南北朝时期的南梁（502年—557年）** ，由萧衍建立，史称萧梁。

#### English:
Although the dynasty founded by Zhu Wen called itself “Liang” or “Great Liang”, **there had already been a famous dynasty in Chinese history with the dynastic name “Liang” — the Southern Liang (502–557) of the Northern and Southern Dynasties period**, founded by Xiao Yan, known historiographically as the Xiao Liang.

### 3.2 史家加“后”字以示区别 / Historians Added “Later” to Differentiate

#### 中文：
正如元代史学家**胡三省**在《资治通鉴·后梁纪一》注中所说： **“《通鉴》以前纪已有萧梁，故此称曰后梁。”** 意思是：《资治通鉴》前面的纪事中已经有萧梁（南梁），所以这里称其为后梁。史家为了**避免前后混淆**，故在“梁”字前加一“后”字，称为“后梁”。

#### English:
As the Yuan dynasty historian **Hu Sanxing** stated in his annotation to the _Comprehensive Mirror for Aid in Government (Zizhi Tongjian), “Since the previous records of the Comprehensive Mirror already contain the Xiao Liang, it is therefore referred to as the Later Liang.”_ Historians added the prefix “Later” (後/后) before “Liang” **to avoid confusion** between the two dynasties that shared the same name.

## 四、其他称谓 / Other Designations

#### 中文：
后梁因皇帝姓朱，为与南梁相区别，又被称为 **“朱梁”** 。后唐及不承认后梁的各地藩镇则称其为 **“伪梁”** 。

#### English:
Because the emperors of the Later Liang were surnamed Zhu, it was also referred to as **“Zhu Liang”** to distinguish it from the Southern Liang. The Later Tang and various regional warlords who did not recognize the Later Liang called it **“Pretender Liang” (Wei Liang)** .
