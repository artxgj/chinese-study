## 2. Bilingual Response

### 中文部分 (Chinese Section)

**摘要**  
這四個詞構成了一條從「客觀呈現」到「深度剖析」的進階序列。**「描述」是畫圖——客觀呈現外貌或過程；「說明」是指南——把事理、方法講清楚讓對方明白；「解釋」是破案——揭示內在原因、機制或邏輯；「詳解」是拆解——對前者的全方位、細緻化擴展，強調詳細和周全。** 掌握「畫圖 vs 講清 vs 破案 vs 拆解」這個意象，就能精準選詞。

**詳細說明**

**1. 「描述」——客觀呈現，重在「怎麼樣的」**  
- **核心用法**：用語言或文字把一個事物、場景、過程或狀態的**外在特徵、形貌、順序**如實說出來。重點在於「客觀再現」，不涉及深層原因或判斷。  
- **語感**：中性、寫實、記錄性強。像是給人「畫一幅畫」或「放一段影片」。  
- **側重點**：**「事物的表象或過程的軌跡」**。  
- **常見搭配**：描述外貌、描述經過、描述景色、描述心情。  
- **例句**：  
  - 請**描述**一下事故發生的整個經過。（把過程按時間順序說出來）  
  - 這篇文章生動地**描述**了江南水鄉的風光。（用文字再現景色）

**2. 「說明」——使人明白，重在「為什麼／怎麼做」**  
- **核心用法**：把一個道理、功能、規則或操作步驟**解釋清楚**，讓對方理解或知道該如何行動。重點在於「傳達有效信息以消除疑惑」。常帶有指導性或告知性。  
- **語感**：清晰、指導性強、務實。像是「看說明書」或「聽人講解規則」。  
- **側重點**：**「事理或操作的清晰傳達」**。  
- **常見搭配**：說明原因、說明用法、說明規則、說明情況。  
- **例句**：  
  - 請**說明**這台機器的操作步驟。（指導對方怎麼做）  
  - 他向我**說明**了遲到的理由。（把原因講清楚，讓我知道）

**3. 「解釋」——剖析內因，重在「為什麼會這樣」**  
- **核心用法**：針對一個現象、問題或疑難，**揭示其背後的原因、邏輯、機制或含義**。重點在於「穿透表象，挖出深層理由」。通常帶有「解惑」的意味，即對方原本不懂或誤解。  
- **語感**：學術性、邏輯性強，帶有「破解疑難」的張力。像是「偵探破案」或「老師講原理」。  
- **側重點**：**「內在成因或含義的闡明」**。  
- **常見搭配**：解釋現象、解釋原因、解釋詞義、解釋術語。  
- **例句**：  
  - 物理老師向我們**解釋**了彩虹形成的原理。（揭開背後的物理機制）  
  - 請**解釋**一下你為什麼做出這個決定。（我要知道你的深層動機）

**4. 「詳解」——全方位拆解，重在「細緻而全面」**  
- **核心用法**：對某個問題、題目或知識點進行**詳細、透徹、一步步的剖析**。可以看作是「解釋」的加強版，強調的是「詳盡無遺」和「逐層展開」，通常用於教學、解題或技術文檔中。  
- **語感**：教學感強、條理分明、耐心十足。像是「老師在黑板上把每一步都寫出來」。  
- **側重點**：**「細緻入微的全面闡述」**。  
- **常見搭配**：詳解例題、詳解歷史事件、詳解技術方案、圖文詳解。  
- **例句**：  
  - 這本書**詳解**了歷年高考數學真題。（不只是給答案，還把每一步思路寫出來）  
  - 他在部落格上**詳解**了如何搭建個人網站。（從零開始，一步一步說）

**5. 對比總表（一目了然）**

| 詞語     | 核心意象         | 焦點層面             | 深淺程度                           | 典型搭配           | 回答的隱含問題               |
| :------- | :--------------- | :------------------- | :--------------------------------- | :----------------- | :--------------------------- |
| **描述** | 畫圖／錄像       | 外在表象、過程軌跡   | 最淺，停留在「是什麼」             | 描述景色、描述經過 | 「它長什麼樣／發生了什麼？」 |
| **說明** | 說明書           | 事理、功能、操作方法 | 中等，重在「如何理解／操作」       | 說明用法、說明規則 | 「這是怎麼回事／該怎麼做？」 |
| **解釋** | 破案／解謎       | 內在原因、機制、邏輯 | 較深，穿透到「為什麼」             | 解釋原理、解釋原因 | 「它為什麼會這樣？」         |
| **詳解** | 拆解／手把手教學 | 全方位細節、逐層剖析 | 最深，覆蓋「是什麼→怎麼做→為什麼」 | 詳解例題、詳解步驟 | 「能從頭到尾細說一遍嗎？」   |

**6. 實戰選詞心法**  
- 如果你只想陳述一個事實或畫面 → 用 **「描述」**，別想太多。  
- 如果你想讓對方按照步驟做某件事 → 用 **「說明」**，重在可操作性。  
- 如果對方滿臉問號，你要挖出背後原因 → 用 **「解釋」**，重在解惑。  
- 如果對方說「說清楚一點」或你要寫教學文章 → 用 **「詳解」**，告訴對方「我會從頭細講」。  
- **檢驗法**：把「詳細地」放在前面試試看——「詳細地描述」和「詳細地解釋」都成立，但前者只是把外貌講細，後者是把根源挖深；而「詳細地詳解」會重複累贅（因為「詳」本身已有詳細之意）。

### English Section

**Abstract**  
These four verbs form a progressive sequence from "objective presentation" to "deep analysis." **「描述」(miáoshù) is drawing a picture – objectively presenting appearance or process; 「說明」(shuōmíng) is providing a manual – clearly explaining principles or methods so others understand; 「解釋」(jiěshì) is solving a mystery – revealing underlying causes, mechanisms, or logic; 「詳解」(xiángjiě) is taking things apart – an exhaustive, detailed expansion of the former, emphasizing thoroughness and comprehensiveness.** Master the mental imagery of "drawing vs. clarifying vs. investigating vs. dissecting," and you'll choose precisely.

**Detailed Explanation**

**1. 「描述」(miáoshù) – Objective Representation, Focus on "What It Looks Like"**  
- **Core Usage**: Using language or writing to faithfully present the **external features, appearance, sequence, or state** of an object, scene, process, or condition. The focus is on "objective reproduction," without delving into underlying reasons or judgments.  
- **Tone**: Neutral, documentary, record‑oriented. Like "painting a picture" or "playing a video" for someone.  
- **Emphasis**: **"The surface appearance or the trajectory of a process."**  
- **Common Collocations**: 描述外貌 (describe appearance), 描述经过 (describe the course of events), 描述景色 (describe scenery), 描述心情 (describe one's mood).  
- **Examples**:  
  - 請**描述**一下事故發生的整個經過。(Please **describe** the entire course of the accident – narrating the sequence chronologically.)  
  - 這篇文章生動地**描述**了江南水鄉的風光。(This article vividly **depicts** the scenery of the Jiangnan water towns – reproducing the view in words.)

**2. 「說明」(shuōmíng) – Making Things Clear, Focus on "Why/How"**  
- **Core Usage**: **Clarifying** a principle, function, rule, or operational procedure so that others understand or know how to act. The focus is on "conveying effective information to eliminate doubt." Often carries instructional or informational intent.  
- **Tone**: Clear, instructive, practical. Like "reading a manual" or "listening to someone explain the rules."  
- **Emphasis**: **"Clear communication of logic or operations."**  
- **Common Collocations**: 說明原因 (explain the reason), 說明用法 (explain the usage), 說明規則 (explain the rules), 說明情況 (explain the situation).  
- **Examples**:  
  - 請**說明**這台機器的操作步驟。(Please **explain** the operating steps of this machine – instructing how to do it.)  
  - 他向我**說明**了遲到的理由。(He **explained** to me the reason for being late – making the cause clear so I understand.)

**3. 「解釋」(jiěshì) – Uncovering Internal Causes, Focus on "Why It Happened"**  
- **Core Usage**: Aiming at a phenomenon, problem, or difficulty, **revealing the underlying reasons, logic, mechanisms, or implications**. The focus is on "penetrating the surface to dig out the deep rationale." Usually carries a "dispelling confusion" tone – the other party originally didn't understand or had a misunderstanding.  
- **Tone**: Academic, logical, with the tension of "solving a puzzle." Like "a detective solving a case" or "a teacher explaining a principle."  
- **Emphasis**: **"Clarifying internal causes or meanings."**  
- **Common Collocations**: 解釋現象 (explain a phenomenon), 解釋原因 (explain the reason), 解釋詞義 (explain the meaning of a word), 解釋術語 (explain a term).  
- **Examples**:  
  - 物理老師向我們**解釋**了彩虹形成的原理。(The physics teacher **explained** the principle behind the formation of rainbows to us – uncovering the physical mechanism.)  
  - 請**解釋**一下你為什麼做出這個決定。(Please **explain** why you made this decision – I want to know your deeper motivation.)

**4. 「詳解」(xiángjiě) – Comprehensive Deconstruction, Focus on "Thorough and Detailed"**  
- **Core Usage**: Providing a **detailed, thorough, step‑by‑step analysis** of a problem, question, or knowledge point. It can be seen as the intensified version of 「解釋」, emphasizing "exhaustive completeness" and "layer‑by‑layer unfolding." Often used in teaching, problem‑solving, or technical documentation.  
- **Tone**: Strongly pedagogical, methodical, and patient. Like "a teacher writing out every step on the blackboard."  
- **Emphasis**: **"Meticulously comprehensive elaboration."**  
- **Common Collocations**: 詳解例題 (detailed explanation of example problems), 詳解歷史事件 (detailed analysis of historical events), 詳解技術方案 (detailed breakdown of a technical solution), 圖文詳解 (illustrated detailed explanation).  
- **Examples**:  
  - 這本書**詳解**了歷年高考數學真題。(This book **provides a detailed breakdown** of past years' college entrance exam math questions – not just giving answers, but laying out every step of the reasoning.)  
  - 他在部落格上**詳解**了如何搭建個人網站。(He gave a **detailed walkthrough** on his blog of how to build a personal website – starting from scratch, step by step.)

**5. Comparison Table (At a Glance)**

| Word     | Core Imagery                     | Focus Level                                    | Depth                                           | Typical Collocations | Implicit Question Answered                |
| :------- | :------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------- | :------------------- | :---------------------------------------- |
| **描述** | Drawing / Recording              | External appearance, process trajectory        | Shallowest – stays at "what"                    | 描述景色, 描述经过   | "What does it look like / What happened?" |
| **説明** | Manual / Guide                   | Principles, functions, operations              | Medium – focuses on "how to understand/operate" | 説明用法, 説明規則   | "What's this about / How do I do it?"     |
| **解釋** | Detective work / Mystery solving | Internal causes, mechanisms, logic             | Deeper – penetrates to "why"                    | 解釋原理, 解釋原因   | "Why did this happen?"                    |
| **詳解** | Dissection / Hand‑holding        | Comprehensive details, layer‑by‑layer analysis | Deepest – covers "what→how→why"                 | 詳解例題, 詳解步驟   | "Can you walk me through it from A to Z?" |

**6. Practical Decision‑Making Guide**  
- If you merely want to state a fact or present a scene → Use **「描述」** – don't overthink it.  
- If you want someone to follow steps to do something → Use **「説明」** – emphasis on operability.  
- If the other person is full of question marks and you need to reveal the hidden cause → Use **「解釋」** – focus on resolving confusion.  
- If they say "explain it more clearly" or you're writing a tutorial → Use **「詳解」** – conveying "I'll walk you through it thoroughly."  
- **Quick test**: Try putting 「詳細地」(in detail) in front. 「詳細地描述」and 「詳細地解釋」both work – but the former just makes the description more detailed, while the latter digs deeper into the root cause. 「詳細地詳解」is redundant (since 「詳」already implies detail).
