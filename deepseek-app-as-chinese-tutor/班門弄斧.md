### 班門弄斧（Bān Mén Nòng Fǔ）— 中英雙語詳解

---

#### 一、成語簡介 / Idiom Overview

拼音：bān mén nòng fǔ

字面意思：在魯班門前舞弄斧子

釋義：比喻在行家面前賣弄本領，不自量力。

英文對應：to show off one's slight accomplishments before an expert / to teach a fish how to swim

感情色彩：含貶義，也可用作自謙之詞

---

#### 二、人物介紹 — 魯班 / Who Is Lu Ban?

魯班（約公元前507年—約公元前444年），春秋時期魯國人，是中國歷史上最著名的能工巧匠。他姓公輸，名班，因是魯國人，故稱「魯班」。

Lu Ban (c. 507 BC – c. 444 BC) was a native of the State of Lu during the Spring and Autumn Period. He is the most celebrated master craftsman in Chinese history.His surname was Gongshu and his given name was Ban; because he came from the State of Lu, he is known as Lu Ban.

魯班技藝超群，發明了多種木工工具（如鋸、刨、墨斗等），被後世尊奉為木匠的祖師爺。關於他的傳說遍及華夏大地，他是工匠界的鼻祖。

Lu Ban was exceptionally skilled and invented many carpenter's tools (such as the saw, plane, and ink marker). He is revered by later generations as the patron saint of carpenters.Legends about him spread throughout China; he is considered the founding father of craftsmanship.

#### 三、成語出處 / Origin of the Idiom

「班門弄斧」最早出自唐代文學家柳宗元（773—819）的《王氏伯仲唱和詩序》：

「操斧於班、郢之門，斯強顏耳。」

意思是：拿著斧頭在魯班和郢都匠石（另一位能工巧匠）的門前耍弄，這實在是厚著臉皮（不自量力）啊。

The idiom first appeared in "Preface to the Poems of the Wang Brothers" by the Tang Dynasty literary giant Liu Zongyuan (773–819):

"To wield an axe before the gates of Ban and Ying — this is nothing but barefaced audacity."

後來，北宋歐陽修在《與梅聖俞書》中寫道：「昨在真定，有詩七八首，今錄去，班門弄斧，可笑可笑。」這是該成語第一次以完整四字形式出現。

Later, Ouyang Xiu of the Northern Song Dynasty wrote in a letter: "Yesterday in Zhending, I wrote seven or eight poems; now I send them to you — wielding an axe before Lu Ban's gate — how laughable!"This is the first time the idiom appeared in its complete four-character form.

明代詩人梅之煥在《題李白墓》中也有名句：

「采石江邊一堆土，李白之名高千古；來來往往一首詩，魯班門前弄大斧。」

The Ming Dynasty poet Mei Zhihuan also wrote a famous verse at Li Bai's tomb:

"By the Caishi River a mound of earth, / Li Bai's fame echoes through the ages; / Here and there, visitors scribble a poem or two — / Wielding a great axe before Lu Ban's gate."

#### 四、成語故事 / The Story Behind the Idiom

##### 中文版

春秋戰國時期，魯國有一位名叫魯班的能工巧匠。他聰明靈巧，擅長製作各種精巧的器具，被人們稱為「巧人」，民間歷來把他奉為木匠的始祖。

傳說魯班曾用木頭雕刻了一隻鳳凰，栩栩如生，竟然在空中飛翔了三天三夜不掉下來。魯班還發明了雲梯等攻城器械，為宮殿、樓舍的設計建造做出了傑出貢獻。

與此同時，楚國京城郢都也有一位名叫「石」的工匠，人們稱他為「匠石」。他的本領據說與魯班不相上下。匠石有一個好朋友，常在鼻子尖上薄薄地塗一層白灰，匠石手持斧子，瞄準鼻子尖，「唰」的一下，就用斧子把白灰掃掉了，卻連鼻子尖的皮都蹭不到。

柳宗元因此說：如果有人敢拿著斧頭在魯班和匠石的門前賣弄，那就太不自量力了。

後來，明代詩人梅之煥去憑弔李白墓，發現墓旁石壁上被來往遊人題滿了拙劣的詩句，不堪入目。他氣憤之餘寫下了一首詩：「采石江邊一堆土，李白之名高千古；來來往往一首詩，魯班門前弄大斧。」諷刺那些在李白（大詩人）面前賣弄詩才的人。

從此，「班門弄斧」便成為一個家喻戶曉的成語。

##### English Version

During the Spring and Autumn and Warring States periods, there lived in the State of Lu a master carpenter named Lu Ban.He was exceptionally clever and skilled at making exquisite utensils. People called him the "Ingenious Man," and he has long been regarded as the founding father of carpentry.

Legend has it that Lu Ban once carved a wooden phoenix so lifelike that it flew in the sky for three days and three nights without falling.Lu Ban also invented siege weapons such as scaling ladders and made outstanding contributions to the design and construction of palaces and buildings.

At the same time, in Ying, the capital of the State of Chu, there lived a craftsman named "Shi," known as "Craftsman Shi."His skills were said to be on par with Lu Ban's. Craftsman Shi had a good friend who would apply a thin layer of white powder on the tip of his nose. Craftsman Shi would then take his axe, aim at the tip of the nose, and with a "swish," sweep away the powder without even touching the skin.

This is why Liu Zongyuan remarked: If anyone dared to wield an axe before the gates of Lu Ban and Craftsman Shi, they would be utterly overestimating themselves.

Much later, the Ming Dynasty poet Mei Zhihuan visited Li Bai's tomb and found the stone walls surrounding it covered with poorly-written poems left by passing tourists — an eyesore. In indignation, he wrote: "By the Caishi River a mound of earth, / Li Bai's fame echoes through the ages; / Here and there, visitors scribble a poem or two — / Wielding a great axe before Lu Ban's gate."He was satirizing those who dared to show off their poetic talents in front of Li Bai — one of China's greatest poets.

From then on, "Ban Men Nong Fu" became a widely-known idiom.

#### 五、含義與用法 / Meaning and Usage

##### 中文

「班門弄斧」指在行家面前賣弄本領，不自量力。

用法：在句中可作謂語、定語、狀語、賓語。

例句：

- 我在各位老師傅面前談操作經驗，實在是班門弄斧。
- 在他面前賣弄書法，簡直是班門弄斧。

兩種語境：

1. 批評他人：諷刺那些在專家面前炫耀淺薄才藝的人
2. 自謙：表示自己不敢在行家面前賣弄本領

##### English

"Ban Men Nong Fu" means to show off one's skills in front of an expert — to overestimate one's own abilities and embarrass oneself.

Usage: Can function as a predicate, attributive, adverbial, or object in a sentence.

Examples:

- Discussing operational experience in front of all these master workers — I am truly "wielding an axe before Lu Ban's gate."
- Showing off one's calligraphy in front of him is nothing but "Ban Men Nong Fu."

Two Contexts:

1. Criticizing others: Ridiculing those who display lesser skills before experts
2. Self-deprecation: Expressing modesty, indicating one dare not show off in front of true masters

八、現代啟示 / Modern Lesson

「班門弄斧」這個成語告訴我們：為人應當謙虛，不要隨便在行家面前賣弄本事。 

然而在現代社會，也有人提出新的觀點——「弄斧必須到班門」。意思是：要想真正提高技藝，就應該敢於在大行家面前展示自己，接受高手的指點和批評，這樣才能進步。

This idiom teaches us: One should remain humble and not flaunt one's skills before true experts. 

However, in modern society, some have offered a new perspective — "If you must wield an axe, do it at Lu Ban's gate." This means: to truly improve your skills, you must be brave enough to display them before true masters, accept their guidance and criticism, and thus make progress.
