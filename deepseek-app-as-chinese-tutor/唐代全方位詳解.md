# 唐代全方位詳解 — A Comprehensive Overview of the Tang Dynasty

## 一、歷史 — History

### 中文

唐朝（618年－907年）是中國歷史上繼隋朝之後的大一統中原王朝，共歷二十一帝，享國二百八十九年。隋末天下群雄並起，唐國公李淵趁勢在晉陽起兵，於618年稱帝建立唐朝，定都長安。唐太宗李世民繼位後開創了「貞觀之治」；唐高宗承貞觀遺風，開創「永徽之治」。690年，武則天改國號為周；705年神龍革命後唐朝復辟。

唐朝前期政治清明、社會安定，國力強盛。唐太宗廣任賢良，發揮制度作用。唐代實行三省六部制，中樞政體分為中書省、門下省和尚書省。科舉制度在唐代得到進一步發展，成為選拔人才的重要途徑。安史之亂（755-763年）是唐朝由盛轉衰的轉折點。此後藩鎮割據、宦官專權，國力逐漸衰退，最終於907年滅亡。

### English

The Tang Dynasty (618–907 CE) was a major imperial dynasty of China that succeeded the Sui Dynasty, spanning 289 years with twenty-one emperors. In the late Sui period, amid widespread rebellions, Li Yuan — Duke of Tang — raised troops in Jinyang and established the Tang Dynasty in 618, with its capital at Chang’an (modern-day Xi’an). Emperor Taizong (Li Shimin) ushered in the "Reign of Zhenguan"; Emperor Gaozong continued this legacy with the "Reign of Yonghui". In 690, Wu Zetian declared herself emperor and changed the dynastic name to Zhou; the Tang was restored in 705 after the Shenlong Coup.

In its early period, the Tang enjoyed good governance, social stability, and immense national strength. Emperor Taizong appointed capable officials and strengthened institutional frameworks. The Tang employed the "Three Departments and Six Ministries" system — the Department of State Affairs, the Chancellery, and the Secretariat. The imperial examination system was further developed during the Tang and became a key mechanism for selecting talent. The An Lushan Rebellion (755–763 CE) marked a turning point, after which the dynasty declined due to regional military governors' autonomy and eunuch domination, eventually falling in 907.

## 二、疆域 — Territory

### 中文

唐朝疆域空前遼闊，極盛時期東起朝鮮半島，南抵越南順化一帶，西達中亞鹹海，北包貝加爾湖至葉尼塞河下游。公元669年（唐高宗總章二年）疆域面積達到頂峰，約1237萬平方公里。唐朝也是自秦漢以來第一個不使用前朝長城、也不修築長城的統一王朝。其鼎盛時期，中亞的綠洲地帶皆受唐朝支配。唐朝對周邊民族採用羈縻政策，通過設立都護府等方式管理廣大邊疆地區。唐代地理學家賈耽於801年繪製了《海內華夷圖》，涵蓋唐朝本土及域外數百個國家，是當時已知世界的巨型地圖。

### English

The Tang Dynasty boasted unprecedentedly vast territories. At its zenith, its domain stretched east to the Korean Peninsula, south to present-day Huế in Vietnam, west to the Aral Sea in Central Asia, and north to Lake Baikal and the lower Yenisei River. In 669 CE (the second year of the Zongzhang era under Emperor Gaozong), the empire reached its maximum extent of approximately 12.37 million square kilometers. The Tang was also the first unified dynasty since the Qin and Han that did not use or build the Great Wall. At its peak, the oasis regions of Central Asia were under Tang dominion. The Tang employed a "loose-rein" policy (羈縻 jīmí) toward neighboring peoples and administered its vast frontiers through protectorates (都護府 dūhùfǔ). In 801, the Tang geographer Jia Dan completed the _Hainei Huayi Tu_ (Map of China and the Barbarian States Within the Seas), a giant map of the known world covering Tang territories and hundreds of foreign countries.

## 三、文化 — Culture

### 中文

唐代文化燦爛輝煌，是中國文化的黃金時代。唐詩是唐代文學的最高成就，留存至今的唐詩近五萬首，作者兩千餘人。著名詩人包括李白、杜甫、王維、白居易等。唐代也是散文、傳奇小說和敦煌變文蓬勃發展的時期。

佛教在唐代達到鼎盛，中國本身成為佛教學習的重要中心，吸引各國學僧前來。長安和洛陽兩京匯聚了來自中亞、阿拉伯、波斯、朝鮮和日本的商人與使節，國際化程度極高。景教（基督教聶斯脫利派）在長安也有活動，廣州則建有清真寺。唐代社會開放包容，皇室李氏本身即具有非漢族血統。唐代婦女的社會地位相對較高，在宮廷中獲得更多自主權。唐三彩是唐代最具代表性的陶瓷工藝，以黃、綠、白三色釉彩著稱。唐代書法有顏真卿、柳公權等大家，繪畫有閻立本、吳道子、周昉等名家。

### English

Tang culture was splendid and brilliant, representing a golden age of Chinese civilization. Tang poetry is the greatest literary achievement of the dynasty; nearly 50,000 poems by over 2,000 poets survive to this day. Renowned poets include Li Bai, Du Fu, Wang Wei, and Bo Juyi. The Tang also witnessed the flourishing of prose, legendary fiction, and Dunhuang bianwen (transformation texts).

Buddhism reached its zenith during the Tang, with China itself becoming a major center of Buddhist learning that attracted students and pilgrims from other countries. The two capitals, Chang’an and Luoyang, were flooded with traders and envoys from Central Asia, Arabia, Persia, Korea, and Japan, making them truly international metropolises. A Nestorian Christian community existed in Chang’an, and mosques were established in Guangzhou. Tang society was liberal and tolerant of foreign views; the royal family itself, surnamed Li, was of non-Han Chinese origin. Women enjoyed relatively higher social status during the Tang, attaining greater freedom and influence at court. Tang sancai ("three-colored") glazed wares — featuring yellow, green, and creamy white — are the most iconic Tang ceramics. Tang calligraphy produced masters such as Yan Zhenqing and Liu Gongquan, while painting was advanced by artists like Yan Liben, Wu Daozi, and Zhou Fang.

## 四、科技 — Science and Technology

### 中文

唐代科學技術成就卓著，是中國科技史上的高峯時期。**四大發明**中的兩項在唐代取得重大進展：

**雕版印刷術**：雕版印刷術最遲出現於唐玄宗以前，在唐代得到廣泛應用。現存世界上最早的、有確切日期的雕版印刷品是868年（唐咸通九年）印刷的《金剛經》。唐代還出現了雕版印刷的報紙《開元雜報》。

**火藥**：火藥發明於唐朝中期，唐代中期的書籍中已有製作火藥的配方記載；唐朝末年火藥開始用於軍事。

**天文學**：唐代傑出天文學家僧一行（張遂）編制了《大衍歷》，比較準確地反映了太陽運行規律。他通過實測，得出地球子午線1°的長度約合123.7公里，是世界上用科學方法實測地球子午線長度的創始人。李淳風審定並注解了中國古代算學名著。

**醫學**：唐代完成了世界上第一部由國家編定頒佈的藥典——《唐本草》（又稱《新修本草》）。

**造紙術的傳播**：怛羅斯之戰（751年）後，被俘的唐軍將造紙術傳入阿拉伯地區，繼而西傳至歐洲。

### English

The Tang Dynasty achieved remarkable scientific and technological accomplishments, representing a peak period in Chinese scientific history. Two of the **Four Great Inventions** saw major advances during the Tang:

**Woodblock Printing**: Woodblock printing appeared no later than the reign of Emperor Xuanzong and was widely applied during the Tang. The earliest surviving dated woodblock-printed work in the world is the _Diamond Sutra_, printed in 868 CE (the ninth year of the Xiantong era). The Tang also saw the emergence of the _Kaiyuan Zabao_ (Kaiyuan Miscellaneous Gazette), the earliest known printed newspaper.

**Gunpowder**: Gunpowder was invented in the mid-Tang period, with recipes for its production recorded in texts from that era; by the late Tang, gunpowder had begun to be used in warfare.

**Astronomy**: The eminent Tang astronomer Yi Xing (Zhang Sui) compiled the _Dayan Calendar_, which accurately reflected the laws of solar movement. Through empirical measurement, he calculated that one degree of the Earth's meridian was approximately 123.7 kilometers, making him the first in the world to scientifically measure the meridian's length. Li Chunfeng reviewed and annotated ancient Chinese mathematical classics.

**Medicine**: The Tang completed the world's first nationally compiled and promulgated pharmacopoeia — the _Tang Bencao_ (Newly Revised Materia Medica).

**Spread of Papermaking**: After the Battle of Talas (751 CE), captured Tang soldiers introduced papermaking to the Arab world, from which it subsequently spread westward to Europe.

## 五、經濟 — Economy

### 中文

唐代經濟繁榮，是當時世界上最大的經濟體之一，僅與阿拔斯哈里發國相當。

**農業**：唐代得益於南方肥沃的農田，農業生產大幅提高。大運河得到延伸和擴建，加快了從長江流域富庶地區向北方運送糧食的速度。

**賦稅制度**：唐朝前期實行**租庸調法**，以均田制為基礎，按丁徵收定額賦稅並服徭役。唐朝中期以後，因均田制瓦解，780年（唐德宗建中元年）宰相楊炎主持推行**兩稅法**，取消按人丁徵稅的做法，改為按土地和財產多少徵收地稅和戶稅。兩稅法按夏、秋兩季以貨幣形式徵收。

**手工業**：唐代手工業發達，紡織、陶瓷、金屬加工等行業均有長足進步。唐朝後期出現了棉花種植壟斷。唐代瓷器遠銷海外，成為重要出口商品。

**貨幣**：唐高祖李淵鑄造「開元通寶」，成為唐代通行貨幣。唐代實行複雜的金融體系，承認穀物、錢幣和紡織品均可作為貨幣使用。

**城市**：長安成為世界上最富有、人口最多的城市，擁有約百萬居民，包括來自其他民族和國家的人士。

### English

The Tang economy was prosperous and was one of the largest economies in the world at the time, rivaled only by the Abbasid Caliphate.

**Agriculture**: The Tang benefited from the rich farmlands of southern China, leading to significant increases in agricultural production. The Grand Canal was extended and expanded, speeding the transport of grain from the wealthy Yangtze River region to the north.

**Taxation System**: In the early Tang, the **Zuyongdiao** (land-tax, corvée, and household-tax) system was implemented, based on the equal-field system (juntian), requiring fixed taxes and corvée labor per adult male. After the mid-Tang, as the equal-field system collapsed, Chancellor Yang Yan introduced the **Two-Tax Law** in 780 CE (the first year of the Jianzhong era under Emperor Dezong), which abolished per-capita taxation and instead levied land tax and household tax based on land and property holdings. The Two-Tax Law was collected in currency twice a year, in summer and autumn.

**Handicrafts**: Tang handicrafts were highly developed, with textiles, ceramics, and metalworking all making great strides. A monopoly on cotton cultivation emerged in the late Tang. Tang porcelain was exported far overseas and became a major export commodity.

**Currency**: Emperor Gaozu (Li Yuan) minted the "Kaiyuan Tongbao" coin, which became the standard currency of the Tang. The Tang operated a complex financial system that recognized grain, coins, and textiles as forms of money.

**Cities**: Chang’an became the richest and most populous city in the world, with approximately one million inhabitants, including people from other ethnic groups and countries.

## 六、貿易 — Trade

### 中文

唐代貿易極度繁榮，通過陸上和海上兩條絲綢之路與世界緊密相連。

**陸上絲綢之路**：唐代確保了陸上貿易路線——絲綢之路——的和平與安全，這條路線遠至羅馬。唐朝與超過300個國家和地區有交往，每年各國使節絡繹不絕。陸路交通以長安為中心，經由中亞通往阿拉伯地區。長安作為絲綢之路的東端起點，成為全球銀行業和貿易的中心。西市中聚集了大量波斯、大食和中亞諸國商人。

**海上絲綢之路**：唐代海上貿易同樣繁榮。安史之亂後，海上絲綢之路更加興盛。廣州成為唐代最大的海外貿易中心，朝廷於714年（唐玄宗開元二年）在廣州設置市舶司（後稱市舶使），負責管理進出口船舶、徵收關稅（「下碇稅」）及收購珍貴物品。唐代宗年間每年有四千餘艘阿拉伯商船抵達廣州。揚州、明州（寧波）、泉州也逐漸成為中外貿易中心。

**貿易商品**：中國主要出口絲綢、瓷器和紙張；進口商品包括香料、藥品、珠寶、紡織品、珍珠、珊瑚和芳香木材等。絲綢之路上的貿易不僅促進了商品流通，也推動了技術、宗教和文化的交流。

### English

Tang trade flourished to an extraordinary degree, connecting China with the world through both the overland and maritime Silk Roads.

**Overland Silk Road**: The Tang secured peace and safety on the overland trade routes — the Silk Road — which extended as far as Rome. The Tang communicated with over 300 countries and regions, with ambassadors arriving each year. Overland transport was centered on Chang’an, passing through Central Asia to the Arab region. As the eastern terminus of the Silk Road, Chang’an became a global hub of banking and trade. The Western Market (西市 Xīshì) was crowded with merchants from Persia, the Arab world, and Central Asian states.

**Maritime Silk Road**: Maritime trade also flourished during the Tang. After the An Lushan Rebellion, the Maritime Silk Road grew even more prominent. Guangzhou became the largest overseas trade center of the Tang. In 714 CE (the second year of the Kaiyuan era under Emperor Xuanzong), the court established the Maritime Trade Supervisorate (市舶司 shìbósī, later 市舶使 shìbóshǐ) in Guangzhou to manage import/export vessels, collect customs duties (下碇稅, the "xiàdìng tax"), and purchase precious goods. During the reign of Emperor Daizong, over 4,000 Arab merchant ships arrived in Guangzhou each year. Yangzhou, Mingzhou (Ningbo), and Quanzhou also gradually emerged as centers of Sino-foreign trade.

**Trade Goods**: China primarily exported silk, porcelain, and paper; imported goods included spices, medicines, jewelry, textiles, pearls, coral, and aromatic woods. Trade along the Silk Roads not only facilitated the exchange of goods but also promoted the transmission of technologies, religions, and cultures.
