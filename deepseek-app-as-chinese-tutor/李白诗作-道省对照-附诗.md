### 补充说明（Additional Notes）

**·** 唐代的“道”最初为 10 道，玄宗时增至 15 道，辖区辽阔，与现代省界并非精确对应。

  _The Tang circuits were large supervisory regions (initially 10, later 15 under Emperor Xuanzong); their boundaries do not perfectly match today’s provinces._

**·** 同一首诗可能涉及多个道，表中仅选取最具代表性的地点作为示例。

  _A single poem may refer to multiple circuits; the table lists only the most representative location for each._

**·** 另有 陇右道（甘肃、青海）和 河北道（河北）在诗中也曾提及，但因现存作品极少，未列入主表。

  _A few poems mention Longyou (Gansu/Qinghai) and Hebei circuits, but these are marginal and omitted from the main list._
