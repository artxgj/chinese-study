# 七闽族详解 / A Detailed Explanation of the Qimin Tribe

---

## 一、定义与名称由来 / I. Definition and Name Origin

### 中文：
「七闽」是福建最古老的称谓之一，最早见于《周礼·夏官·职方氏》：「辨其邦国、都鄙、四夷、八蛮、七闽、九貉、五戎、六狄之人民」。七闽指的是周朝时散居在今福建和浙江南部的七支以蛇为图腾的闽族部落。东汉郑玄注称：「闽，蛮之别也……四、八、七、九、五、六，周之所服国数也」，意即「七」代表臣服于周王朝的七个方国或部族数量。七闽并非行政区划的定数，而是出自西周在闽「所服国数也」。

### English:
"Qimin" (Seven Min) is one of the oldest appellations for Fujian, first recorded in the _Rites of Zhou_ (Zhouli) - "Distinguishing the peoples of the states, towns, Four Yi, Eight Man, Seven Min, Nine Mo, Five Rong, and Six Di". Qimin refers to the seven Min tribes that scattered across present-day Fujian and southern Zhejiang during the Zhou Dynasty, all worshipping the snake as their totem. Eastern Han scholar Zheng Xuan commented: "Min is a branch of Man... the numbers four, eight, seven, nine, five, and six represent the number of states subjugated by the Zhou", meaning "seven" denotes the number of small states or tribes that pledged allegiance to the Zhou royal court. Qimin was not a fixed administrative division but derived from the Western Zhou's "number of subjugated states in Min".

---

## 二、族群归属 / II. Ethnic Classification

### 中文：
七闽族属于古闽族（亦称闽族、古闽人、先秦闽族），是百越的一支。考古可追溯到距今约5000年的新石器时代。在国际学术界，古闽族被歸類為南島語族。他们是福建最早的土著民族之一。

### English:
The Qimin people belong to the ancient Min tribe (also known as the Min tribe, ancient Min people, or pre-Qin Min tribe), which was one branch of the Baiyue (Hundred Yue) peoples. Archaeological evidence traces them back to the Neolithic Age, approximately 5,000 years ago. In international academia, the ancient Min are classified as part of the Austronesian language family. They were among the earliest indigenous peoples of Fujian.

---

## 三、地理分布 / III. Geographical Distribution

### 中文：
七闽的地域涵盖今福建全境及浙江南部、广东东部潮梅一带、江西东南部。部分学者认为古闽族亦分布在台湾，甚至认为台湾少数民族是其后裔，但此说未在学界形成主流观点。

### English:
The territory of Qimin covered the entire area of present-day Fujian, as well as southern Zhejiang, the Chaozhou-Meizhou area of eastern Guangdong, and southeastern Jiangxi. Some scholars believe the ancient Min also lived in Taiwan and even consider Taiwanese indigenous peoples to be their descendants, though this view has not become mainstream in academia.

---

## 四、起源與早期歷史 / IV. Origins and Early History

### 中文：
「闽」的名称最早见于《周礼》，而《山海经·海内南经》则最早提及「闽」字。考古方面，古闽族人在福建沿海和闽江流域发展出了昙石山文化和黄土仑文化。大约在距今3000年前的夏商时代，古闽族已进入青铜器时代。他们从事渔猎和原始农业，拥有一定的陶瓷、纺织和造船技术。

### English:
The name "Min" first appeared in the Rites of Zhou, while the Classic of Mountains and Seas (Shanhaijing) was the earliest text to mention the character "Min". Archaeologically, the ancient Min developed the Tanshishan culture and Huangtulun culture along the Fujian coast and the Min River basin. Approximately 3,000 years ago, during the Xia-Shang period, the ancient Min had already entered the Bronze Age. They engaged in fishing, hunting, and primitive agriculture, and possessed certain skills in ceramics, textiles, and shipbuilding.

---

## 五、文化習俗 / V. Culture and Customs

### 中文：
七闽族最显著的文化特征是**蛇图腾崇拜**。这一崇拜记载于上古神话传说中，在今天的福建部分地区仍有残留。闽族是「蛮」的一种，服饰有别于越人，可能为「顶髻徒跣」（头顶梳髻、赤足），但沿海闽族人可能也像越人一样「断发文身」（剪短头发、在身上刺青）。山区古闽族有崖葬、悬棺葬（船棺葬）的丧葬习俗。他们的居住场所经历了从半地穴式建筑到干栏式建筑的发展。船只则是重要的交通工具。

### English:
The most distinctive cultural feature of the Qimin tribe was **snake totem worship**. This worship was recorded in ancient myths and legends, and some remnants still exist in parts of Fujian today. The Min were considered a type of "Man" (southern barbarian). Their attire differed from the Yue people—possibly "topknot and barefoot"—though coastal Min may have also practiced "short hair and body tattoos" like the Yue. In mountainous areas, the ancient Min practiced cliff burial and hanging coffin burial (boat-coffin burial). Their dwellings evolved from semi-subterranean structures to stilt houses. Boats were important means of transportation.

---

## 六、七個部族 / VI. The Seven Tribes

### 中文：
清代蔡永兼在《西山杂记》中记载了七闽的七大族群：


1\. **泉郡畲家** — 泉州地区的畲族

2\. **三山之蜒户** — 福州地区（三山为福州别称）的蜒户（水上居民）

3\. **剑州之高山** — 剑州（今南平）地区的高山族

4\. **邵武之武夷** — 邵武地区的武夷族

5\. **漳岩之龙门** — 漳州岩区的龙门族

6\. **漳郡之南太武** — 漳州南太武地区部族

7\. **汀赣之客家** — 汀州（今龙岩）与赣州（江西）地区的客家

### English:
Qing Dynasty scholar Cai Yongjian recorded the seven major Qimin tribal groups in his _Miscellaneous Records of Xishan_:


1\. **Shejia of Quanjun** — She people in the Quanzhou area

2\. **Yanhu of Sanshan** — Tanka (boat-dwelling people) in the Fuzhou area (Sanshan being an alternate name for Fuzhou)

3\. **Gaoshan of Jianzhou** — Gaoshan people in the Jianzhou area (present-day Nanping)

4\. **Wuyi of Shaowu** — Wuyi people in the Shaowu area

5\. **Longmen of Zhangyan** — Longmen people in the Zhangzhou rock area

6\. **Nantaiwu of Zhangjun** — Tribes in the Nantaiwu area of Zhangzhou

7\. **Hakka of Tinggan** — Hakka people in the Tingzhou (present-day Longyan) and Ganzhou (Jiangxi) areas

---

## 七、與越人融合 / VII. Integration with the Yue People

### 中文：
七闽族历史上最重要的事件是与越人的融合。战国晚期（约公元前334年），越国被楚国所灭后，越国南下的于越人进入闽地。由于地理上的邻近，早在越侯允常在位时期就有于越族人到闽族人居住地定居。古闽族（七闽）与于越人融合，形成了**闽越族**。闽越族是百越的一支，也是后来闽越国的前身。

### English:
The most significant event in Qimin history was their integration with the Yue people. In the late Warring States period (c. 334 BC), after the Yue Kingdom was destroyed by the State of Chu, the Yuyue people from the Yue Kingdom migrated south into the Min region. Due to geographical proximity, Yuyue people had already settled in Min territory as early as the reign of Marquis Yunchang of Yue. The ancient Min (Qimin) integrated with the Yuyue people to form the **Minyue people**. The Minyue were a branch of the Baiyue and the predecessor of the later Minyue Kingdom.

---

## 八、閩越國與秦漢時期 / VIII. The Minyue Kingdom and the Qin-Han Period

### 中文：
七闽之地最早建立的政权是战国时期越国后裔建立的闽越国。公元前334年，闽越国建立。秦汉时期，七闽地域与闽中郡、闽越国的辖地范围存在历史延续性。然而，闽越国在汉武帝时期因反叛中央王朝而被平定，居民被大规模迁出，导致福建地区有三百多年几乎消失于历史记载中。

### English:
The earliest regime established in the Qimin region was the Minyue Kingdom, founded by descendants of the Yue Kingdom during the Warring States period. In 334 BC, the Minyue Kingdom was established. During the Qin and Han dynasties, the Qimin region showed historical continuity with the territory of Minzhong Commandery and the Minyue Kingdom. However, after the Minyue Kingdom rebelled against the central dynasty during Emperor Wu of Han's reign, it was suppressed, and its residents were largely relocated, causing Fujian to virtually disappear from historical records for over 300 years.

---

## 九、後裔與現代傳承 / IX. Descendants and Modern Legacy

### 中文：
七闽的后裔主要通过以下途径延续至今：


1\. **闽越人融合**：七闽与于越人融合成闽越人，闽越人又逐渐融入汉族及其他南方民族之中。

2\. **畲族**：七闽中的「泉郡畲家」被认为是畲族的祖先之一。畲族至今仍是福建及周边地区的重要少数民族。

3\. **客家**：七闽中的「汀赣之客家」与后来的客家民系有渊源关系。

4\. **蜒户（疍民）** ：七闽中的「三山之蜒户」是福建沿海水上居民——疍民的先民。

5\. **文化遺產**：蛇图腾崇拜在福建部分地区仍有残留；昙石山文化遗址等考古发现见证了古闽族文明；「七闽」一词本身演变为福建的代称，与宋代以后出现的「八闽」并用于称呼福建。

### English:
The descendants of the Qimin people have continued to the present day through the following channels:


1\. **Integration into the Minyue people**: The Qimin integrated with the Yuyue to form the Minyue people, who in turn gradually assimilated into the Han Chinese and other southern ethnic groups.

2\. **She people**: The "Shejia of Quanjun" among the seven Qimin tribes is considered one of the ancestors of the She people. The She remain an important ethnic minority in Fujian and surrounding areas today.

3\. **Hakka people**: The "Hakka of Tinggan" among the seven Qimin tribes has historical connections with the later Hakka ethnic group.

4\. **Tanka people**: The "Yanhu of Sanshan" were the ancestors of the Tanka (boat-dwelling people) along the Fujian coast.

5\. **Cultural heritage**: Snake totem worship still has remnants in parts of Fujian; archaeological discoveries such as the Tanshishan cultural site bear witness to the ancient Min civilization; the term "Qimin" itself evolved into a synonym for Fujian, used alongside "Bamin" (Eight Min)—which originated from the eight administrative regions established in Fujian during the Song Dynasty—to refer to the province.
