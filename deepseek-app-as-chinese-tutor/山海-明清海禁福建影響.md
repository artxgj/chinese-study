# 明清海禁政策及其對福建的影響（中英雙語詳解）

# The Ming-Qing Maritime Ban (Haijin) and Its Impact on Fujian (Bilingual Detailed Analysis)

---

## 一、明代海禁的原因 / I. Reasons for the Ming Maritime Ban

明太祖朱元璋建立明朝後，於洪武四年（1371年）正式推行海禁政策。其原因主要有以下幾點：

After establishing the Ming dynasty, the Hongwu Emperor formally implemented the maritime ban (Haijin) in 1371. The main reasons were as follows:

### （一）政治軍事因素 / Political and Military Factors

1. **防範元朝殘餘勢力**：朱元璋為防範逃亡海上的張士誠、方國珍等元末割據勢力的餘部，嚴禁沿海人民出海貿易。 <br>
   **Countering Yuan remnants**: To prevent the remaining forces of Zhang Shicheng and Fang Guozhen from rallying at sea, the Hongwu Emperor strictly prohibited coastal residents from engaging in overseas trade. <br>
2. **防倭與外交考量**：明政府鑑於倭寇騷擾及對日本外交的失敗，實施海禁以封鎖海疆，期望達到「閉其關聽其自服而不之討」的效果。 <br>
   **Defense against Japanese pirates (Wokou) and diplomatic concerns**: Due to Japanese pirate raids and failed diplomacy with Japan, the Ming government enforced the maritime ban to seal off the coast, hoping to force Japan into submission through isolation.  <br>
3. **防範權臣勾結外國**：朱元璋懾於日本可能幫助胡惟庸奪取帝位的傳聞，因此厲行海禁以鞏固皇權統治。 <br>
   **Preventing officials from colluding with foreign powers**: The Hongwu Emperor, fearing that Japan might assist Hu Weiyong in seizing the throne, strictly enforced the maritime ban to consolidate imperial rule.

### （二）經濟與思想因素 / Economic and Ideological Factors

1. **國家壟斷海外貿易**：明朝試圖以國家力量壟斷海上貿易，僅允許朝貢貿易形式存在，嚴禁私人出海貿易。下西洋只是皇家的特權，百姓斷不可染指。 <br>
   **State monopoly** over maritime trade: The Ming state sought to monopolize maritime trade, permitting only the tribute-trade system while strictly prohibiting private overseas commerce.   <br>
2. **財政考量**：鄭和下西洋花費巨大——永樂年間新建和改建約二千艘海船，每艘寶船造價約五六千銀兩。加上蒙古對北方邊境威脅增大，在資源有限的情況下，明朝戰略重心轉向北方。 <br>
   **Fiscal considerations**: Admiral Zheng He's voyages were enormously expensive—during the Yongle reign, about 2,000 ships were built or refitted, each large treasure ship costing roughly 5,000–6,000 taels of silver. With increasing Mongol threats on the northern border, the Ming shifted its strategic focus northward under resource constraints.  <br>
3. **小農經濟思維**：明代統治者基於自給自足的小農經濟觀念，對海洋貿易缺乏重視。 <br>
   **Agrarian economic mindset**: Ming rulers, rooted in a self-sufficient small-peasant economy, placed little value on maritime trade.

### （三）法律層面 / Legal Framework

《大明律》中設有「海禁」條款，以立法形式強令軍民遵守。主要內容包括： <br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;The "Great Ming Code" contained specific "maritime ban" clauses, legally compelling all subjects to comply. Key provisions included:

- 禁止官民建造二桅以上遠洋船出海，違者處斬並梟首示眾；
- Prohibiting officials and commoners from building ocean-going vessels with two or more masts—violators were beheaded and their heads displayed publicly；
- 禁止私運貨物出海及私販洋貨；
- Prohibiting the private shipment of goods overseas and the private sale of foreign goods；
- 禁止將人口、軍器出境下海，違者處絞刑。
- Prohibiting the export of people or military equipment by sea—violators were hanged.

## 二、清代海禁的原因 / II. Reasons for the Qing Maritime Ban

清代前期海禁政策的推行，主要原因如下： <br>
The early Qing maritime ban was implemented for the following primary reasons:

### （一）打擊鄭氏抗清勢力 / Combating the Zheng Anti-Qing Forces

1. **防範鄭成功反攻**：明朝敗亡後，鄭成功等政治勢力矢志反清復明，長期依靠海上力量與清朝周旋。 <br>
   **Preventing Zheng Chenggong's counterattack**: After the fall of the Ming, Zheng Chenggong and his forces were determined to restore the Ming and relied on their naval power to resist the Qing. <br>
2. **經濟封鎖**：清廷了解到走私活動是鄭氏集團賴以生存的重要財政來源，因此頒布禁海令，切斷對鄭氏的一切物資與商品供應。 <br>
   **Economic blockade**: The Qing court recognized that smuggling was a vital financial source for the Zheng regime, and thus issued the maritime ban to cut off all material and commodity supplies to Zheng's forces. <br>
3. **遷界令（1661年）** ：順治十八年，清政府正式頒布遷海令，強行將沿海地區民眾內遷30至50里，人為形成沿海無人地帶，徹底切斷鄭氏政權與內地的聯繫。福建因是鄭成功勢力的主要活動空間，成為遷海令執行最嚴格的省份之一。 <br>
   **The Great Clearance (Frontier Shift) of 1661**: In 1661, the Qing government ordered coastal residents to move inland 30 to 50 li (approximately 15–25 km), creating an artificial no-man's land along the coast to sever all contact between the Zheng regime and the mainland. Fujian, being the primary base of Zheng Chenggong's operations, became one of the provinces where the policy was most strictly enforced. <br>

### （二）自然經濟與閉關思維 / Natural Economy and Isolationist Thinking

清代前期，封建自然經濟相對穩定，自給自足的小農經濟對外來商品需求很小，使清統治者固步自封。
In the early Qing, the feudal natural economy was relatively stable; the self-sufficient small-peasant economy had little demand for foreign goods, fostering an insular mindset among Qing rulers.

### （三）後期針對外國商人 / Later Targeting of Foreign Merchants

前期海禁主要針對台灣鄭氏，後期則轉向針對外國商人。乾隆二十二年（1757年）實行「一口通商」政策，僅允許外國人在廣州一地進行貿易。 <br>
While the early ban targeted the Zheng regime in Taiwan, it later shifted toward restricting foreign merchants. In 1757, the Qianlong Emperor implemented the "single-port trade" policy, permitting foreign trade only at Guangzhou.

## 三、海禁對福建的影響 / III. Impact of the Maritime Ban on Fujian

福建「八山一水一分田」，耕地有限，沿海居民世代依海為生。海禁政策對福建造成了極其深遠的影響。 <br>
Fujian is characterized by "80% mountains, 10% water, and 10% arable land"—limited farmland, with coastal residents relying on the sea for generations. The maritime ban had profound and far-reaching impacts on Fujian.

---

### （一）人口影響 / Impact on Population

**1. 大規模人口內遷與傷亡 / Mass Internal Relocation and Casualties**

清初遷界令下，福建沿海居民被迫在極短時間內（通常為三日）撤離家園，房屋全部焚毀。據記載：
Under the early Qing Frontier Shift, Fujian's coastal residents were forced to evacuate within an extremely short period (usually three days), with all houses burned. Records show:

- 康熙元年（1662年）遷海後，福建巡撫許世昌奏稱「海上新遷之民，死亡者八千五百餘人」；
- In 1662, Fujian Governor Xu Shichang reported that "among the newly relocated coastal people, over 8,500 have died"；
- 南安縣等地「人口已什去八九」；
- In Nan'an County and other areas, "eight or nine out of ten people have been lost"；
- 「上自遼東，下至廣東，皆遷徙……百姓失業流離，死亡者以億萬計」。
- "From Liaodong in the north to Guangdong in the south, all were relocated... the people lost their livelihoods and were displaced, with countless deaths".

**2. 海外移民加速 / Accelerated Overseas Emigration**

海禁政策迫使大量福建沿海居民向南洋、台灣及鄰近外島遷移。明中葉海禁鬆弛後，閩粵海洋文化向環中國海區域傳播，構成以閩粵人為核心的海洋貿易網絡。 <br>
The maritime ban forced large numbers of Fujian's coastal residents to migrate to Southeast Asia, Taiwan, and nearby offshore islands. After the relaxation of the ban in the mid-Ming, Fujianese and Cantonese maritime culture spread throughout the seas surrounding China, forming a maritime trade network centered on Fujianese and Cantonese merchants.

3. 海盜化與社會動盪 / Piracy and Social Unrest

「海禁一嚴，無所得食，則轉掠海濱，為害尤酷」。失去生計的沿海民眾被迫鋌而走險，部分人淪為海盜，形成「時商時盜、亦商亦盜」的獨特現象。
&nbsp;&nbsp;"When the maritime ban was strictly enforced, the people had no means of livelihood and turned to plundering the coast, causing great harm". Coastal residents who lost their livelihoods were forced to take desperate risks; some turned to piracy, creating a unique phenomenon of "sometimes merchants, sometimes pirates".

---

### （二）經濟影響 / Impact on Economy

**1. 農業與土地荒廢 / Agriculture and Land Abandonment**

遷界導致福建大量農田荒廢。據記載：「自遷界以來，民田廢棄二萬餘頃，虧減正供約計有二十餘萬之多」。 <br>
The Frontier Shift caused vast tracts of farmland in Fujian to be abandoned. Records state: "Since the relocation, over 20,000 qing (approximately 130,000 hectares) of farmland have been abandoned, resulting in a loss of over 200,000 in tax revenue".

**2. 漁業與鹽業崩潰 / Collapse of Fishing and Salt Industries**  <br>

遷界使福建漁業陷入絕境，海鹽生產基本停頓。沿海數千里土地荒廢。 <br>
The Frontier Shift pushed Fujian's fishing industry to the brink of collapse, and salt production came to a virtual standstill. Thousands of li of coastal land were left desolate.

**3. 富商巨室衰敗 / Decline of Merchant Elites**

「往時閩中巨室皆擅海舶之利……是以富甲天下。自海禁嚴而閩貧矣」。曾經因海外貿易富甲一方的福建巨商，在海禁之下日漸衰敗。  <br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;"In the past, Fujian's great families all profited from ocean-going ships... thus they were the wealthiest under heaven. Since the maritime ban was strictly enforced, Fujian has become poor". The wealthy Fujianese merchant families who had once prospered from overseas trade gradually declined under the ban.

**4. 地方財政惡化 / Deterioration of Local Finances**

賦稅減少導致「賦稅日缺，國用不足」。福建總督范承謨曾上奏反映這一困境。  <br>
Reduced tax revenue meant "daily shortfalls in tax collection and insufficient state funds". Fujian Governor Fan Chengmo memorialized the court about this predicament.

---

### （三）貿易影響 / Impact on Trade

**1. 合法貿易萎縮 / Contraction of Legal Trade**

- **泉州港衰落**：明清海禁是泉州港由繁榮走向衰落的關鍵原因。福建省市舶司由泉州遷至福州，使泉州失去國際貿易地位。 <br>
  Decline of Quanzhou Port: The Ming-Qing maritime ban was the key reason for Quanzhou Port's decline from prosperity. The Fujian Provincial Shipping Administration was relocated from Quanzhou to Fuzhou, depriving Quanzhou of its international trade status.
- **福州港受限**：成化年間，福州成為專通琉球的朝貢貿易港口，民間貿易受到嚴重壓制。
  Fuzhou Port restricted: During the Chenghua reign, Fuzhou became a port exclusively for tribute trade with the Ryukyu Kingdom, with private trade severely suppressed.

**2. 走私貿易興起 / Rise of Smuggling**   <br>

明初的海禁政策影響了正常的福建沿海海洋貿易，走私貿易隨之興起。福建擁有悠久的海上貿易傳統，面對嚴厲的海禁政策，民眾為求生路只得轉向走私貿易。月港正是在此背景下異軍突起。 <br>
The early Ming maritime ban disrupted normal coastal trade in Fujian, giving rise to smuggling. Fujian had a long tradition of maritime trade; facing severe restrictions, the people turned to smuggling to survive. Yuegang (Moon Harbor) emerged precisely under these circumstances.

**3. 月港的興起與隆慶開關 / Rise of Yuegang and the Longqing Opening**

月港地處九龍江入海口，港道水淺、暗礁密布，大型官艦難以深入，在海禁高壓下反而獲得相對安全的生長空間。至正德、嘉靖年間，月港已從一個小漁村發展為「方物之珍，家貯戶峙」的繁華市鎮。 <br>
Located at the mouth of the Jiulong River with shallow channels and numerous hidden reefs that made it difficult for large official warships to enter, Yuegang gained a relatively safe space to grow under the高压 maritime ban. By the Zhengde and Jiajing reigns, Yuegang had developed from a small fishing village into a prosperous town where "rare goods were stored in every household".

隆慶元年（1567年），明穆宗批准在月港部分開放海禁，史稱「隆慶開關」。月港由此成為大明王朝唯一合法的海上貿易口岸。 <br>
In 1567, the Longqing Emperor approved the partial opening of maritime trade at Yuegang, historically known as the "Longqing Opening". Yuegang thus became the only legal maritime trade port of the Ming dynasty.

**4. 對外貿易中斷與恢復困難 / Trade Interruption and Difficult Recovery**

清初遷界使中外貿易斷絕二十餘年，沿海港口淪為空港。康熙二十二年（1683年）清朝收復台灣後逐步解除海禁。但復界後沿海商貿活動雖一度恢復，總體趨勢持續低落。  <br>
The early Qing Frontier Shift中断了 Sino-foreign trade for over twenty years, with coastal ports becoming empty harbors. After the Qing收复了 Taiwan in 1683, the maritime ban was gradually lifted. However, although coastal trade recovered somewhat after the restoration, the overall trend remained one of continued decline.

---

## 四、總結 / IV. Conclusion

明清兩代的海禁政策，根源於政治軍事防禦、國家壟斷貿易及小農經濟思維。明代海禁以防倭、防元朝殘餘及鞏固皇權為主要目的；清代海禁則以打擊鄭氏抗清勢力為核心動機，並在順治年間達到極端——推行殘酷的遷界令。

The maritime ban policies of the Ming and Qing dynasties originated from political and military defense concerns, state monopoly over trade, and small-peasant economic thinking. The Ming ban aimed primarily at defending against the Japanese, suppressing Yuan remnants, and consolidating imperial power; the Qing ban was centered on combating the Zheng anti-Qing forces, reaching its extreme form during the Shunzhi reign with the brutal Frontier Shift policy.

對福建而言，海禁政策的影響堪稱災難性：

For Fujian, the impact of the maritime ban was nothing short of catastrophic:

- **人口方面**：大規模強制內遷導致大量傷亡與流離失所，同時加速了福建人向海外移民的進程，也催生了海盜問題； <br>
  **Population**: Mass forced relocation caused large-scale casualties and displacement, while simultaneously accelerating Fujianese emigration overseas and giving rise to piracy;
- **經濟方面**：農田荒廢、漁鹽業崩潰、富商衰敗、地方財政惡化，沿海經濟遭到全面破壞；  <br>
  **Economy**: Farmland abandonment, collapse of fishing and salt industries, decline of wealthy merchants, and deterioration of local finances—the coastal economy was comprehensively devastated; 
- **貿易方面**：合法貿易萎縮，泉州港衰落，但走私貿易興起，月港在夾縫中崛起成為「隆慶開關」後的唯一合法貿易口岸。然而清初遷界使貿易再度中斷，復界後也難以恢復昔日榮光。  <br>
  **Trade**: Legal trade contracted and Quanzhou Port declined, but smuggling rose and Yuegang emerged as the only legal trade port after the Longqing Opening. However, the early Qing Frontier Shift interrupted trade once again, and even after restoration, it could not regain its former glory. 

海禁政策雖在某種程度上達到了維護海疆安定的短期政治目的，但從長遠來看，其對福建社會經濟的破壞以及對中國海洋事業發展的阻滯，影響極為深遠。

Although the maritime ban achieved the short-term political goal of maintaining coastal stability to some extent， in the long run, its destructive impact on Fujian's society and economy, as well as its stifling effect on China's maritime development, were extraordinarily far-reaching.
