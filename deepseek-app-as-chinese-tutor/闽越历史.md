# 闽越国历史详解 — A Detailed History of the Minyue Kingdom

---

## 一、闽越的创立 — The Founding of Minyue

### 中文

闽越国是战国至西汉时期存在于今中国福建地区的一个地方政权。其创立与越国的灭亡直接相关。

越国灭亡与南迁：公元前334年，越国末代君王无疆兴兵伐楚，兵败战殁，越国主力覆灭。无疆长子驺玉率领宗族千余部众浮海南奔，辗转入闽，最初落脚于今福州长乐一带，后定居于今福州市晋安区新店镇古城村一带。南迁的于越遗民与福建本土古老的七闽土著部落长期杂居共处、互通婚姻、逐步融合，形成闽越族。

早期闽越国：驺玉在福建建立早期闽越政权，成为闽越开国始祖。此后历经驺尊、驺亲、驺恒、驺朝、驺睦等君王，政权逐步稳固。

无诸受封立国：无诸（驺无诸）为越王勾践后裔、无疆七世孙。秦统一后，无诸被削去王号，降为君长。秦末，无诸率闽中士卒北上协助诸侯灭秦；楚汉相争时，他又出兵辅佐刘邦打败项羽。汉高祖五年（公元前202年） ，刘邦封无诸为闽越王，闽越国正式建立。无诸随后在福州冶山之麓筑建都城“冶城”，这是福州建城的开始。

### English

The Minyue Kingdom was a regional regime that existed in what is now Fujian Province, China, from the Warring States period to the Western Han Dynasty. Its founding was directly linked to the fall of the Yue state.

The Fall of Yue and Southward Migration: In 334 BCE, Wujiang, the last king of Yue, launched a military campaign against Chu and was defeated and killed, leading to the collapse of Yue's main forces. Wujiang's eldest son, Zou Yu, led over a thousand clansmen southward by sea to Fujian. They initially settled in the area of present-day Changle, Fuzhou, and later established themselves in what is now Gucheng Village, Xindian Town, Jin'an District, Fuzhou. The migrating Yue people gradually intermingled and intermarried with the local indigenous "Seven Min" tribes, eventually forming the Minyue people.

The Early Minyue State: Zou Yu established an early Minyue regime in Fujian, becoming the founding ancestor of Minyue. He was succeeded by rulers including Zou Zun, Zou Qin, Zou Heng, Zou Chao, and Zou Mu, during which the regime gradually consolidated.

Wuzhu Receives Title and Establishes the Kingdom: Wuzhu (Zou Wuzhu) was a descendant of King Goujian of Yue and the seventh-generation grandson of Wujiang. After the Qin unified China, Wuzhu was demoted from king to chieftain. At the end of the Qin Dynasty, Wuzhu led troops from central Fujian northward to assist the vassal states in overthrowing Qin. During the Chu-Han contention, he supported Liu Bang in defeating Xiang Yu. In 202 BCE (the fifth year of Emperor Gaozu of Han) , Liu Bang bestowed upon Wuzhu the title of King of Minyue, officially establishing the Minyue Kingdom. Wuzhu then built the capital "Ye City" at the foot of Ye Mountain in Fuzhou, marking the beginning of Fuzhou's urban history.

## 二、闽越的灭亡 — The Fall of Minyue

### 中文

闽越国从无诸受封立国至灭亡，正统政权存续92年（公元前202年—公元前110年）。

内部矛盾与叛乱：闽越国后期与汉廷矛盾加深。余善是闽越王郢的弟弟。元鼎五年（公元前111年），汉朝发兵征讨南越，余善请求出兵相助，但行军途中借口海风太大拒绝前进，并暗中与南越联络。次年（公元前110年），余善得知汉军有进攻东越的意图，自称“东越武帝” ，刻皇帝之玺自立，发兵拒守要道，公开反叛汉朝。

汉朝平叛：汉武帝大怒，派横海将军韩说从句章出发由海路进攻，楼船将军杨仆、中尉王温舒等从多路同时进攻东越。在汉军压力下，闽越内部发生分裂——繇王居股与建成侯敖合谋杀死余善，率众向汉朝横海将军韩说投降。

国除与迁徙：汉武帝元封元年（公元前110年）冬，汉朝废除闽越国，将闽越族民悉数迁徙至江淮一带。少数闽越人藏匿于荒山野岭得以幸存。闽越国就此灭亡。

### English

From Wuzhu's enfeoffment to its fall, the legitimate Minyue regime lasted 92 years (202 BCE – 110 BCE).

Internal Conflict and Rebellion: In its later years, Minyue's conflicts with the Han court intensified. Yushan was the younger brother of King Ying of Minyue. In 111 BCE (the fifth year of the Yuanfeng era), the Han Dynasty launched a campaign against Nanyue. Yushan requested to send troops to assist, but on the way he used "strong winds" as an excuse to halt progress and secretly contacted Nanyue. The following year (110 BCE), upon learning that Han forces were planning to attack Dongyue, Yushan proclaimed himself "Emperor Wu of Dongyue" , had an imperial seal carved, and openly rebelled against the Han.

Han Suppression: Emperor Wu of Han was enraged and dispatched General Han Shuo to attack from the sea via Juzhang, while Louchuan General Yang Pu and others launched coordinated offensives from multiple routes. Under Han military pressure, internal divisions emerged within Minyue—King Jugu and the Marquis of Jiancheng Ao conspired to kill Yushan and surrendered to General Han Shuo.

Abolition and Relocation: In the winter of 110 BCE (the first year of the Yuanfeng era of Emperor Wu of Han) , the Han Dynasty abolished the Minyue Kingdom and forcibly relocated the Minyue people to the Jianghuai region (the area between the Yangtze and Huai Rivers). A small number of Minyue people managed to survive by hiding in remote mountains and forests. Thus, the Minyue Kingdom came to an end.

## 三、闽越的经济与贸易 — Economy and Trade of Minyue

### 中文

闽越国的经济主要有三大支柱：冶炼业、水稻种植、纺织业。

冶炼业：闽越立国后大兴冶炼业，推广铁器具。铁制农具有犁、锄、锸、镢等；铁制工具有斧、锤、凿、锯、环首刀等；兵器有矛、刀、剑、匕首、镞等，使用相当广泛。闽越国拥有十分发达的冶铁技术。福建松溪县有湛卢山，相传为欧冶子铸剑之地；福州冶山有欧冶池。

水稻种植：水稻是闽越主要的粮食作物。考古发现，福建东张遗址有新石器时代晚期的稻草痕迹，永春九兜山商周遗址的陶瓮内壁印有稻杆和谷壳痕迹，证明当地原始居民以稻作为主。

纺织业：考古发掘出土了大量陶制纺轮。武夷山白岩悬棺中发现大麻、苎麻、丝、木棉棉布四种质料的纺织品残片。纺织技术应是楚、吴、越纺织技术传入或相互交流中发展起来的。

其他产业：闽越国还有较为发达的建筑业、造船业、制陶业和交通业。闽越国与相邻诸国经济文化交流密切，南迁民众带来了先进的生产工具和科学技术。

### English

The economy of the Minyue Kingdom rested on three pillars: metallurgy, rice cultivation, and textiles.

Metallurgy: After establishing the kingdom, Minyue vigorously developed the smelting industry and promoted iron tools. Iron agricultural tools included plows, hoes, spades, and picks; iron tools included axes, hammers, chisels, saws, and ring-headed knives; weapons included spears, swords, daggers, and arrowheads—all widely used. Minyue possessed highly advanced iron-smelting technology. Mount Zhanlu in Songxi County, Fujian, is traditionally said to be the place where the legendary swordsmith Ou Yezi forged swords, and Ye Mountain in Fuzhou has the Ou Yezi Pool.

Rice Cultivation: Rice was the main staple crop of Minyue. Archaeological discoveries—including rice straw traces at the Dongzhang site in Fujian from the late Neolithic period and rice husk impressions on pottery from the Shang-Zhou site at Jiudoushan in Yongchun—confirm that the local indigenous people practiced rice farming.

Textiles: Numerous ceramic spinning wheels have been excavated. Textile fragments made of hemp, ramie, silk, and kapok were discovered in the cliff coffins at Baiyan in Wuyishan. The textile technology likely developed through the introduction of and exchange with Chu, Wu, and Yue techniques.

Other Industries: Minyue also had relatively developed construction, shipbuilding, pottery, and transportation industries. The kingdom maintained close economic and cultural exchanges with neighboring states, and the southward migrants brought advanced production tools and technologies.

## 四、闽越出名的人物 — Famous Figures of Minyue

### 中文

《史记·东越列传》着重刻画了两位对闽越国影响巨大的君王。

1. 无诸（Wuzhu）

无诸（约生于战国晚期，卒于公元前192年），姓驺氏，为越王勾践后裔。他是闽越国的开国君王，也是福建历史上有文字记载的第一位统治者。秦末率兵助诸侯灭秦，楚汉相争时助刘邦击败项羽。公元前202年被汉高祖封为闽越王，建都冶城。他积极吸收中原先进文化技术，发展生产，使闽越国的经济社会得到较大发展。无诸被后世尊奉为“开闽始祖”。

2. 余善（Yushan）

余善是闽越王郢的弟弟。公元前135年，郢发兵攻打南越，汉朝以此为借口入侵闽越。余善与大臣合谋杀死了郢，向汉朝投降。汉朝册封他为“东越王”。元鼎五年（公元前111年），余善在汉朝征讨南越时暗中背叛汉朝。次年（公元前110年），他自称“东越武帝” ，公开反汉。最终被繇王居股与建成侯敖合谋杀死，闽越国随之灭亡。余善是导致闽越亡国的关键人物。

3. 其他君主

其他重要君主包括：开国始祖驺玉（无疆长子，率众南迁入闽）；闽越王郢（公元前135年发兵攻打南越引发汉朝干预）；繇王丑（汉朝立为繇王管理闽越）；繇王居股（与敖合谋杀余善后降汉）。

### English

The "Records of the Grand Historian: Treatise on the Eastern Yue" highlights two kings who had a profound impact on the Minyue Kingdom.

1. Wuzhu

Wuzhu (born around the late Warring States period, died 192 BCE), surnamed Zou, was a descendant of King Goujian of Yue. He was the founding king of the Minyue Kingdom and the first ruler of Fujian recorded in written history. At the end of the Qin Dynasty, he led troops to help the vassal states overthrow Qin, and during the Chu-Han contention, he assisted Liu Bang in defeating Xiang Yu. In 202 BCE, Emperor Gaozu of Han enfeoffed him as King of Minyue, and he established his capital at Ye City. He actively adopted advanced Central Plains culture and technology to develop production, significantly advancing Minyue's economy and society. Wuzhu is revered by later generations as the "Founding Ancestor of Fujian".

2. Yushan

Yushan was the younger brother of King Ying of Minyue. In 135 BCE, Ying attacked Nanyue, prompting the Han to intervene. Yushan conspired with ministers to kill Ying and surrendered to Han. The Han court enfeoffed him as "King of Dongyue". In 111 BCE, while Han was campaigning against Nanyue, Yushan secretly betrayed Han. The following year (110 BCE), he proclaimed himself "Emperor Wu of Dongyue" and openly rebelled. He was ultimately killed in a conspiracy by King Jugu and the Marquis of Jiancheng Ao, leading to the fall of Minyue. Yushan was the pivotal figure responsible for the kingdom's downfall.

3. Other Rulers

Other notable rulers include: founding ancestor Zou Yu (eldest son of Wujiang, led the southward migration into Fujian); King Ying (attacked Nanyue in 135 BCE, triggering Han intervention); King Chou (established by Han to govern Minyue); and King Jugu (surrendered to Han after killing Yushan).

## 五、闽越语对闽南语的影响 — The Influence of Minyue Language on Minnan (Hokkien)

中文

闽越语是闽南语的底层语言之一。

语言渊源：古代闽越人所使用的闽越语与壮侗语系（Tai-Kadai languages）存在一定的血缘关系。战国后期越国灭亡后，越国王族退入福建建立闽越国，与当地原住民融合为闽越族，形成了闽越语。

汉语的传入与融合：公元前110年汉武帝灭闽越后，汉朝驻军多为江东的吴人和江西的楚人，他们将自己的母语——古吴语和古楚语（古湘语）带入了福建地区，融入了闽越语元素，最后形成了原始闽语。福建原住民的语言基底是由古闽越语、古吴语、古楚语等多源交融、层叠覆盖所构成的。

闽南语的形成：中原汉人大规模入闽后，汉语逐渐取代了闽越语。但在民族融合过程中，闽南语吸收了一些闽越语成分。泉州方言实际上是汉语与闽越语交融的结果。闽南语为汉藏语系的一支，是古代闽越语与汉人入闽带来的汉语的结合。闽南语中仍有古闽越语词汇成分的保留，这是语言学界的共识。受福建多山地形的阻隔和漫长时间的演变，闽方言逐渐分化出福州话、闽南话等次方言，而古闽越语的影响显而易见。

### English

The Minyue language is one of the substrate languages of Minnan (Hokkien) .

Linguistic Origins: The Minyue language used by the ancient Minyue people has a certain genetic relationship with the Tai-Kadai language family. After the fall of the Yue state in the late Warring States period, the Yue royal family retreated to Fujian and established the Minyue Kingdom, merging with the local indigenous people to form the Minyue ethnicity and the Minyue language.

Introduction and Fusion of Chinese: After Emperor Wu of Han destroyed Minyue in 110 BCE, the Han garrisons were mostly Wu people from Jiangdong and Chu people from Jiangxi. They brought their native languages—Old Wu and Old Chu (Old Xiang)—into Fujian, which integrated with Minyue elements and eventually formed Proto-Min Chinese. The linguistic substrate of Fujian's indigenous population was formed by the multi-source integration and layering of Old Minyue, Old Wu, and Old Chu.

Formation of Minnan: After large-scale Han Chinese migration into Fujian, Chinese gradually replaced the Minyue language. However, during the process of ethnic integration, Minnan absorbed some Minyue elements. The Quanzhou dialect is essentially the result of the fusion of Chinese and Minyue. Minnan is a branch of the Sino-Tibetan language family, representing a fusion of the ancient Minyue language and the Chinese brought by Han migrants. Linguists generally agree that Minyue lexical elements are preserved in various Min dialects. Due to Fujian's mountainous terrain and the passage of time, Min dialects gradually differentiated into sub-dialects such as Fuzhou dialect and Minnan, with the influence of Old Minyue being clearly evident.
