五代十國時期，福建省的疆域主要屬於**閩國** (909–945)。

#### 🗺️ 疆域與政權

閩國是“十國”之一，統治區域與今日**福建省大致相當**，由**王審知**於909年建立。其疆域在極盛時轄有**福、泉、建、汀、漳**五州，大致相當於今天的福州、泉州、南平、龍岩、漳州等地。閩國於945年被南唐所滅。

---

#### 📈 經濟與貿易

閩國的經濟以**海洋貿易**為核心，這是由其地理條件和時代背景共同決定的。

· **政策推動**：統治者積極招徠外商、鼓勵貿易。王審知在福州開闢甘棠港，設立類似海關的 **“榷貨務”**；其後更設立專門管理海外貿易的**“市舶司”**。

· **貿易路線與商品**：福州港是當時主要的外貿港口。航線北達朝鮮半島，南至越南、印尼，西達波斯灣和阿拉伯地區。出口商品以**陶瓷和鐵器**為主；進口則有**象牙**、**犀角**、**香藥**、**珍珠**等珍貴舶來品。

· **社會影響**：活躍的貿易帶來了**“百貨隨潮船入市，萬家沽酒戶垂簾”**的繁榮景象，加速了福建的開發，使其從“蠻荒之地”變為**“海濱鄒魯”**。

---

#### 💎 總結

總而言之，福建在五代十國時期**屬閩國**。這個沿海政權通過積極發展**海外貿易**累積了財富，促進了社會繁榮，其政策與實踐也為日後中國東南沿海的海洋貿易發展奠定了重要基礎。

### 📝 English Version

During the Five Dynasties and Ten Kingdoms period, the area of present-day **Fujian** province was primarily the territory of the **Min Kingdom** (闽国, 909–945 AD).

#### Territory and Regime
The Min was one of the "Ten Kingdoms", and its domain was roughly **coterminous with modern Fujian** province. It was founded by **Wang Shenzhi** in 909 AD. At its peak, it administered five prefectures: **Fu, Quan, Jian, Ting, and Zhang**. The kingdom was conquered by the **Southern Tang** in 945 AD.

#### Economy and Trade
The Min's economy was centered on **maritime trade**:

· **Pro-Trade Policies**: Rulers actively attracted foreign merchants. Wang Shenzhi opened **Gantang Port** and established a trade office called the **"Quehuowu"**; later, a dedicated **"Maritime Trade Supervisorate"** (Shibosi) was set up.

· **Routes and Goods**: Fuzhou Port was a major hub. Trade routes reached the Korean Peninsula, Vietnam, Indonesia, and as far as the Persian Gulf and Arabia. Major exports were **ceramics and ironware**, while imports included **ivory, rhino horn, spices, and pearls**.

· **Impact**: Thriving trade brought prosperity, accelerated Fujian's development, and transformed it into a culturally advanced region.

#### Summary
In summary, Fujian was part of the **Min Kingdom** during this period. This coastal state achieved prosperity through **maritime trade**, laying important groundwork for the maritime trade development of Southeast China in later dynasties.
