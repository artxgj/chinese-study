# 五代十国朝代名来源详解

## 一、总论 / Overview

“五代十国”（907年—979年）是中国历史上的一段大分裂时期。这一称谓出自北宋欧阳修所著的《新五代史》，是对五代（907年—960年）与十国（902年—979年）的合称。

“五代十国” _(Five Dynasties and Ten Kingdoms)_ refers to a period of great fragmentation in Chinese history (907–979). The term comes from _Ouyang Xiu’s New History of the Five Dynasties_ and is a collective name for the Five Dynasties (907–960) and the Ten Kingdoms (902–979).

五代指唐朝灭亡后在中原地区依次更替的五个政权——梁、唐、晋、汉、周。由于这些国号在前朝都曾被使用过，后世史家为便于区分，在国号前加“后”字，称为后梁、后唐、后晋、后汉、后周。

The Five Dynasties refer to the five regimes that succeeded one another in the Central Plains after the fall of the Tang Dynasty — Liang, Tang, Jin, Han, and Zhou. Since these dynastic names had all been used in earlier periods, later historians added the character “后” (Later) before each name to distinguish them, hence Later Liang, Later Tang, Later Jin, Later Han, and Later Zhou.

十国指五代之外同时或相继存在的十个地方割据政权，即吴、南唐、吴越、前蜀、后蜀、闽、南楚（楚）、南汉、南平（荆南）、北汉。

The Ten Kingdoms refer to ten regional separatist regimes that existed alongside or successively after the Five Dynasties, namely Wu, Southern Tang, Wuyue, Former Shu, Later Shu, Min, Southern Chu (Chu), Southern Han, Nanping (Jingnan), and Northern Han.

## 二、五代国号来源 / Origins of the Five Dynasties’ Names

### 1. 后梁（907—923年）/ Later Liang (907–923)

后梁为朱温所建。朱温原为黄巢部下，后叛降唐朝，被唐僖宗任命为宣武军节度使，以汴州（今河南开封）为根据地。汴州在战国时期为魏国国都大梁所在地，先秦有“以国都代国名”的习惯，故魏国亦称梁国。朱温因势力根基在故魏之地，被唐朝封为**梁王**，篡唐后便以**梁**为国号。

The Later Liang was founded by Zhu Wen. Zhu Wen was originally a general under Huang Chao, but later defected to the Tang court. He was appointed military governor of Xuanwu by Emperor Xizong of Tang, with Bianzhou (modern Kaifeng, Henan) as his base. Bianzhou was the location of Daliang, the capital of the State of Wei during the Warring States period. In pre-Qin times, there was a custom of “using the capital’s name to represent the state,” so Wei was also called Liang. Because Zhu Wen’s power base lay in the former Wei territory, he was enfeoffed as **Prince of Liang** by the Tang court and adopted **Liang** as his dynastic name after usurping the throne.

### 2. 后唐（923—936年）/ Later Tang (923–936)

后唐由沙陀族人李存勖建立。唐朝中期，沙陀族归附唐朝，其首领被赐**李姓**。黄巢起义时，沙陀首领李克用助唐收复长安有功，被封为河东节度使，后又进封**晋王**。朱温废唐建梁后，晋国视梁朝为伪朝，仍奉唐朝正朔。李克用之子李存勖灭梁后，以**唐**为国号，意在表明自己是唐朝正统的继承者。

The Later Tang was founded by Li Cunxu, who was of Shatuo ethnicity. In the mid-Tang period, the Shatuo tribe submitted to the Tang and its chieftains were granted the imperial surname Li. During the Huang Chao Rebellion, the Shatuo chieftain Li Keyong helped the Tang recover Chang’an and was made military governor of Hedong, later enfeoffed as Prince of Jin. After Zhu Wen deposed the Tang and established Liang, the State of Jin regarded Liang as illegitimate and continued to use the Tang calendar. After Li Cunxu (Li Keyong’s son) destroyed Liang, he adopted Tang as his dynastic name to proclaim himself the legitimate successor of the Tang Dynasty.

### 3. 后晋（936—947年）/ Later Jin (936–947)

后晋由沙陀族人石敬瑭建立。石敬瑭为后唐明宗李嗣源的女婿，曾任河东节度使。河东地区是唐末五代沙陀族势力的根据地，该地在历史上被称为**晋**。石敬瑭以**晋**为国号，既因其统治根基在晋地，也因“大唐”的政治影响力已微乎其微，而他本人又不姓李，不便再以唐为号。

The Later Jin was founded by Shi Jingtang, also of Shatuo ethnicity. Shi Jingtang was a son-in-law of Later Tang Emperor Mingzong (Li Siyuan) and served as military governor of Hedong. The Hedong region was the base of Shatuo power in the late Tang and Five Dynasties period, and was historically known as **Jin**. Shi Jingtang adopted **Jin** as his dynastic name both because his power base lay in the Jin region and because the political influence of the “Great Tang” had faded, while he himself did not bear the Li surname and thus could not conveniently claim the Tang name.

### 4. 后汉（947—951年）/ Later Han (947–951)

后汉由沙陀族人刘知远建立。刘知远家世寒微，既居汉地，便冒**汉姓刘氏**。为与历史上强盛的大汉王朝攀附关系、增强政权的正统性与号召力，刘知远以**汉**为国号。

The Later Han was founded by Liu Zhiyuan, also of Shatuo ethnicity. Liu Zhiyuan came from a humble background; living in Han Chinese territory, he adopted the **Han surname Liu**. To associate himself with the powerful Han Dynasty of history and strengthen his regime’s legitimacy and appeal, Liu Zhiyuan chose **Han** as his dynastic name.

### 5. 后周（951—960年）/ Later Zhou (951–960)

后周由后汉大将郭威建立。郭氏源出姬姓。西周时，周武王封其叔**虢仲**于西虢、**虢叔**于东虢。“虢”通“郭”，这两支虢国灭亡后，其裔孙便以**郭**为氏。郭威以**周**为国号，意在追溯远祖的姬周渊源。

The Later Zhou was founded by Guo Wei, a general of the Later Han. The Guo clan traced its origin to the Ji surname. In the Western Zhou period, King Wu of Zhou enfeoffed his uncles **Guo Zhong** at Western Guo and **Guo Shu** at Eastern Guo. The character “虢” (Guo) is interchangeable with “郭” (Guo); after these two Guo states were destroyed, their descendants adopted **Guo (郭)** as their surname. Guo Wei adopted **Zhou** as his dynastic name to trace his lineage back to the Ji-surnamed Zhou Dynasty.

## 三、十国国号来源 / Origins of the Ten Kingdoms’ Names

### 1. 吴（902—937年）/ Wu (902–937)

吴国为杨行密所建，又称杨吴、南吴。唐昭宗天复二年（902年），唐朝封杨行密为**吴王**，政权初具雏形，国号**吴**由此而来。919年，杨隆演称吴国王，正式建立吴国。

The Wu Kingdom was founded by Yang Xingmi, also known as Yang Wu or Southern Wu. In 902, the Tang court enfeoffed Yang Xingmi as **Prince of Wu**, marking the initial formation of the regime — hence the dynastic name **Wu**. In 919, Yang Longyan proclaimed himself King of Wu, formally establishing the kingdom.

### 2. 南唐（937—975年）/ Southern Tang (937–975)

南唐由徐知诰（后改名李昪）建立。937年，徐知诰代吴称帝，初改国号为齐。后为附会唐朝、增强正统性，徐知诰恢复李姓，自称是唐宗室后裔，改国号为**唐**，史称南唐。

The Southern Tang was founded by Xu Zhigao (later renamed Li Bian). In 937, Xu Zhigao deposed the Wu emperor and initially adopted Qi as his dynastic name. Later, to associate himself with the Tang Dynasty and enhance his legitimacy, Xu Zhigao resumed the surname Li, claimed descent from the Tang imperial house, and changed the dynastic name to **Tang** — hence Southern Tang in history.

### 3. 吴越（907—978年）/ Wuyue (907–978)

吴越由钱镠建立。唐昭宗天复二年（902年），唐朝封钱镠为**越王**；天祐元年（904年），钱镠请改封为**吴越王**。国号**吴越**即由此而来。吴越又称义忠国。

Wuyue was founded by Qian Liu. In 902, the Tang court enfeoffed Qian Liu as **Prince of Yue**; in 904, Qian Liu requested and received the title **Prince of Wuyue**. The dynastic name **Wuyue** derives from this title. Wuyue was also known as Yizhong State.

### 4. 前蜀（907—925年）/ Former Shu (907–925)

前蜀由王建建立。907年朱温篡唐建后梁，王建不承认后梁正统，在成都自立为帝，国号**大蜀**，史称前蜀。917年王建曾改国号为“大汉”，次年又恢复为蜀。“前”字为史家所加，以区别于后来孟知祥建立的后蜀。

The Former Shu was founded by Wang Jian. In 907, when Zhu Wen usurped the Tang and established Later Liang, Wang Jian refused to recognize Later Liang’s legitimacy and declared himself emperor in Chengdu with the dynastic name Great Shu — hence Former Shu in history. Wang Jian briefly changed the name to “Great Han” in 917 but reverted to Shu the following year. The character “前” (Former) was added by later historians to distinguish it from the Later Shu founded by Meng Zhixiang.

### 5. 后蜀（934—965年）/ Later Shu (934–965)

后蜀由孟知祥建立。孟知祥原为后唐西川节度使，后唐应顺元年（934年），孟知祥在成都称帝，国号**蜀**，史称后蜀。“后”字为史家所加，以区别于王建的前蜀。

The Later Shu was founded by Meng Zhixiang. Meng Zhixiang was originally the Later Tang military governor of Xichuan. In 934, he declared himself emperor in Chengdu with the dynastic name **Shu** — hence Later Shu in history. The character “后” (Later) was added by historians to distinguish it from Wang Jian’s Former Shu.

### 6. 闽（909—945年）/ Min (909–945)

闽国由王审知建立。909年，后梁封王审知为**闽王**。926年，王延翰自称“大闽国王”，正式建立闽国。933年王延钧称帝，国号大闽。国号**闽**来源于福建地区的古称——无诸时期的闽越国。

The Min Kingdom was founded by Wang Shenzhi. In 909, the Later Liang enfeoffed Wang Shenzhi as **Prince of Min**. In 926, Wang Yanhan proclaimed himself “Great King of Min,” formally establishing the Min Kingdom. In 933, Wang Yanjun declared himself emperor with the dynastic name **Great Min**. The name **Min** comes from the ancient name of the Fujian region — the Minyue Kingdom from the Wuzhu period.

### 7. 南楚（907—951年）/ Southern Chu (907–951)

南楚由马殷建立，又称马楚、楚。马殷被后梁封为**楚王**，以**楚**为国号。国号**楚**源自先秦时期的楚国，是战国七雄之一。史称南楚或马楚，以区别于先秦的楚国。

The Southern Chu was founded by Ma Yin, also known as Ma Chu or simply Chu. Ma Yin was enfeoffed as **Prince of Chu** by the Later Liang and adopted **Chu** as his dynastic name. The name **Chu** derives from the ancient State of Chu of the pre-Qin period, one of the Seven Warring States. It is called Southern Chu or Ma Chu in history to distinguish it from the pre-Qin Chu state.

### 8. 南汉（917—971年）/ Southern Han (917–971)

南汉由刘岩（刘䶮）建立。917年，刘岩在番禺（今广州）称帝，初国号为**大越**。次年，刘岩以**汉朝刘氏后裔**的身份，改国号为**汉**，史称南汉。

The Southern Han was founded by Liu Yan (Liu Yan). In 917, Liu Yan declared himself emperor in Panyu (modern Guangzhou) with the initial dynastic name **Great Yue**. The following year, claiming descent from the **Liu imperial house of the Han Dynasty**, Liu Yan changed the dynastic name to **Han** — hence Southern Han in history.

### 9. 南平（924—963年）/ Nanping (924–963)

南平由高季兴建立，又称荆南。高季兴原为后梁荆南节度使。后唐同光二年（924年），高季兴受后唐封为**南平王**，建都荆州，史称南平。又因其方镇名为荆南，故亦称荆南。

Nanping was founded by Gao Jixing, also known as Jingnan. Gao Jixing was originally the Later Liang military governor of Jingnan. In 924, Gao Jixing was enfeoffed as **Prince of Nanping** by the Later Tang and established his capital in Jingzhou — hence Nanping in history. It is also called Jingnan after his military commandery.

### 10. 北汉（951—979年）/ Northern Han (951–979)

北汉由刘崇建立。951年，后汉被郭威篡夺，改国号为周（后周）。刘崇是后汉高祖刘知远的弟弟，据河东十二州在太原称帝，**沿用后汉的国号**，史称北汉。北汉是十国中唯一位于北方的政权。

The Northern Han was founded by Liu Chong. In 951, the Later Han was usurped by Guo Wei, who changed the dynastic name to Zhou (Later Zhou). Liu Chong, the younger brother of Later Han founder Liu Zhiyuan, controlled the twelve prefectures of Hedong and declared himself emperor in Taiyuan, **continuing to use the Han dynastic name** — hence Northern Han in history. Northern Han was the only one of the Ten Kingdoms located in the north.

## 四、总结 / Summary

五代十国的朝代名来源大致可归纳为以下几类：

The origins of the dynastic names of the Five Dynasties and Ten Kingdoms can be broadly summarized into the following categories:

**一、沿用前朝国号以争取正统** — 后唐（继承唐朝）、后汉（攀附汉朝）、南唐（附会唐朝）、南汉（攀附汉朝）、北汉（延续后汉）。

**1\. Adopting previous dynastic names to claim legitimacy** — Later Tang (inheriting Tang), Later Han (associating with Han), Southern Tang (connecting to Tang), Southern Han (connecting to Han), Northern Han (continuing Later Han).

**二、源于封爵或封地** — 后梁（梁王）、后晋（晋王/晋地）、吴（吴王）、吴越（吴越王）、闽（闽王）、楚（楚王）、南平/荆南（南平王/荆南节度使）。

**2\. Deriving from titles or enfeoffed territories** — Later Liang (Prince of Liang), Later Jin (Prince of Jin/Jin region), Wu (Prince of Wu), Wuyue (Prince of Wuyue), Min (Prince of Min), Chu (Prince of Chu), Nanping/Jingnan (Prince of Nanping/military governor of Jingnan).

**三、源于地理名称** — 前蜀、后蜀（蜀地，即今四川地区）。

**3\. Deriving from geographical names** — Former Shu and Later Shu (the Shu region, i.e., modern Sichuan area).

**四、源于姓氏或族源追溯** — 后周（郭氏追溯姬周渊源）。

**4\. Deriving from surname or ancestral origins** — Later Zhou (the Guo clan tracing its origins to the Ji-surnamed Zhou Dynasty).

**五、史家为区分而加前缀** — 五代加“后”字（后梁、后唐、后晋、后汉、后周）；十国加“前”“后”“南”“北”等字以区分同名政权（前蜀/后蜀、南唐/后唐、南汉/北汉等）。

**5\. Prefixes added by historians for distinction** — The Five Dynasties add “后” (Later): Later Liang, Later Tang, Later Jin, Later Han, Later Zhou; the Ten Kingdoms add “前” (Former), “后” (Later), “南” (Southern), “北” (Northern) etc. to distinguish regimes sharing the same name: Former Shu/Later Shu, Southern Tang/Later Tang, Southern Han/Northern Han, etc.
