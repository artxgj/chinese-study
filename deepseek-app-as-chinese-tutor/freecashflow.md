# Breakdown of Free Cash Flow's importance and its relationships with key financial terms.

---

## The Importance of Free Cash Flow (FCF) | 自由现金流的重要性##

**English**: FCF is the true measure of a company's profitability. Unlike accounting profits (net income), FCF represents the actual cash generated after maintaining or expanding the asset base. It is the "owner's earnings"—the cash available to pay dividends, repay debt, buy back shares, or fund growth without external financing. A positive FCF indicates financial health and flexibility; a negative FCF can signal trouble, even if the company reports accounting profits.

**中文**： FCF是衡量企业真实盈利能力的黄金标准。与会计利润（净利润）不同，FCF代表企业在维持或扩大资产基础后实际产生的现金。它是“所有者收益”——可用于分红、还债、回购股票或投资增长而无需外部融资的现金。正向FCF代表财务健康和灵活性；负向FCF即使账面上盈利，也可能预示风险。

## Relationships with FCF | FCF与这些术语的关系

### FCF & Working Capital (WC) | 营运资本

**Formula (公式)**: FCF = Operating Cash Flow – CAPEX, where **Operating Cash Flow** = Net Income + Non-cash charges – **Increase in WC**.

**English**: An increase in Working Capital (e.g., building inventory or giving more credit to customers) is a cash outflow, which reduces FCF. A decrease in WC frees up cash, increasing FCF. Efficient WC management is key to boosting FCF.

**中文**： 营运资本增加（如增加库存或放宽客户账期）属于现金流出，会减少FCF。营运资本减少则释放现金，增加FCF。高效的营运资本管理是提升FCF的关键。

### FCF & CAPEX | 资本性支出

**English**: CAPEX is the direct subtraction in the FCF formula. Maintenance CAPEX (keeping assets running) is mandatory and reduces FCF. Growth CAPEX (expanding capacity) also reduces current FCF but is expected to generate higher future FCF. High FCF with minimal CAPEX often indicates a "cash cow."

**中文**： CAPEX是FCF公式中的直接减项。维护性CAPEX（维持运转）属强制支出，会减少FCF。增长性CAPEX（扩产）虽减少当期FCF，但预期能创造更高的未来FCF。高FCF且低CAPEX通常代表“现金牛”企业。

### FCF vs. General Cash Flow (CF) | 现金流总览

**English**: CF is the umbrella term (all three activities). FCF is a subset focusing only on Operating CF minus Investing CF (specifically CAPEX). FCF isolates discretionary cash, whereas total CF includes financing flows (debt/equity issuances), which are not generated by business operations.

**中文**： CF是总称（含三大活动）。FCF是子集，仅关注经营现金流减去投资现金流（特指CAPEX）。FCF隔离出可自由支配的现金，而总CF包含筹资性流入（发债/发股），这些并非业务本身产生的。

### FCF, DCF & WACC (The Valuation Trio) | 估值三要素

**English**: DCF valuation projects future FCFs over several years. These projected FCFs are then discounted back to today’s value using the WACC as the discount rate.
- Higher FCF → Higher intrinsic value.
- Higher WACC (higher risk/cost) → Lower present value (even if FCF is stable).
    
  In essence, FCF is the numerator (cash), WACC is the denominator (risk), and DCF is the final result (value).

**中文**： DCF估值法预测未来数年的FCF，然后用WACC作为折现率，将这些预测FCF折现回现值。
- FCF越高 → 内在价值越高。
- WACC越高（风险/成本越高）→ 现值越低（即使FCF稳定）。
    
  本质上，FCF是分子（现金），WACC是分母（风险），DCF是最终结果（价值）。

---

## Summary Formula | 总结公式链

FCF = (Net Income + Depreciation - ΔWC) - CAPEX

DCF Value = Σ (FCF_t / (1 + WACC)^t)
