## 閩越的創立與滅亡 (The Founding and Fall of Minyue)

### 創立 (Founding)

- 背景 (Background)：閩越的建立與春秋戰國時期越國的滅亡直接相關。公元前334年，越國被楚國所滅。
- 建國 (Establishment)：越國滅亡後，越王族後裔（如無諸的祖先）航海入閩，與當地原住民「七閩」融合，建立了閩越國。
- 受封漢朝 (Han Dynasty Investiture)：秦朝統一後，閩越王被降為「君長」。秦末，閩越王無諸率兵助劉邦擊敗項羽。公元前202年，漢高祖劉邦正式封無諸為閩越王，定都東冶（今福州）。

### 滅亡 (Fall)

- 內部分裂與對抗 (Internal Strife and Conflict)：無諸死後，其子孫與漢廷摩擦加劇。西元前135年，閩越王郢攻打南越，引發漢朝干預。郢被其弟余善所殺，余善隨後被漢朝冊封為「東越王」。
- 余善反漢與亡國 (Yushan's Rebellion and Fall)：西元前111年，余善得知漢朝有攻伐之意，遂自稱「東越武帝」，發兵反漢。漢武帝派大軍從海陸多路進攻。西元前110年，閩越內部發生政變，余善被殺，向漢軍投降。
- 結局 (Aftermath)：閩越國滅亡，存在約92年（前202-前110年）。漢武帝將大部分閩越人強行遷徙至江淮地區。

## 經濟貿易情況 (Economy and Trade)

閩越國的經濟相當發達，主要體現在以下幾個方面：

- 冶鐵業 (Iron Smelting)：閩越國大興冶煉業，鐵製工具（犁、鋤、斧等）和武器廣泛使用，技術先進。
- 農業 (Agriculture)：以水稻種植為主，輔以畜牧、採集和漁獵。
- 手工業 (Handicrafts)：紡織業（使用大麻、絲等）、造船業、製陶業和建築業均相當發達。
- 貿易 (Trade)：閩越與周邊吳、越、楚等國有密切的經濟文化交流，先進技術隨之傳入。

## 出名人物 (Famous Figures)

- 無諸 (Wuzhu)：閩越國的開國君王，越王勾踐的後裔。他協助劉邦建立漢朝，於公元前202年被封為閩越王。他積極引進中原文化，發展經濟，被後世尊為「開閩始祖」。
- 余善 (Yushan)：閩越國的末代君主，無諸的後裔。他在殺兄（閩越王郢）後被漢朝封為「東越王」，最終於公元前111年反漢，導致閩越國在次年（前110年）滅亡。

## 閩越語對閩南語的影響 (Influence of Minyue Language on Minnan)

- 語言底層 (Linguistic Substratum)：閩南語的形成並非憑空而來，其底層包含了古閩越語的成分。在漢人大量入閩前，當地通行閩越語。
- 融合過程 (Fusion Process)：漢武帝滅閩越後，大量漢人（尤其是吳、楚地區的軍民）進入福建，帶來了古漢語（古吳語、古楚語）。這些語言與當地殘留的閩越語交融，最終形成了最早的「原始閩語」。
- 詞彙遺留 (Lexical Remnants)：雖然現代閩南語的主體是古漢語，但語言學界普遍認為其中仍保留了一些閩越語的詞彙，閩南語是漢語與閩越語交融的結果。

Here is a detailed bilingual (Chinese-English) explanation of the history of Minyue.

## The Founding and Fall of Minyue

### Founding

- Background: The establishment of Minyue is directly linked to the fall of the Yue state during the Warring States period. In 334 BC, Yue was conquered by the state of Chu.
- Establishment: After Yue's fall, members of the Yue royal family (ancestors of Wuzhu) fled by sea to Fujian and merged with the local "Seven Min" tribes to found the Minyue kingdom.
- Han Dynasty Investiture: After the Qin unification, the Minyue king was demoted to "Lord". At the end of the Qin dynasty, King Wuzhu of Minyue led his troops to help Liu Bang defeat Xiang Yu. In 202 BC, Emperor Gaozu of Han officially enfeoffed Wuzhu as the King of Minyue, with its capital at Dongye (modern-day Fuzhou).

### Fall

- Internal Strife and Conflict: After Wuzhu's death, friction between his descendants and the Han court escalated. In 135 BC, Minyue King Ying attacked Nanyue, prompting Han intervention. Ying was killed by his brother Yushan, who was subsequently enfeoffed by the Han as the "King of Dongyue".
- Yushan's Rebellion and Fall: In 111 BC, Yushan, learning of a planned Han attack, declared himself "Emperor of Dongyue" and rebelled. Emperor Wu of Han dispatched a large force to attack from multiple routes. In 110 BC, a coup occurred within Minyue; Yushan was killed and surrendered to Han forces.
- Aftermath: The Minyue kingdom fell after approximately 92 years (202-110 BC). Emperor Wu forcibly relocated most of the Minyue people to the Jianghuai region.

### Economy and Trade

The economy of Minyue was quite developed, mainly in the following areas:

- Iron Smelting: Minyue vigorously developed its iron industry. Iron tools (plows, hoes, axes, etc.) and weapons were widely used, indicating advanced technology.
- Agriculture: The primary crop was rice, supplemented by animal husbandry, gathering, and fishing.
- Handicrafts: Textile (using hemp, silk, etc.), shipbuilding, pottery, and construction industries were quite advanced.
- Trade: Minyue had close economic and cultural exchanges with neighboring states like Wu, Yue, and Chu, facilitating the introduction of advanced technology.

### Famous Figures

- Wuzhu: The founding king of Minyue, a descendant of King Goujian of Yue. He assisted Liu Bang in establishing the Han dynasty and was enfeoffed as the King of Minyue in 202 BC. He actively introduced Central Plains culture and developed the economy, revered by later generations as the "Ancestor of the Min".
- Yushan: The last king of Minyue, a descendant of Wuzhu. After killing his brother (King Ying), he was enfeoffed by the Han as the "King of Dongyue". He ultimately rebelled against the Han in 111 BC, leading to the kingdom's fall the following year (110 BC).

### Influence of Minyue Language on Minnan

- Linguistic Substratum: The formation of Minnan (Southern Min) did not occur in a vacuum; it has a substratum that includes elements of Ancient Minyue. Before the large-scale Han migration, Minyue was the local language.
- Fusion Process: After Emperor Wu of Han conquered Minyue, a large number of Han people (especially soldiers and civilians from Wu and Chu regions) entered Fujian, bringing Old Chinese (Old Wu and Old Chu dialects). These languages merged with the residual Minyue language, eventually forming the earliest "Proto-Min" language.
- Lexical Remnants: Although the main body of modern Minnan is Old Chinese, linguists generally agree that it retains some vocabulary from Minyue. Minnan is considered a result of the fusion between Chinese and Minyue.
