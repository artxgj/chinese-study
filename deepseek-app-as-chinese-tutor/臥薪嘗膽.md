## 二、故事背景 | Story Background

### 中文

春秋時期（西元前770—476年），吳國和越國是南方兩個相鄰的諸侯國，兩國之間長期不和，衝突不斷。

西元前496年，越王勾踐剛剛即位，吳王闔閭趁機派兵攻打越國。然而，闔閭在檇李（今浙江嘉興西南）一戰中被越軍打得大敗，身受重傷。闔閭回到吳國後不久便傷重去世。

### English

During the Spring and Autumn Period (770–476 BC), the State of Wu and the State of Yue were two neighboring vassal states in the south. The two states had long been at odds, with constant conflicts between them.

In 496 BC, King Goujian of Yue had just ascended the throne when King Helü of Wu seized the opportunity to launch an attack against Yue. However, Helü was badly defeated by the Yue army at the Battle of Zuili (southwest of present-day Jiaxing, Zhejiang) and was severely wounded. Soon after returning to Wu, Helü died from his injuries.

## 三、夫差立志復仇 | Fuchai Swears Revenge

### 中文

闔閭臨終前，囑咐兒子夫差一定要為他報仇，消滅越國。夫差牢記父親的遺言，即位後立志復仇。

為了激勵自己，夫差每天讓侍從站在庭院中，每當他經過時，侍從就大聲提醒他：「夫差，你忘了越王殺死你父親的仇恨嗎？」夫差則流著淚回答：「不敢忘記！」

夫差嚴格操練軍隊，把吳軍訓練成了一支極具戰鬥力的精銳之師。

### English

On his deathbed, Helü instructed his son Fuchai to avenge his death and destroy the State of Yue. Fuchai kept his father's last words firmly in mind and, after ascending the throne, was determined to seek revenge.

To spur himself on, Fuchai ordered his attendants to stand in the courtyard every day. Whenever he passed by, they would loudly remind him: "Fuchai, have you forgotten the hatred of your father being killed by the King of Yue?" Fuchai would then answer with tears: "I dare not forget!"

Fuchai drilled his army rigorously, turning the Wu forces into a highly effective and elite fighting force.

---

## 四、吳國大敗越國，勾踐被俘 | Wu Crushes Yue; Goujian Captured

### 中文

三年後，西元前494年，夫差率領大軍攻打越國。越王勾踐不聽大夫范蠡的忠告，貿然出兵迎戰。結果越軍在夫椒之戰中慘敗。

勾踐率領殘兵五千人退守會稽山（今浙江紹興），被吳軍團團包圍。在危亡關頭，勾踐採納了大夫范蠡和文种的建議，派文種前往吳軍大營求和。

### English

Three years later, in 494 BC, Fuchai led a massive army to attack the State of Yue. King Goujian of Yue, ignoring the advice of his minister Fan Li, rashly sent his troops to meet the enemy. The Yue army suffered a crushing defeat at the Battle of Fujiao.

Goujian retreated to Mount Kuaiji (present-day Shaoxing, Zhejiang) with only 5,000 remaining soldiers, where they were completely surrounded by the Wu army. At this critical moment of survival, Goujian heeded the advice of his ministers Fan Li and Wen Zhong, sending Wen Zhong to the Wu camp to sue for peace.

---

## 五、忍辱負重，勾踐入吳為奴 | Enduring Humiliation: Goujian Becomes a Slave in Wu

### 中文

吳王夫差原本不願接受求和，但在越國送上大量財寶和美女的賄賂下，加上越國表示願意臣服，夫差最終同意了。然而，吳國大臣伍子胥極力反對，勸夫差趁機滅掉越國，但夫差沒有採納。

根據和議，勾踐將國家大事託付給大夫文種處理，自己則帶著夫人和大夫范蠡前往吳國，淪為人質。夫差為了羞辱勾踐，讓他在父親闔閭的墓旁住在一間破舊的石屋裡，每天為吳王看墓、餵馬。

勾踐表面上對夫差恭順服從，像一頭溫順的羔羊，但心中從未忘記國恥家仇。

### English

King Fuchai of Wu was initially unwilling to accept the surrender, but after Yue offered large amounts of treasures and beautiful women as bribes, and promised to submit to Wu, Fuchai eventually agreed. However, the Wu minister Wu Zixu strongly opposed this and urged Fuchai to destroy Yue while he had the chance, but Fuchai did not take his advice.

Under the peace agreement, Goujian entrusted state affairs to Minister Wen Zhong and went to Wu himself, along with his wife and Minister Fan Li, as a hostage. To humiliate Goujian, Fuchai had him live in a shabby stone house beside his father Helü's tomb, where Goujian was ordered to guard the tomb and raise horses.

Outwardly, Goujian appeared obedient and submissive to Fuchai, like a tame lamb, but in his heart he never forgot the humiliation of his country and his people.

---

## 六、臥薪嘗膽 | Sleeping on Brushwood and Tasting Gall

### 中文

幾年後，夫差認為勾踐已經徹底順服，對他不再有威脅，便釋放他返回越國。

勾踐回到越國後，拒絕住進原來的王宮。為了時刻提醒自己不忘國恥和復仇的決心，他每天睡在柴草上（臥薪），並在屋中懸掛一隻苦膽。每天吃飯前和臨睡前，他都要親口品嘗苦膽的苦味（嘗膽）。

《史記·越王勾踐世家》記載：「越王勾踐反國，乃苦身焦思，置膽於坐，坐臥即仰膽，飲食亦嘗膽也。」

### English

After several years, Fuchai came to believe that Goujian had been completely subdued and was no longer a threat, so he released him and allowed him to return to Yue.

After returning to Yue, Goujian refused to live in his former palace. To constantly remind himself of his national humiliation and his determination for revenge, he slept on brushwood every night (sleeping on brushwood) and hung a gall-bladder in his hut. Before every meal and before going to bed each night, he would taste the bitterness of the gall-bladder (tasting gall).

The Records of the Grand Historian – House of King Goujian of Yue records: "After King Goujian of Yue returned to his state, he subjected himself to hardship and deep thought. He placed a gall-bladder at his seat, and whether sitting or lying down, he would look up and taste it, and he would also taste it when eating and drinking."

## 七、發奮圖強，復國滅吳 | Striving for Revival and Destroying Wu

### 中文

勾踐回國後，採取了一系列富國強兵的措施：

1\. **親自耕作**：勾踐親自下田耕種，他的夫人親自織布，過著簡樸的生活。

2\. **禮賢下士**：他廣納賢才，重用范蠡和文種等能臣。

3\. **發展農業**：大力發展農業生產，增強國力。

4\. **教育民眾**：加強對民眾的教育和訓練。

5\. **秘密備戰**：暗中聚集軍隊，積極準備復仇。

6\. **麻痺敵人**：不斷向夫差進獻貴重禮物，麻痺吳王的警惕。

經過十年的臥薪嘗膽、勵精圖治，越國國力日益強大。

### English

After returning to his state, Goujian implemented a series of measures to strengthen his country and military:

1\. **Personal farming**: Goujian personally worked in the fields, and his wife wove cloth herself, living a simple and frugal life.

2\. **Respecting the wise**: He welcomed talented people and appointed capable ministers like Fan Li and Wen Zhong.

3\. **Developing agriculture**: He vigorously developed agricultural production to enhance national strength.

4\. **Educating the people**: He strengthened education and training for his people.

5\. **Secret military preparation**: He secretly accumulated military forces and actively prepared for revenge.

6\. **Lulling the enemy**: He continuously sent luxurious gifts to Fuchai to dull the Wu king's vigilance.

After ten years of sleeping on brushwood and tasting gall, and governing with great effort, the State of Yue grew increasingly powerful.

---

## 八、一舉滅吳 | The Final Destruction of Wu

### 中文

西元前473年，勾踐看準時機，親自率領大軍攻打吳國。此時的吳國因夫差驕奢淫逸、連年征戰而國力空虛，加上伍子胥早已被夫差賜死，吳國再無能人可抵擋越軍。

越軍一舉攻破吳國都城，夫差兵敗自殺，吳國滅亡。勾踐終於完成了復仇大業，成為春秋時期的最後一位霸主。

### English

In 473 BC, Goujian seized the right opportunity and personally led a large army to attack the State of Wu. By this time, Wu had been weakened by Fuchai's extravagant lifestyle and years of continuous warfare. Moreover, Wu Zixu had long been forced to commit suicide by Fuchai, leaving Wu with no capable leader to resist the Yue forces.

The Yue army breached the Wu capital in one swift attack. Fuchai was defeated and committed suicide, and the State of Wu was destroyed. Goujian finally accomplished his revenge and became the last hegemon of the Spring and Autumn Period.

## 九、成語釋義與用法 | Idiom Meaning and Usage

### 中文

「臥薪嘗膽」後來演變為成語，用來形容人**刻苦自勵、發奮圖強，立志雪恥報仇或實現遠大目標**。

**近義詞**：發憤圖強、宵衣旰食
**反義詞**：樂不思蜀
**褒貶**：含褒義

### English

"Sleep on brushwood and taste gall" later became an idiom used to describe a person **who undergoes self-imposed hardships to strengthen one's resolve, striving diligently with determination to achieve vengeance or realize an ambitious goal**.

**Synonyms**: Strive for strength, work tirelessly
**Antonyms**: Indulge in pleasure and forget one's home
**Connotation**: Positive / commendatory

## 十、《史記》原文 | Original Text from Records of the Grand Historian

### 原文（文言文）

「越王勾踐反國，乃苦身焦思，置膽於坐，坐臥即仰膽，飲食亦嘗膽也。」

### 白話翻譯

越王勾踐回到自己的國家後，便使自己身體勞苦，心中焦慮思索。他把一個苦膽放在座位旁邊，無論坐著還是躺著，都要抬頭嘗一嘗苦膽；吃飯和喝水的時候，也要嘗一嘗苦膽。

### Original Text (Classical Chinese)

"After King Goujian of Yue returned to his state, he subjected himself to hardship and deep thought. He placed a gall-bladder at his seat, and whether sitting or lying down, he would look up and taste it, and he would also taste it when eating and drinking."

### Vernacular Translation

After King Goujian of Yue returned to his own state, he made his body endure hardship and his mind endure anxious thought. He placed a bitter gall-bladder beside his seat. Whether sitting or lying down, he would raise his head and taste the gall; and he would also taste it when eating and drinking.

---

> **備註**：據考證，《史記》原文僅記載勾踐「嘗膽」（品嘗苦膽），並未明確記載「臥薪」（睡在柴草上）。「臥薪」的細節是後世在流傳過程中逐漸增添的，但兩者結合後更生動地體現了勾踐刻苦自勵的精神，成為這個成語的完整意象。
