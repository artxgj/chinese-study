# 闽国（909年—945年）完整详解

# The Min Kingdom (909–945): A Complete Detailed Overview

---

## 一、历史 / History

### 1.1 政权建立 / Founding of the Regime

闽国是五代十国之一，由王潮、王审知兄弟在唐末率光州固始（今河南固始）移民入闽后建立。王氏兄弟先攻取汀州、漳州、泉州，继而夺取福州，控制了福建全境。

The Min Kingdom was one of the Ten Kingdoms during the Five Dynasties and Ten Kingdoms period. It was established after brothers Wang Chao and Wang Shenzhi led immigrants from Gushi, Guangzhou (modern-day Gushi, Henan) into Fujian in the late Tang dynasty. The Wang brothers first captured Tingzhou, Zhangzhou, and Quanzhou, then took Fuzhou, gaining control over the entire Fujian region.

### 1.2 统治历程 / Reign History

闽国共历六主，享国三十七年（909—945）。若自893年王潮攻占福州算起，王氏统治福建共52年。

The Min Kingdom had six rulers and lasted thirty-seven years (909–945). If counting from 893 when Wang Chao captured Fuzhou, the Wang family ruled Fujian for a total of 52 years.

历代君主 / Successive Rulers:


&bull; **王审知 (909–925)**：闽太祖，开国明君，推行休养生息政策

&bull; **王延翰 (925–927)**：闽嗣王，926年称“大闽国王”

&bull; **王延钧 (927–935)**：闽惠宗，933年称帝

&bull; **王继鹏 (936–939)**：闽康宗

&bull; **王延羲 (939–944)**：闽景宗

&bull; **王延政 (943–945)**：闽天德帝，943年另立“殷国”

王审知在位期间，通过招引贤才、办学兴教、减刑省役、降税利农、睦邻通商等政策，使闽地出现了政治安定、经济繁荣的局面。然而王审知去世后，闽国政变内乱不断，最终于945年被南唐攻灭。

During Wang Shenzhi's reign, through policies such as attracting talented people, establishing schools, reducing punishments and corvée, lowering taxes to benefit agriculture, and maintaining friendly relations for trade, the Min territory enjoyed political stability and economic prosperity. However, after Wang Shenzhi's death, the kingdom was plagued by constant coups and internal strife, and was eventually conquered by the Southern Tang in 945.

## 二、疆域 / Territory

### 2.1 疆域范围 / Territorial Extent

闽国的统治区域与今福建省大致相当。基本领域为唐代福建的五州，即**福州、建州、泉州、汀州、漳州**。

The Min Kingdom's territory roughly corresponded to modern-day Fujian Province. Its basic domain comprised the five prefectures of Tang-dynasty Fujian: **Fuzhou, Jianzhou, Quanzhou, Tingzhou, and Zhangzhou**.

### 2.2 都城 / Capitals

闽国先后定都于：

The Min Kingdom had successive capitals at:


&bull; **长乐府（今福建福州）**：909—945年间为主要都城

&bull; **建州（今福建建瓯）**：945年为都城

&bull; **Changle Prefecture (modern-day Fuzhou, Fujian)**：the primary capital from 909 to 945

&bull; **Jianzhou (modern-day Jian'ou, Fujian)**：the capital in 945

### 2.3 地理特征 / Geographic Features

闽国地处东南沿海，境内多山，相对封闭和崎岖。沿海地区拥有几个对外贸易港口，主要集中在福州与泉州境内。其疆域为后世福建的行政区划奠定了框架。

The Min Kingdom was located on the southeastern coast, with a mountainous and relatively isolated terrain. The coastal areas had several foreign trade ports, mainly concentrated in Fuzhou and Quanzhou. Its territory laid the framework for the later administrative divisions of Fujian.

## 三、经济 / Economy

### 3.1 农业 / Agriculture

王审知非常重视农业生产，委派官吏“出巡州县，劝课农桑”，把因战乱流亡的农民招抚回乡，鼓励开荒造田。

Wang Shenzhi placed great emphasis on agricultural production, dispatching officials "to tour prefectures and counties, encouraging farming and sericulture," and recruiting displaced peasants to return home while encouraging land reclamation.

#### 农业成就 / Agricultural Achievements:


&bull; **耕地面积**：据《淳熙三山志》记载，当时垦田达14,140顷16亩有余

&bull; **赋税政策**：实行“什一”之税，“敛不加暴”，轻徭薄赋

&bull; **作物推广**：鼓励栽种茶树，大量生产出口茶叶；推广双季稻

&bull; **水利工程**：围海造田，扩大耕地面积

&bull; **Cultivated** land：According to the Chunxi Sanshan Zhi, reclaimed land reached over 14,140 qing and 16 mu

&bull; **Tax policy**：Implemented a "one-tenth" tax, "without excessive collection," with light corvée and low taxes

&bull; **Crop promotion**：Encouraged tea cultivation for large-scale export; promoted double-cropping rice

&bull; **Water conservancy**：Reclaimed land from the sea to expand arable area

### 3.2 手工业 / Handicraft Industry

闽国的手工业在农业发展的基础上稳步增长。福建出产的**陶器**、**铁器**大量销于国外。冶炼业、纺织业、造船业等均有发展。

The handicraft industry of the Min Kingdom grew steadily on the foundation of agricultural development. **Pottery** and **ironware** produced in Fujian were exported in large quantities abroad. Smelting, textiles, and shipbuilding all saw development.

### 3.3 货币 / Currency

闽国铸造了多种货币，包括**铜钱、铁钱、铅钱**。

The Min Kingdom minted various currencies, including **bronze coins, iron coins, and lead coins**.

#### 货币特色 / Currency Features:


&bull; **贞明元年（915年）**：汀州宁化县发现铅矿，王审知当年设置铅场，纳入官办

&bull; **贞明二年（916年）**：铸造开元通宝铅钱，这是中国最早上市流通的铅钱

&bull; 因铅钱又大又软，容易磨损，被称为“大糯钱”

&bull; **915 (Zhenming 1)**：Lead mines were discovered in Ninghua County, Tingzhou; Wang Shenzhi established official lead fields

&bull; **916 (Zhenming 2)**：Cast Kaiyuan Tongbao lead coins—the earliest lead coins to circulate in China

&bull; Due to being large and soft, easily worn out, they were called "Da Nuo Qian" (big soft coins)

## 四、贸易 / Trade

### 4.1 海上贸易 / Maritime Trade

闽国充分利用福建沿海的地理优势，积极拓展海外贸易。王审知充分利用泉州、福州等天然良港，鼓励海上贸易，**奠定了宋代海运大发展的基础**。

The Min Kingdom fully leveraged Fujian's coastal geographic advantages to actively expand overseas trade. Wang Shenzhi made full use of natural deep-water ports such as Quanzhou and Fuzhou to encourage maritime trade, **laying the foundation for the great development of maritime shipping in the Song dynasty**.

### 4.2 港口建设 / Port Development

#### 甘棠港 / Gantang Port:


&bull; 王审知开辟黄岐澳“甘棠港”

&bull; 福州成为对外贸易的重要港口

&bull; 甘棠港北通日本、高丽，南联南洋诸岛，甚至远达阿拉伯地区，是著名的货物集散地

&bull; Wang Shenzhi developed the "Gantang Port" at Huangqi Ao

&bull; Fuzhou became an important port for foreign trade

&bull; Gantang Port reached Japan and Goryeo to the north, the Nanyang islands to the south, and even as far as the Arabian region—a famous entrepôt

#### 泉州港 / Quanzhou Port:

&bull; 王审知支持扩建和改造泉州港

&bull; 福州、泉州成为**当时中国最大的两个港口**

&bull; 泉州因此闻名世界，成为**海上丝绸之路的起点城市**

&bull; Wang Shenzhi supported the expansion and renovation of Quanzhou Port

&bull; Fuzhou and Quanzhou became **the two largest ports in China at that time**

&bull; Quanzhou became world-renowned as the **starting point of the Maritime Silk Road**

### 4.3 贸易政策与管理 / Trade Policy and Management

#### 政策措施 / Policy Measures:


&bull; 取消闽江流域关卡，畅通山区与沿海城市的物资交流

&bull; 简化通关手续，“尽去繁苛，纵其交易”

&bull; 设立“**榷货务**”（后更名为**市舶司**），由张睦担任，负责管理对外贸易

&bull; Removed checkpoints along the Min River basin to facilitate the exchange of goods between mountainous areas and coastal cities

&bull; Simplified customs procedures—"removed all繁琐 restrictions and allowed free trade"

&bull; Established the "**Quehuowu**" (later renamed the **Maritime Trade Supervisory Office**), headed by Zhang Mu, to manage foreign trade

### 4.4 贸易商品与伙伴 / Trade Goods and Partners

#### 出口商品 / Exports:


&bull; 陶器、铁器

&bull; 茶叶

#### 进口商品 / Imports:


&bull; 象牙、犀角、珍珠、香药等

&bull; 玳瑁、琉璃、犀象器、珍玩、奇品、海味

#### 贸易伙伴 / Trading Partners:


&bull; 日本、高丽（朝鲜半岛）

&bull; 南洋诸岛

&bull; 阿拉伯地区

&bull; 许多伊斯兰教徒经常到泉州进行贸易活动

#### Exports:

&bull; Pottery and ironware

&bull; Tea

#### Imports:

&bull; Ivory, rhinoceros horn, pearls, aromatic medicines, etc.

&bull; Tortoiseshell, glass, rhinoceros and elephant artifacts, curios, exotic products, seafood

#### Trading Partners:

&bull; Japan and Goryeo (Korean Peninsula)

&bull; Nanyang islands

&bull; The Arabian region

&bull; Many Islamic merchants frequently came to Quanzhou for trade

### 4.5 贸易成效 / Trade Achievements

在张睦掌管榷货务期间，“**招来蛮裔商贾，敛不加暴，而国用日以富饶**”。闽国多次向中原王朝进贡，福州曾“贡玳瑁、琉璃、犀象器，并珍玩、香药、奇品、海味，色类良多，**价累千万**”。统治者日常也大量消耗来自海外的珍稀物品，“日焚龙脑、薰陆诸香无算”。

During Zhang Mu's tenure at the Quehuowu, he "**attracted foreign merchants, collected taxes without excess, and the state treasury grew increasingly prosperous**". The Min Kingdom frequently paid tribute to the Central Plains dynasties; Fuzhou once "tributed tortoiseshell, glass, rhinoceros and elephant artifacts, curios, aromatic medicines, exotic products, and seafood—of many varieties, **worth tens of millions**". The rulers also consumed large quantities of rare overseas goods daily—"burning countless amounts of borneol and frankincense every day".

## 五、总结 / Summary

闽国虽仅存续三十余年，但在福建历史上留下了深远影响。王审知时期的**休养生息政策、农业开发、港口建设与海上贸易拓展**，为福建后来的经济发展奠定了坚实基础，也为宋元时期泉州成为“东方第一大港”铺平了道路。

Although the Min Kingdom lasted only slightly over thirty years, it left a profound impact on Fujian's history. The **policies of rehabilitation, agricultural development, port construction, and maritime trade expansion** during Wang Shenzhi's reign laid a solid foundation for Fujian's subsequent economic development and paved the way for Quanzhou to become the "largest port in the East" during the Song and Yuan dynasties.
