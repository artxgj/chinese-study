### 當時誰治福建省？

**戰國時期，福建省不屬於七雄任何一國直接管轄**。 <br>
當地主要為百越部落中的「閩」族聚居地。戰國中後期（約西元前334年），被楚國擊敗的越國王族南遷入閩，與土著融合建立閩越國。因此福建當時屬閩越國，獨立於中原爭霸之外。

### Who Ruled Fujian at That Time?

**During the Warring States period, Fujian was not directly ruled by any of the Seven Powers.** <br>
This area was mainly inhabited by the Min people, a branch of the Bai-Yue indigenous tribes. Around 334 BCE, the defeated royal family of the Yue Kingdom migrated south into Fujian and merged with the local population to establish the Minyue Kingdom. Thus, Fujian was then part of the Minyue Kingdom, independent of the Central Plains states.
