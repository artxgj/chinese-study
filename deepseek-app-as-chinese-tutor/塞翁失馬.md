#### 塞翁失馬 (Sài Wēng Shī Mǎ)

#### Literal Translation: The old man at the border lost his horse.

---

#### 📖 成語故事 (The Story)

##### 中文：

戰國時期，靠近邊塞的一位老翁丟了一匹馬。鄰居都來安慰他，他卻說：「這怎麼知道不是福呢？」幾個月後，那匹馬不僅自己回來，還帶回一匹駿馬。鄰居祝賀他，他卻說：「這怎麼知道不是禍呢？」後來他兒子騎那匹駿馬摔斷了腿。鄰居安慰他，他又說：「這怎麼知道不是福呢？」一年後外族入侵，健壯青年全被徵兵戰死，唯獨他兒子因跛腳免於出征，保全了性命。

##### English:

During the Warring States period, an old man living near the frontier lost his horse. His neighbors came to console him, but he said, "How do you know this isn't a blessing?" Months later, the horse returned with a fine wild stallion. The neighbors congratulated him, but he said, "How do you know this isn't a disaster?" Later, his son broke his leg riding that stallion. The neighbors consoled him again, but he said, "How do you know this isn't a blessing?" A year later, barbarians invaded. All able-bodied young men were drafted and died in battle—except his son, who was spared because of his crippled leg.

---

#### 💡 核心寓意 (Core Meaning)

##### 中文：

比喻禍福相依，壞事在特定條件下可以變成好事，好事也可能變成壞事。提醒人們要以長遠、辯證的眼光看待得失，得意時不妄喜，失意時不氣餒。

##### English:

This idiom illustrates that fortune and misfortune are interdependent. A apparent loss can turn into a gain under certain conditions, and vice versa. It teaches us to view successes and failures with a long-term, philosophical perspective—stay humble in good times and resilient in bad times.

---

#### 📝 用法與例句 (Usage & Examples)

##### 中文用法：

多用於安慰遭遇損失或挫折的人，或形容事情結果難以預料。常見搭配：「塞翁失馬，焉知非福」。

##### English Usage:

Used to comfort someone who has suffered a loss, or to describe an unpredictable outcome. Often paired with the second half: "Sài Wēng shī mǎ, yān zhī fēi fú" (How do you know it’s not a blessing?).

| 中文例句                             | English Example                                                                                    |
| -------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 這次沒拿到訂單雖然可惜，但塞翁失馬，也許能讓我們找到更好的客戶。 | Losing this order is a pity, but a loss may turn out to be a gain—maybe we'll find better clients. |
| 他被裁員後反而創業成功，真是塞翁失馬。              | After being laid off, he started a successful business—truly blessing in disguise.                 |

#### 🧠 哲學背景 (Philosophical Context)

出自《淮南子·人間訓》，根源於道家思想，與老子「禍兮福之所倚，福兮禍之所伏」一脈相承，體現了中國傳統文化中樸素的辯證法智慧。

(Originating from Huainanzi, this story is rooted in Daoism and echoes Laozi’s saying: "Disaster leans on fortune, and fortune hides in disaster"—showcasing ancient Chinese dialectical wisdom.)
