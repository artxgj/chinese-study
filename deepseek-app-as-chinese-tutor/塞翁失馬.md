## 二、完整原文（文言文）/ Complete Original Text (Classical Chinese)

> 近塞上之人，有善術者，馬無故亡而入胡。人皆吊之，其父曰：「此何遽不為福乎？」居數月，其馬將胡駿馬而歸。人皆賀之，其父曰：「此何遽不能為禍乎？」家富良馬，其子好騎，墮而折其髀。人皆吊之，其父曰：「此何遽不為福乎？」居一年，胡人大入塞，丁壯者引弦而戰。近塞之人，死者十九。此獨以跛之故，父子相保。

## 三、完整英文翻譯 / Complete English Translation

> There was an old man at a frontier fort in the north who understood Taoism. One day he lost his horse, which wandered into the land of the Hu tribesmen. His neighbors came to condole with him, and the man said, "How do you know that this is bad luck?" After a few months, the horse returned with some fine horses of the Hu breed, and the people congratulated him. The old man said, "How do you know that this is good luck?" He then became very prosperous with so many horses. The son one day broke his legs riding, and all the people came to condole with him again. The old man said, "How do you know that this is bad luck?" One day the Hu tribesmen invaded the frontier fort. All the young men fought with arrows to defend it, and nine tenths of them were killed. Because the son was a cripple, both father and son escaped unharmed.

## 六、寓意與啟示 / Meaning and Lessons

### 中文 / Chinese

「塞翁失馬」闡述了禍與福的對立統一關係，揭示了 **「禍兮福所倚，福兮禍所伏」** 的道理。

這則寓言啟發人們用發展的眼光、辯證地看問題：


&bull; **身處逆境不消沉**：樹立「柳暗花明」的樂觀信念

&bull; **身處順境不迷醉**：保持「死於安樂」的憂患意識

需要特別注意的是：福與禍的轉化需要一定的條件，不能誤解為轉化是必然的。

### English / English

The idiom "Sai Weng Lost His Horse" illustrates the unity of opposites between misfortune and fortune, revealing the principle that **"Misfortune may be the doorstep of fortune, and fortune may be the cradle of misfortune."**

This fable teaches us to view problems with a **developmental and dialectical perspective**:


&bull; **In adversity, do not lose heart**: Maintain an optimistic belief that "every cloud has a silver lining"

&bull; **In prosperity, do not become complacent**: Keep a sense of vigilance even in times of ease

It is important to note that the transformation between fortune and misfortune requires specific conditions; one should not assume that such transformation is inevitable.

## 七、現代用法 / Modern Usage

### 中文 / Chinese

「塞翁失馬」常用於安慰遭遇暫時損失或挫折的人，表示**雖然暫時受到損失，但也許反而因此能得到好處**。也指**壞事在一定條件下可以轉化為好事**。

**例句**：這次考試雖然失利了，但塞翁失馬，焉知非福，說不定能讓你更清楚地認識自己的不足。

### English / English

"Sai Weng Shi Ma" is often used to comfort those who have suffered temporary losses or setbacks, meaning that **although one has suffered a loss for now, one may eventually gain something from it**. It also indicates that **bad things can turn into good things under certain conditions**.

**Example**: Although you failed this exam, a loss may turn out to be a gain — perhaps this will help you see your weaknesses more clearly.
